Well today was Logan's first trip to Roger Williams Park Zoo. &nbsp;It wasn't nearly as well documented as Evan's <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2011/08/three-little-monkeys.html">first trip</a>&nbsp;but it is tough to juggle two little boys on one of the first hot, summery days of the year when you go to the zoo during school vacation week! &nbsp;What was I thinking?!?! &nbsp;We met Auntie Karin, Sean and Grammy and Emily &amp; Lia there. &nbsp;We had a fun day, complete with a trip to Hasbro's Big Backyard to play in the water and Del's. &nbsp;After today, I'm not sure how many "outings" I will be taking by myself this summer. &nbsp;Maybe we'll just spend more time at the "beach house"!<br />
