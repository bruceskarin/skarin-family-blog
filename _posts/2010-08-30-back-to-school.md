I can’t believe that today was the first day of school and that this weekend is Labor Day weekend.  Why is it that the summer always goes by so quickly?  Today was my first day of school.  Well, it should have been anyway.  However, a few weeks ago, Bruce and I decided that I would take this first semester off to spend the time with Evan so that he would be a little older before going to daycare full time.  So lucky me, I still have another four and a half months off.  As I think about going back to school, I can’t quite wrap my head around it but I suppose I would have mentally prepared myself if I needed to return today.  Instead, Evan and I had a quiet day, getting some things done around the house and running a few errands.  I am really looking forward to the time we will have together this fall and during the holidays!!<br />
