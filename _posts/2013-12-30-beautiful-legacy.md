<div class="MsoNormal">
I see ornaments from my childhood on the Christmas tree</div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
And I can’t believe you are gone.</div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
I see gifts you gave the boys</div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
And I can’t believe you are gone.</div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
I hear you say “Hi, dear” as you did every time I called</div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
And I can’t believe you are gone.</div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
I see your photograph on my dresser; I can almost hear you
laugh</div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
And I can’t believe you are gone.</div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
<br /></div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
But then I remember, you are not gone</div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
You live on in us all!</div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
In the happiness of your children </div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
As they enjoy their lives;</div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
In the strength of your grandchildren </div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
As they forge their own paths;</div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
In the smiles and laughs of your great-grandchildren </div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
As they learn about the world.</div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
<br /></div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
You have taught us all so much.</div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
How to live life to the fullest;</div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
How to be confident and courageous in times of strife;</div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
How to look for the good in everyone;</div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
How a good education can open any door;</div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
How hard work leads to amazing results;</div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
And how family is the most important thing in the world</div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
I will always cherish this beautiful legacy you have given
us.&nbsp;</div>
<div class="MsoNormal">
<br /></div>
