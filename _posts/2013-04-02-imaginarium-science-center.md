It has become a bit of a tradition during our trips to Fort Myers for Gramma and Mommy to have a day of brunch/lunch and shopping while the boys do whatever it is that they decide to do. &nbsp;Sometimes they stay at the condo and swim, sometimes they go to a park and playground. &nbsp;Today they decided to go to the Imaginarium Science Center and escape the sun for the afternoon. &nbsp;Evan was a bit too young for many of the exhibits and activities but he had fun all the same. &nbsp;This is definitely a place that we will visit again when the boys are a little older. <br />
