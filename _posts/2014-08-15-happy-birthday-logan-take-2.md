Logan turned two the day after we returned from a short vacation to Pennsylvania that included a trip to Sesame Place. &nbsp;Needless to say, we were wiped out and didn't do much to celebrate that day. With his party coming up tomorrow, we decided to do a bit of a birthday make-up today. &nbsp;He opened a couple of birthday presents, we went to the concert on the common and had pizza and a movie night with a movie of Logan's choosing...Cinderella! It has turned into a week plus long celebration for our little guy. &nbsp;Not a bad deal when you are two! &nbsp;Love you, pal!<br />
