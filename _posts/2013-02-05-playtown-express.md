Today we went to <a href="http://www.playtownexpress.com/">PlayTown Express</a>. &nbsp;We were supposed to meet the Wheeler's there but unfortunately Dylan was sick. &nbsp;We decided to check it out on our own anyway and boy am I glad we did. &nbsp;Evan loved it! &nbsp;This is probably the first time that we have been to a place like this where he actually played on the equipment and explored on his own for the majority of the time we were there. &nbsp;It usually takes him a while to warm up and then he plays for what seems like 10 minutes before we are ready to leave. &nbsp;There were lots of things to climb on, jump on, bounce on, slide on, ride on...it was great! &nbsp;We played for a while, had a snacky lunch and then played for a while longer. &nbsp;We will certainly be going back and bringing friends too! &nbsp;Well worth the price of admission, especially for active little boys!<br />
