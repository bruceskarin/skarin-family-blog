I wanted to do something special with Evan as a family, just the three of us, before the baby arrived so today we went to Davis Farmland. &nbsp;The weather wasn't great but we had a wonderful day anyway! &nbsp;We began our day meandering through the animal showcase. &nbsp;Evan loved seeing, petting and feeding all of the animals. &nbsp;It was great how he let them eat right out of his hand without being scared at all. &nbsp;Then we took a tour of the farm on the safari hayride. &nbsp;When the hayride was over, it began to rain a bit so we headed inside for some lunch. &nbsp;The rain had mostly stopped by the time we finished lunch and Evan had fun playing on the tractors and in the tool shop at Imagine Acres for a while. &nbsp;It was starting to get late but we wanted to check out the Adventure Play &amp; Spray water park. &nbsp;We took a stroll over to that side of the farmland and after taking a ride on the go-carts, Evan played in the spray park and the bubble blast for a bit. &nbsp;Evan was a bit sad when it was time to go but he was asleep within 10 minutes of being back in the car. &nbsp;It was a wonderfully fun family day with our little guy! &nbsp;I loved seeing and hearing the excitement on his face and in his voice all day! &nbsp;A special day for a very special boy about to become a big brother!<br />
