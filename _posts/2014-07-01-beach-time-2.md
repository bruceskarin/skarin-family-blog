We started our Cape week vacation with 4 straight days at 3 different beaches. &nbsp;Days 1 and 2 were at Sea Street beach, day 3 was at Cold Storage Beach and day 4 was at the beach in Brewster, just across from the house that Adria and Avi rented. &nbsp;Each day we would load up the car with gear and kids, drive the 1/2 mile to the beach, unload everyone but the driver and go get set up on the beach while the driver would return the car to the house. &nbsp;It was a great set up. &nbsp;The only thing better would have been if we were directly on the beach. &nbsp;The boys had so much fun playing in the water, building sandcastles, and exploring each different beach. &nbsp;My favorite was Cold Storage...by far! &nbsp;It may have been all because of the extra time with the ladies in the afternoon while the guys were back at the house with the boys but I would go back there in a minute!<br />
