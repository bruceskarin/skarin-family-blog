It is hard for me to believe that it has already been two years since we first laid eyes on our little guy. &nbsp;It seems like just yesterday that I held him for the first time. &nbsp;Then I think about all that has happened over the last two years and it seems impossible that all of that could have happened in just two years! &nbsp;Today was a rather low-key day at home with mom, dad, Omi and Grandpa. &nbsp;We had hoped to be able to take a few hours to do something special but unfortunately today is the only day this week that it isn't supposed to rain so Daddy and Grandpa needed to take advantage of the nice weather to do some painting for the basement. &nbsp;Even though we didn't do anything "special" to celebrate, Evan still had a fun day playing in the yard and with his new toys!<br />
