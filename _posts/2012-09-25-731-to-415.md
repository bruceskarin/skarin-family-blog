The first seven weeks of Evan's life we took 731 pictures of him. &nbsp;The first seven weeks of Logan's life we took 415. &nbsp;Second child syndrome, right? &nbsp;Well maybe not. &nbsp;If you add in the 118 pictures that we have of both boys together, the new total is 533. &nbsp;If you then count all of the photos that are organized in other folders from apple picking, Papa &amp; Gramma's anniversary, Omi &amp; Grandpa's visit, Daddy's birthday, Labor Day weekend, and Uncle Dan &amp; Auntie Karin's cookout, I'd say that we have probably just about as many pictures of Logan as we did of Evan after 7 weeks. &nbsp;At times it is definitely a bit more challenging to take photos - sometimes I feel like it would be great if I was an octopus so I could have enough hands to do everything. &nbsp;Plus for the first few weeks of Logan's life he slept so much that, even as cute as he is awake and sleeping, we didn't take too many pictures. &nbsp;All in all, I'd say we are doing pretty well documenting his life as much as we did his big brother's.<br />
Here is a sampling of some of the best photos that haven't appeared in any other post. &nbsp;Please forgive me for not captioning every photo...I do have two little boys to take care of you know. :-) Enjoy!<br />
