Today Evan and I drove up to Manch-Vegas (aka Manchester NH) to visit my friend Amanda who is home from Cancun for a few weeks.  We spent the afternoon by the pool with Erica, Adley, Nathaniel, Amanda and Eileen.  It was a fun way to spend a hot day!  Once Erica and the boys left, Amanda and I had a chance to catch up for a while, just the two of us…something that doesn’t happen very often!  Thanks for having us up!<br />
