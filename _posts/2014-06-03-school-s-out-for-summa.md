It's hard to believe that Evan's first year of preschool at Here We Grow has already come to an end! Even as long and as cold as this winter was, it seems like just yesterday that we were dropping him off for the first time, crying as we left him. &nbsp;Evan has learned so much this year and grown up quite a bit as well. &nbsp;When I asked him what his favorite thing about school was this year, his response was "playing with my friends!" &nbsp;I am so glad to hear this because that is the main reason that we wanted him to attend preschool. &nbsp;Could I have taught him most of what he learned this year? &nbsp;Sure! &nbsp;But could he have gained independence and confidence learning how to interact with his peers being home with me? &nbsp;Nope. &nbsp;Such a great first year experience thanks to Mrs. Coyne and Mrs. Corey. &nbsp;Evan is already asking about next year! &nbsp;I hope he never looses that desire for learning! &nbsp;Such a great performance by an adorable group of children!<br />
