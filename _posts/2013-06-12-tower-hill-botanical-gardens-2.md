After being in South Carolina for four days with three amazing women, I was rested and relaxed and ready for a new adventure with my boys. &nbsp;We met Jackie and the girls at the Tower Hill Botanical Gardens for a picnic lunch and a leisurely tour through the gardens. &nbsp;It was wonderful to see them, as it always is, and fun to see the kids together. The grounds were a bit wet from all the rain we had over the weekend and it was quite windy when we first arrived but all in all a great way to spend the day!<br />
<br />
There is a cute story about the first photo. &nbsp;Just before the picture was taken the following took place after we decided to walk down to the pergola:<br />
<br />
Evalyn: Do you want to hold my hand?<br />
Evan: (shaking his hands about shoulder level) Oh no, no, no<br />
Me: Evan, you could just say no thank you<br />
Evan: No, thank you<br />
Evalyn starts walking towards the pergola<br />
Evan: Oh, wait, yes, yes, I do want to hold your hand as he grabs Evalyn's hand and they start walking together.<br />
<br />
So cute!<br />
