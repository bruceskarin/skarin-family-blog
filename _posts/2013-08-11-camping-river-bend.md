Uncle Dan and Auntie Karin have been talking about buying a camper for a couple of years now and this week Uncle Dan bought one for Auntie Karin for her birthday! &nbsp;Well sort of. &nbsp;They bought a big pop up camper and picked it up on Saturday afternoon. &nbsp;Uncle Dan arrived back in Warwick with it around 4 o'clock, they loaded up and headed out for their first camping trip at River Bend in Oneco, CT and we joined them! &nbsp;I wasn't sure how it would go with Logan (picturing dirt and pine needle camp sites with him crawling around and putting things in his mouth) but it was great. &nbsp;A nice big grassy site with lots of space for the boys to play and roam. &nbsp;This was quite the camping experience too. &nbsp;Not only does the camper have everything you could ever need...running water, electricity, heat, etc...the campground had all sorts of events including a karaoke dance party on Saturday night and an extensive bingo game Sunday morning. &nbsp;Papa, Gramma and Grammy even came for a visit on Sunday. &nbsp;It was a beautiful weekend and we were so glad to be there on their inaugural camping trip! &nbsp;Looking forward to making lots of camping memories for years to come! &nbsp;(Did I really just type that??? Obviously, it's not roughin' it!)<br />
