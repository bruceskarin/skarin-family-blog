Today was the only rainy, cool day we had on our vacation.  We had a low-key relaxed start to our day and were greeted by a bunny on the patio this morning.  After Evan's nap, we went to Bucket's for lunch and then hit a few souvenir shops in search of a couple new magnets.  We didn't find what we were looking for but we did find a boat and a turtle that will be accompanying Evan to the pool tomorrow.  Bruce, Evan and I had a quiet night at the condo while Gramma and Papa went to see a play with some friends.<br />
