After two weeks of not being able to drive, I was again feeling a bit house bound. &nbsp;So even though Papa and Gramma offered to come to our house to help out today, we decided to go to their house. &nbsp;After packing up and getting everyone loaded into the car, I was beginning to think I was crazy and that it would have been much easier for them to come to us than for us to go to them! &nbsp;Oh well! &nbsp;We spent a nice afternoon by the pool and playing in the yard. &nbsp;It was Logan's first trip to the pool house. &nbsp;I think he may have opened his eyes to see the water for a few seconds even though he was sleeping much of the day! &nbsp;Happy birthday, Papa! &nbsp;Thanks for letting us (once again) invade your house today!<br />
