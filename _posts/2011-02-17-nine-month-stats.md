Yesterday we went to the pediatrician for Evan's nine month well visit.  We were quite anxious to see how big he is.  Here is what we found out:  Height - 29.5 inches (81st percentile), Weight - 22 pounds, 14 ounces (79th percentile) and Head circumference - 47.5 centimeters (94th percentile).  The doctor continues to be thrilled with Evan's progress and strongly urged us to baby proof if we haven't yet.  Evan had his finger pricked for a lead test as well as his 3rd round of immunizations.
