Today was Evan's first day of his second year of preschool...Pre-K 4's...and to quote him "It was AWESOME!" &nbsp;He began the day by bounding out of his room just after 7:30 am, dressed and ready to go. &nbsp;He and Logan watched the end of Henry Hugglemonster before heading down stairs for breakfast. &nbsp;Evan was excited about his breakfast of Cinnamon Toast Crunch and to pack his own lunch bag. &nbsp;As we were cleaning up from breakfast I told him that I was excited for him, his response was, "I'm excited for me too!" &nbsp;We snapped some photos, drove to school, washed his hands (per school policy), waited in line to be greeted by his teachers and he didn't look back! &nbsp;I actually had to ask him to come back and give me a hug and a kiss. &nbsp;I shed a few tears but nothing like <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2013/09/here-we-grow.html">last year</a>. &nbsp;I enjoyed my alone time with Logan, including lunch that seemed a bit strange without Evan, before heading back to pick up Evan. &nbsp;He didn't have a whole lot to say other than "It was awesome!" &nbsp;We continued the special day with a trip to Crystal Caves for mini-golf and ice cream. &nbsp;Not a bad day for a super special 4-year old! &nbsp;I can't wait to see him grow and learn this year! <br />
