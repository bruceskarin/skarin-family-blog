Today we went to visit Sofia and Emily (and Ethan and Stacey) at their new home in Newtown. &nbsp;They moved this summer from western Mass so that Ethan could be closer to his new business in Framingham. &nbsp;It was great to see them all and catch up. &nbsp;I can't believe how grown-up Sofia is. &nbsp;She is a sweet, smart little girl and Evan loves playing with her. &nbsp;Emily was napping for some of the time while we were there but she has a sweet little personality too. &nbsp;It was fun seeing everyone play together and we hope to be able to get together more often now that they are a little closer.<br />
