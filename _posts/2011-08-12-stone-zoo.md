Today we had another Free Fun Friday adventure!  This time we went to the Stone Zoo in Stoneham and this time Daddy joined us!!  We got started a little later than I had originally hoped but it turned out to be ok because the zoo wasn't that big.  We started the afternoon with a picnic lunch and then started our stroll through the zoo.  Because of the Free Fun Friday event it was quite crowded.  But once we got going, it thinned out a bit.  This was Evan's first trip to a zoo and I would say that he liked the black bears and the big kitties (the cougar and jaguar) the best.  He had fun looking at all the animals and making as many animal noises as he could!  We ended our day with dinner at Red Robin where Evan was a big fan of the balloons and not a fan of the "Happy Birthday" singing.
