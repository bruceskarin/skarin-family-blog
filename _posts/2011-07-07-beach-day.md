Nicole was on vacation this week and asked Evan and I to join her for a day at the beach.  We were very excited to do so and Daddy was a little jealous.  But as a result of not having any meetings at work, he decided to take a vacation day to join us.  Boy, did we have fun!  It was a perfect day!  Evan loved playing in the sand by the water, so much so that he even tried to eat it a few times.  This was our cue that maybe it was time for lunch!  After lunch the boys napped while the girls gabbed.  Then it was time for more water and sand fun until we had to leave.  As Nicole said when we were leaving, "I think we have a beach bum on our hands!" <br />
