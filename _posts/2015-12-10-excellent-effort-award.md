<br />
Today Logan and I went to Evan's school for a ceremony for all of the students who received the Excellent Effort Award for the month of November. &nbsp;Each month every classroom teacher chooses a student from the classroom who has displayed excellent effort over the course of the month. &nbsp;These students are recognized with a short ceremony and are presented with a certificate. &nbsp;Evan was the chosen student for Ms. Paluses class for the month of November. &nbsp;He was the first boy chosen in his class and he was pretty proud of his award. &nbsp;We are proud of you too, pal! &nbsp;I have a feeling that this is the first of many academic awards you will receive throughout your lifetime. &nbsp;Keep up the good work, kiddo. &nbsp;Keep working hard...Oh, the places you'll go!<br />
