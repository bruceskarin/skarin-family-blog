We have known for a while now that Bruce needed a new car. &nbsp;His car was still running ok and there was nothing really forcing us to test drive or buy a new car. &nbsp;That is until last week. &nbsp;On the 3rd, after a difficult day with Evan, Bruce called to tell me that his car broke down and he was being towed to a garage in Waltham. &nbsp;So that evening, instead of heading to the carnival and fireworks as we had planned, the boys and I drove to Waltham to pick up Daddy. <br />
We celebrated a super fun 4th of July at Papa &amp; Gramma's house. &nbsp;Then on the 5th, while the Maxima was getting a new radiator in Waltham, Daddy went to test drive cars. &nbsp;Well, sort of! &nbsp;He never actually made it to drive any. &nbsp;As he was pulling into the first dealership, he hit an aluminum wrapped concrete sign pole and wrecked my car! &nbsp;Thankfully he was ok but we found 3 days later that my car was totaled! &nbsp;By this time his car was fixed and ready to be picked up but for a short time we were without a car. <br />
Our good friend, Chris, was our lifesaver...once again! &nbsp;He picked Bruce up from the accident and was going to be out of town for 2 weeks and insisted that we keep his Trail Blazer. &nbsp;It worked out perfectly as we looked for, found and bought two new cars...a 2012 Altima for Bruce and a 2008 TSX for me! &nbsp;I still have not seen a photo of the damage and I don't know that I ever will but I am certainly glad that the 2013 car saga is behind us, no one was hurt and we can (almost) laugh about it now. <br />
