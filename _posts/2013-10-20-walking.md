Just a little over 2 weeks ago while we were visiting Papa and Gramma, Logan took his first steps! &nbsp;He was about one month slower than his brother to crawl and also about one month slower to walk than <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2011/06/thirteen-steps.html">Evan</a>. &nbsp;This seems funny to me though because Logan's gross motor skills seem more advanced than Evan's were as far as getting on and off ride on toys and also climbing the stairs. &nbsp;He was walking some when Bruce and I were in the keys but still mainly crawling. &nbsp;Well now, just 2 weeks later, he is mainly walking...walking approaching running!! &nbsp;He can get up easily when he falls, even if he is in the middle of the room. &nbsp;He can turn around quickly, step over things, walk while carrying Barky and other toys and even walk while drinking his milk! &nbsp;While it is amazing to watch I can't help but think...what happened to my baby?? &nbsp;He is growing up so fast!<br />
