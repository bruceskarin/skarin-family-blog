As we approach the end of July, I have a hard time believing that I have already been out of school for 14 weeks and that we have already been home with Evan for almost 11 weeks.  He is 11 weeks old today!  That’s just nuts!!  Where does the time go?  Many days I find myself looking at the clock and wondering how four o’clock always comes so fast and how I will ever find the time to do all the things I used to do during the summer.  I suppose I won’t find time for some things but then I’m sure they weren’t that important to begin with anyway.  Most of my blog posts have been about the adventures that we have had, however big or small.  But we also have lots of days where Evan and I just stay home and enjoy each other.
