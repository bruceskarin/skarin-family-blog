Hoppy Easter everybunny!  Today we celebrated Evan's first Easter with dinner at Uncle Glenn and Aunt Chris's and then dessert at Aunt Diane and Uncle Pat's.  It was nice today to remember just how pregnant I was last Easter.   I remember being very uncomfortable all day but not this year!  This year we had a great day.  It was a decent day weatherwise so we were able to play outside at Uncle Glenn and Aunt Chris's house.  We didn't quite have enough people for the annual wiffleball game but the little boys had fun playing anyway.  It is so nice that the next generation of kids get along so well!  Then on to North Attleboro where Evan participated in his first Easter egg hunt.  He only found one egg but it was a big one with five rolls of smarties so he was satisfied.  Evan also enjoyed all of the goodies that the Easter Bunny left at various houses.
