Before heading to see the Omi, Grandpa and the Smucker's for Christmas, we were fortunate to spend a couple of days in Seattle with Emily and Stephen. &nbsp;It was so nice to see their new house and to have time to get to know Stephen a little better. &nbsp;It worked out perfectly that they live so close to the airport in Seattle and could pick us up after what was already a long evening of traveling. &nbsp;We got settled in for the night and went to bed shortly after considering it was already well after midnight EST. &nbsp;On Monday, we had a lazy morning and then spent the afternoon and evening touring Seattle. &nbsp;The city was all a bustle with holiday events and it was great to spend sometime outside after a nice lunch. &nbsp;We rode a&nbsp;carousel&nbsp;(Evan loved his green horse almost as much as Stephen loved his red one!), we walked through Pike Place Market, strolled past the light display by the waterfront and even saw Santa at Macy's. &nbsp;What a fun day! After our day out and about in the city, we headed back to Emily and Stephen's adorable home for a relaxing evening being entertained by Evan and his dancing skills. &nbsp;Thanks for a great visit, Em and Stephen!<br />
