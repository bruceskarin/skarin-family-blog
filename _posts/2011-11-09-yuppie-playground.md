Today we met Auntie Karin, Danny and Sean at the "yuppie" playground in Pawtuxet Village. &nbsp;Auntie named this playground a few years ago and today was our first time visiting it. &nbsp;It was a beautiful fall day, likely one of the last ones we will have this season, and the playground had something for everyone. &nbsp;We brought a picnic lunch to enjoy when the boys needed a break from playing. &nbsp;Evan loved driving the "woo-woo" (fire truck), Sean loved the slides and Danny was such a good big brother and cousin, always making sure the little boys were safe and <a href="http://youtu.be/dSL4LAcvUC0">having fun</a>. &nbsp;<a href="http://youtu.be/s5pawNNF_js">Danny</a> and <a href="http://youtu.be/iIM5H6lTfI4">Evan</a> loved the spin bucket and Sean knew to stay away...smart boy! &nbsp;It is so nice that Auntie is home on Wednesday's so that we can share these special days together!<br />
