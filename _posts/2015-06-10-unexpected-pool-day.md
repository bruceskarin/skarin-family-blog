In an effort to get back to blogging...(you gotta start somewhere, right??)...Most of the posts from July 2014 until today are (hopefully going to be) post-dated. &nbsp;I really enjoy updating our readers on the lives of these two sweet boys but can't always find the time to do so.<br />
So anyway, in an effort to get back to blogging...today we had an unexpected pool day at Papa &amp; Gramma's house. &nbsp;I brought the boys down to see Papa, expecting to just play in the yard and in the house but Papa surprised us by having the pool open. &nbsp;Good thing he also had some spare bathing suits for the boys. &nbsp;They braved the chilly 67 degree water and were the first ones in for the 2015 season. &nbsp;They had fun re-exploring all of the pool toys and throwing EVERYTHING in the water! &nbsp;Papa has been working very hard in the yard (which looks beautiful) to get everything ready for summer. &nbsp;Great job, Papa! &nbsp;You sure made these little guys day today! &nbsp;It was a nice day despite everyone missing one very special Gramma! &nbsp;It's just not the same without you, Mom! &nbsp;We all love you so much!<br />
