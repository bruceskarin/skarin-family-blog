Back in June, Auntie Karin and I bought a Groupon for Kidz Wurld in North Attleboro figuring we would take the boys sometime for a fun day. &nbsp;It didn't expire until October 3rd so we had plenty of time to use it. &nbsp;Well that time (aka summer) flew by and now it's already the middle of September. &nbsp;Well we went today minus Danny because he was at school and then went to lunch at Chelo's. &nbsp;It was a fun place with lots to do for all ages, I just wish they would allow you to bring snacks and drinks instead of having to purchase them there and that the "extra" rides and games weren't so expensive . &nbsp;The boys had a good time though so we might go back again some other time. &nbsp;It was Logan's first time at a play place where he was actually playing and he was much more adventurous than Evan was at his <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/search?q=zoinks">first time</a>.<br />
