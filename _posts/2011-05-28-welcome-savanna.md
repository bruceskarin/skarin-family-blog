Today we drove five hours out to Cortland, NY for a welcome party for Chuck and Clare's little girl, Savanna.  She was born back in February but they are currently living in Atlanta and so this was the first opportunity we had to meet her.  What a cutie!  The party was held at Clare's parents farm and the weather (which was fore-casted to be thunderstorms) was perfect!  Evan had a great time walking around all the tables and chairs and playing with the dogs.  He even got to meet some horses, which he like from a distance but not too close!  At dusk there were some fireworks which Evan wasn't too fond of.  He liked the colors but not the noises.  We had a great day celebrating a wonderful family!
