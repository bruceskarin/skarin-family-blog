<div>On Friday, July 15th, Evan and I walked to the center of town to attend the first of a series of summer concerts on the common. Most of the music was way before my time but Evan loved dancing and clapping to it anyway. He was entertaining everyone around us and even made the paper today! Too bad you can't see the big smile he had at the time!</div>
