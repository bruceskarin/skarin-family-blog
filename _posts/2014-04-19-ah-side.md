From the moment Logan wakes up in the morning until the moment he goes to bed at night, he would be "ah-side" if we let him. &nbsp;We have been spending quite a bit of time "ah-side" since returning from Florida, both working and playing. &nbsp;If you mention going somewhere or get a coat or shoes, Logan immediately says "shoe on, shoe on, coat on?". &nbsp;And heaven forbid you run outside for one quick minute without him...he gets so upset. <br />
The other day after waking up from his nap, still groggy, he saw Daddy and Evan outside. &nbsp;Daddy was using the rake that I had given to Logan earlier in the day when the boys were helping me with some yard work. &nbsp;Logan looked outside, looked back and me and said, very seriously, "Daddy. &nbsp;Shobel. Mine!" &nbsp;I guess Daddy isn't allowed to use Logan's shobel (aka rake). <br />
