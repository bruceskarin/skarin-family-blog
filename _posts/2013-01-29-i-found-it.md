Anyone that truly knows me, know that I <strike>am OCD</strike>&nbsp;operate on the philosophy of a place for everything and everything in its place. &nbsp;There is a "right" way to load the dishwasher, there are specific places for things in the fridge, there is a particular way to fold the clothes, etc. &nbsp;I have <strike>been forced to</strike> tried to loosen up a bit after having two children but I still try to maintain as much order as possible. &nbsp;As such, each night after the boys are in bed, I reorder and reorganize their toys, making sure everything is put away and accounted for. &nbsp;(To date, we have only lost one screwdriver, which I am pretty sure ended up in the diaper pail when Evan was just starting to be on the move. &nbsp;Everything else always turns up after a couple of days.) &nbsp;Last week, after cleaning up, I couldn't find a little blue gear for our Playskool Busy Gears toy. &nbsp;This drove me nuts! &nbsp;Everyday I would ask Evan if he knew where it was and everyday he would say "I don't know!" &nbsp;Everyday until today! &nbsp;We were playing with his train set and as he was pulling out pieces of the train, he found the gear. &nbsp;He pulled it out of the bin and said "I found it!" and was so pleased with himself. &nbsp;Probably even more happy that he wouldn't have to hear me ask him where it was any more!<br />
