You may remember that Evan created a <a href="http://evan.skarin.us/2012/03/ready-for-beach.html">Florida to-do list</a> just about one week before we left for Fort Myers. While he didn't get to complete item number two (Gramma already had plans with Marsha), he did get to build a sand castle with Papa. &nbsp;Once Evan woke up and had breakfast, we were on our way to Fort Myers Beach. &nbsp;Well after a quick stop so that Papa and Evan could pick out some beach toys together! &nbsp;This was Evan's first trip to Fort Myers Beach seeing as we never made it last year other than our ride out to <a href="http://evan.skarin.us/2011/03/florida-day-ten.html">Sanibel and Captiva</a>&nbsp;. &nbsp;And what a beautiful day it was! &nbsp;Evan had a wonderful time playing in the water with Daddy, riding the waves with Papa and building a sand castle with both Daddy and Papa. &nbsp;After playing on the beach for a while, Evan decided he wanted to take a walk on the pier with Mommy and Papa. &nbsp;Once we got to the end of the pier, Evan watched the pelicans as they begged for a treat from the fishermen and we saw a guy reel in a tiny shark. &nbsp;The sun was hot and everyone was roasting so we packed up and went to Pete's Timeout for lunch before heading back to the condo. &nbsp;This was what is sure to be the first of many fun days spent on Fort Myers Beach for the Skarin's!<br />
