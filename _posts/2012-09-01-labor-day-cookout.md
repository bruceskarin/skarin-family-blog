A few weeks before Logan was born, Bruce told me that our friend Chuck would be visiting from Atlanta Labor Day weekend and that he (Bruce) had offered our place as a location for a BBQ on Saturday for a bunch of our friends. &nbsp;Normally I would have said, "Sure, no problem, we love hosting BBQ's," but I knew we would have a 3ish week old baby and I had no idea how that would be so I couldn't commit to anything. Well the time came to make a decision and I said&nbsp;"Sure, no problem, we love hosting BBQ's," &nbsp;It was a great opportunity to introduce some of our closest friends to Logan. &nbsp;Most people were very appreciative of us hosting but also thought we were crazy, especially the ladies. &nbsp;To some extent, it was crazy but it was also fun! &nbsp;And we don't (or can't) party like we used to...everyone was gone by 10 pm. <br />
