Daddy was camping with the guys and Uncle Dan and Papa were at Pete's bachelor party so Auntie Karin and I thought it would be fun to get the boys together for some mini golf and then meet Gramma and Grammy for dinner at the 99. &nbsp;Fun, huh? &nbsp;Well that all depends on your definition of fun. &nbsp;We had one player who was taking the game quite seriously and getting upset that the other two weren't. &nbsp;We had one player who always got a hole in one...I guess that happens when you start your ball only inches away from the hole. &nbsp;We had one player who was quite distracted and spacey who was quite hard to keep on task. &nbsp;And we had one player who wanted to walk the course instead of ride. &nbsp;I'll let you figure out who was who. &nbsp;Dinner at 99 was...interesting! &nbsp;Nothing really bad just 4 tired boys, 2 stressed mommys and 1 Gramma and 1 Grammy who probably would have had a more enjoyable dinner if they just went out together. &nbsp;Oh well, it's all about making memories, right? &nbsp;Well, it certainly was a memorable night! &nbsp;Next time I say the dads and Papa stay home and the moms, Gramma and Grammy go away!<div>
