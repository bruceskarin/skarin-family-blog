Back in April, I purchased a Living Social deal for <a href="http://www.zoinksfunfactory.com/index.php">Zoinks Fun Factory</a>&nbsp;in North Oxford. &nbsp;It was a BOGO admission for two kids and two adults. &nbsp;Earlier in the week, when I saw it was going to be another rainy Wednesday, I asked Auntie Karin if she and the boys would like to join us for some fun. &nbsp;(Poor Auntie! &nbsp;Wednesday is her day off and it seems as if the weather is often yucky on Wednesdays.) They didn't have any plans and so we met at Zoinks for some play time. <br />
The boys, especially Danny &amp; Sean, had a great time jumping in the bounce houses, sliding down the big inflatable slide and playing on the giant floor pillow. &nbsp;Evan was perfectly happy to sit back and watch all the other kids while bouncing on a little green bouncy ball. &nbsp;He did get out on the giant floor pillow (when there were no other kids on it) to jump around for a little bit. &nbsp;Maybe our second little guy will be a bit more&nbsp;adventurous, like Sean,&nbsp;after watching his big brother! &nbsp;After we were Zoinked out, we enjoyed lunch at Friendly's. &nbsp;All in all, not a bad way to spend an otherwise yucky day!<br />
