This year, for the first time since we have lived in Millbury (just over 6 years!), we attended the annual 4th of July carnival and fireworks and I must say, I was pretty impressed. &nbsp;For a small town like Millbury, they put on a decent carnival (typical to most carnivals around) and a pretty good firework display. &nbsp;Evan had fun riding the train, the ferris wheel and the cars. &nbsp;We enjoyed some carnival food...a dough boy and ice cream...and then took our seats to wait for the fireworks to begin. &nbsp;While we waited, Evan went with Daddy to get a (temporary)&nbsp;<span style="background-color: white;">tattoo and even picked one out for Mommy. &nbsp;A nice little evening not too far from our own backyard! &nbsp;</span><br />
