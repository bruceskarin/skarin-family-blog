Evan had his first school picture day today. &nbsp;If you have been following our boys via Facebook you know that he decided to cut his own hair on Tuesday afternoon, less than 48 hours before picture day! &nbsp;Here is the back story to that haircut. &nbsp;Evan was supposed to be napping on Tuesday afternoon while I was in the basement getting a few things done on the computer. &nbsp;I heard him upstairs playing, I heard the toilet flush and then it was quiet. &nbsp;I figured he wasn't quite ready to nap, needed to use the bathroom and then settled down to sleep. &nbsp;Boy, was I wrong!! &nbsp;After using the bathroom and needing a new pull-up, Evan (using his step stool and potentially something else) got the hair cutting scissors down from the high shelf in the closet in the bathroom. &nbsp;He "needed" them in order to open the new package of pull-ups, even though there were still pull-ups in the drawer in his closet (which I guess he didn't know were there). &nbsp;After attempting to open the package, he proceeded to cut every piece of paper in his room that he could find and also his hair! &nbsp;Now, keep in mind that we had cut his hair about 2 weeks ago in anticipation of picture day so that it wouldn't look "just cut". &nbsp;He cut it so short in some places that in order to fix it we needed to do a super short buzz cut. &nbsp;Good thing he is a boy and that we generally like it short. &nbsp;All in all, no real harm done and his school pictures turned out adorable!<br />
