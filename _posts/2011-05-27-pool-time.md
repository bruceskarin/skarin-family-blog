Nothing like jumping right into summer!  Not that I am complaining.  After having rain and cool weather for much of May, it seems like summer has unofficially arrived, just in time for the holiday weekend!  It is hard to believe that just two weeks ago we had the heat on in the house and then today we are looking for ways to keep cool.  We haven't invested in any outdoor water toys so we had to improvise a little bit but I think Evan still had fun and cooled off anyway.
