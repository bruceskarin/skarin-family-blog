Tonight as Evan was finishing his dinner, he looked out into the dusky sky and said "Ball".  Bruce and I both looked over to see what he was talking about and realized he was seeing the moon.  We explained that it wasn't a ball but it was the moon to which Evan replied "Moon!"  It was so beautiful that Bruce decided to take a few photos and he had a little helper.   <div>
