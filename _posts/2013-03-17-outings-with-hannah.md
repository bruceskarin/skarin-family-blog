In addition to playing in the snow and having lots of fun at home both inside and out, we went on a few fun outings while Hannah was here. &nbsp;We went to Playtown Express, the playground, Irish music class at gymboree, the Boston's Children's Museum, and the New England Aquarium, where Evan and Logan saw their first IMAX 3D movie. &nbsp;Not bad considering we really only had 7 days together. &nbsp;It was fun to show Hannah some of the fun things that we love to do and also to check out some new places together too! &nbsp;We will certainly be going back to the Boston's Children's Museum and the New England Aquarium again!<br />
