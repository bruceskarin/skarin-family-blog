We kicked off April vacation with a play date with the Wheeler's. &nbsp;With everyone's crazy, busy schedules, we don't get to see them as often as we'd like. &nbsp;Evan was really excited to see Derek, Madelyn, and especially Dylan today! &nbsp;He told me that he was "really excited to see Dylan because he's one of the guys in their family that I really like." &nbsp;Too cute! &nbsp;It's so nice to see the kids playing together and having fun. &nbsp;It was a beautiful day so we met at Woolie World for a while then came back to our house for lunch and more playing outside. &nbsp;By the end of the afternoon, everyone was exhausted and no doubt had good naps on the way home. &nbsp;Thanks for a fun day, Wheeler's!<br />
