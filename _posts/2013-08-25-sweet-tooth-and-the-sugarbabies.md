While we were at Jen and Mike's wedding last night, I told Auntie Karin that we should go see Sweet Tooth and the Sugarbabies the next time they are playing at Chelo's Waterfront.  Well that was today!  I wasn't sure if we really had it in us to go and dance the day away again but we did.  It was a beautiful day and it was fun to dance with the boys!  Evan wasn't quite as into it as I hoped he would be (not like<a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/09/good-will-and-them-apples.html"> last year)</a> but Logan sure was...he never stopped dancing all day!
