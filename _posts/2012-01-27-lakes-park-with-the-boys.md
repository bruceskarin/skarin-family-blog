Today the ladies had some long overdue girl time, leaving the boys to fend for themselves. &nbsp;We were planning to go to high tea at the Butterfly Estates but apparently it was booked and "very special" so we simply went for lunch. &nbsp;While it took an extraordinary amount of time to receive four sandwiches and wraps, none of which required cooking just assembling, we had a great time just sitting and chatting and laughing! &nbsp;After lunch, which was quite good despite the long wait, we headed to Beall's to do a little shopping. &nbsp;Shocking I know!<br />
While we were out the boys, big and small, headed to Lakes Park to play on the playgrounds. &nbsp;This, of course, after having popsicles on the patio. &nbsp;While at the park, Papa was interviewed by a local TV station doing a story about the roles of parents and teachers. &nbsp;We still aren't sure why they chose him because he isn't a Fort Myers resident. &nbsp;Maybe it was the bright yellow shirt...maybe he volunteered...yeah, I'm thinking that's it! We all know, Papa isn't shy! &nbsp;I couldn't find the video clip but here is the <a href="http://www.winknews.com/Local-Florida/2012-01-27/FL-lawmakers-propose-school-changes">article</a> for Papa's 15 minutes of fame in Fort Myers. <br />
