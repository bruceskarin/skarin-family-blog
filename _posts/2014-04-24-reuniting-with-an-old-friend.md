With Evan being on vacation from school this week, he had a chance to join Logan and I at Gymboree today. &nbsp;We attended music class with Miss Suzanne and then stayed for play class too. &nbsp;We have been to open play a few times but Evan had said that he wanted to be able to go to the classes again too. &nbsp;Music class was small, only four boys, and the theme was marching music. &nbsp;The boys had fun at both classes and Evan was happy to see Miss Suzanne and join us for music class again. <br />
After class, we had a special school vacation lunch out at Friendly's. &nbsp;At the end of lunch, after ice cream, the boys wanted balloons. &nbsp;All the ones near the door were gone so we asked the manager if there were any more. &nbsp;She returned with two balloons and we headed on our way. &nbsp;While trying to get out the door, on this super windy day, with two little boys and a to-go box, the wind whipped the balloons right off the sticks. &nbsp;I was left with two empty sticks and two heart broken boys. &nbsp;I felt terrible but there was nothing I could do. &nbsp;Lesson learned...balloons and windy days don't mix! &nbsp;(This is actually the second time Evan's balloon from Friendly's was lost because of the wind.)<br />

As I am posting this about 3 weeks after this actually took place, I have to tell another cute story about the balloons. &nbsp;Logan falls asleep in the car quite often for about 10 minutes, especially when we pick Evan up from school. &nbsp;Almost every time that he has woken up in the last few weeks, he looks at me and says "B'loon. &nbsp;Blow 'way. Windy." &nbsp;Poor little guy...I guess this was a bit more upsetting to him than I realized. <br />
