Dear Evan,<br />
I just kissed you good night and tucked you in to bed for the last time before you start kindergarten tomorrow. &nbsp;Tomorrow!!! &nbsp;How can that be? &nbsp;The last five years have flown by, kiddo, but you are ready. &nbsp;You are definitely ready. &nbsp;It's me who isn't! &nbsp;I am so excited for this journey that you are about to begin but part of me is a little sad too. &nbsp;I'm going to miss you! &nbsp;We've been fortunate to have had the last five years together...I'd even go as far as to say that we've been spoiled but I have enjoyed it immensely. &nbsp;Sure there have been moments that I'd like to forget (this morning's incident at Dunkin Donuts even) but overall it's the good times, the happy times that I remember most. <br />
I remember the day you were born and the moment I held you in my arms for the first time and decided that you looked like "an Evan". &nbsp;I remember all the silly faces and noises you used to make when you were just an infant. &nbsp;I remember when you learned to sit up and we would play together on the floor for hours. &nbsp;I remember when you learned to crawl just before we went to see Papa &amp; Gramma in Florida and you demonstrated your crawling on all fours technique. &nbsp;I remember when you started to walk shortly after you turned one which I was quite glad about because I didn't want you to be crawling with shorts on during the summer. &nbsp;All of that was just in the first year of your life!!<br />
There are so many amazing moments from the next four years too. Like when you started talking and it seemed as though you had a new word or two or three everyday. &nbsp;(You pretty much haven't stopped since! &nbsp;Just like momma!) Or all of the adventures we went on together...to the zoo, to the playground, for walks on the bike path, to Papa &amp; Gramma's house, to Washington for Christmas with Omi and Grandpa. &nbsp;So many events both big and small. &nbsp;Meeting your brother for the first time and how you seemed to grow up almost overnight. &nbsp;Beginning preschool and going out into the big wide world for the first time on your own. &nbsp;Learning to ride your bike almost effortlessly after riding the balance bike for a couple of years. &nbsp;Learning to swim in Papa &amp; Gramma's pool this summer...Gramma would be so proud!<br />
And now, my dear child, you are about to embark on your next big adventure. &nbsp;I can't wait to experience school again through your eyes. &nbsp;The love you have for learning is amazing. &nbsp;I hope you never lose it! &nbsp;I hope that you make some wonderful friends. &nbsp;Friends with whom you will travel through school and hopefully have some life-long friendships as a result. &nbsp;I hope that you have great teachers. &nbsp;Teachers who challenge you and understand what makes you tick. &nbsp;Teachers who help you to find your way in this world and who are good role models for you. &nbsp;But most of all, I hope you know just how proud Daddy and I are of you already and how much we love you! &nbsp;You can't measure it, I can't explain it but trust me when I say that I feel as though my heart will burst sometimes from all the love that I have for you! &nbsp;You are going to do amazing things and become a young man that makes us proud...always!! &nbsp;Never forget that! &nbsp;"Kid, you'll move mountains! &nbsp;You're off to great places. &nbsp;Today is your day! &nbsp;Your mountain is waiting so...get on your way!!" &nbsp;I hope you have a wonderful first day of kindergarten, pal! &nbsp;I love you so much!<br />
Love always,<br />
Mom. <br />
