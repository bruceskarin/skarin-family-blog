What started out as a very rough day, ended up being quite the opposite - a delightful spring day with my boys (at least up until the time that I am writing this post - nap time). &nbsp;I came home from boot camp this morning at 6:30 and the first thing I heard was Evan arguing with/yelling at Daddy for some reason. &nbsp;My immediate thought, after the last couple that we have had, was "Oh great, another day like this." &nbsp;We got Evan settled into his morning routine...TV, milk and "snacks" (cereal in a snack container)...and everything was fine...for about 30 minutes. &nbsp;Logan woke up, I plopped him in our bed next to Evan and went to get him a bottle and more milk for Evan. &nbsp;Fed Logan and went back to drying my hair. &nbsp;It was at that point that I heard Evan say "No, Logan" immediately followed by Logan crying. &nbsp;When I asked Evan what had happened, his response was "I punched him". &nbsp;I wasn't in the room so I have no idea how hard but that isn't the point. &nbsp;Evan needs to learn that his recent behavior of hitting Logan and pushing him over is not acceptable. &nbsp;Needless to say, the TV was immediately turned off and Evan was off to his room for a time-out. &nbsp;He kept coming back out of his room, screaming and crying which resulted in him losing his Legos and his box of blocks. &nbsp;And this was all before 7:30!<br />
He finally got it together, calmed down, we chatted about his poor behavior and the rest of the day has been smooth sailing! &nbsp;We had a good breakfast, went to music class, had a great lunch, painted and played outside until nap time. &nbsp;There was <strike>no</strike>&nbsp;a minimal amount of fussing, no major meltdowns and no arguing. &nbsp;I am so thankful that he turned it around so that we could enjoy this beautiful spring day! &nbsp;Let's hope the afternoon behavior and not the morning behavior continues!<br />
