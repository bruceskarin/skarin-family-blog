Today I took Evan to the dentist for the first time. &nbsp;I had made the appointment a few weeks ago after the tooth fairy visited school and delivered brushes, paste and floss for all the kids. &nbsp;He was quite excited about it. &nbsp;I made the appointment for a day that Bruce would be working from home so that I could just take Evan and not have to juggle Logan too. &nbsp;Well, as luck would have it, Bruce needed to go into the office today so off we went, in the middle of Logan's prime nap time, to the dentist. &nbsp; I wasn't sure how it would go but I was hoping for the best. <br />
And the best is exactly what I got! &nbsp;The boys were so good! &nbsp;Evan climbed right up into the hygienist's chair and wasn't scared at all, a bit curious but not scared. &nbsp;He did everything she asked him to do, opened his mouth super wide, rinsed and spit like a pro, and was just perfect for Dr. Dave too! &nbsp;Logan sat on the tooth chair for a bit and then wanted me to hold him. &nbsp;He just looked around, checked everything out and took it all in. &nbsp;It was certainly a proud Momma moment for me! &nbsp;Great to know that our hard work is paying off!! &nbsp;Plus, Evan is part of the "No Cavity Club"! &nbsp;Who says the dentist has to be something to be afraid of?<br />
