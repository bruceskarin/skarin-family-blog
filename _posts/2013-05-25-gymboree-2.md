Is today a Gymboree music day? &nbsp;No but it certainly looks like it thanks to Omi! &nbsp;On Tuesday when we came home from the doctor's office there was a package waiting for us. &nbsp;The package was filled with percussion instruments similar to those that we use at music class. &nbsp;A wave drum, rhythm sticks, bells, maracas, wood blocks, a rain stick...so much fun! &nbsp;This is especially great seeing as music class will be ending in just a few weeks. &nbsp;Now we can have music class at home when ever we want to! &nbsp;Thanks, Omi!<br />
