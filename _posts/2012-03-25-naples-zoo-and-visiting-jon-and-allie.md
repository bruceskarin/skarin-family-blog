Today, after getting a little bit of a late start, we went to the Naples Zoo. &nbsp;After having a quick lunch, we began our tour of the zoo to find most of the animals hidden in their homes or napping. &nbsp;We began to think our trip to the zoo was going to be a bit of a bust. &nbsp;Then, just as we were approaching the lion's den, the lion began to roar. &nbsp;Evan thought he was the Lion King! &nbsp;We continued on past the camels and saw the giraffes eating. &nbsp;We decided the best way to make the most of our visit was to attend the shows and the "meet the trainer" talks. &nbsp;Our favorite was the tiger feeding...they are such big, beautiful animals! &nbsp;We took another tour through the zoo areas we had missed, hoping to catch the last boat ride to see the monkeys. &nbsp;No luck! &nbsp;I guess we will have to go back another time!<br />
<br />
After the zoo, we went to Jon and Allie's house for our annual "salmon on Sundays" dinner with them. &nbsp;We hadn't seen them since September and I think they were amazed at how much Evan had changed in six months. &nbsp;Evan had a wonderful time playing with Frankie and Fergie...he even took Frankie for a walk, holding the leash all on his own! &nbsp;He also loved running around the lanai and exploring Jon and Allie's yard. &nbsp;It is so nice to be able to visit two very special people when we are in Fort Myers!<br />
