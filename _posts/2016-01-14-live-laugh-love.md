<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">Today my brother asked to be a guest writer on our blog...Who am I to say no? &nbsp;Here is what he had to say. &nbsp;Love you, Dan!</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><b>Live, Laugh, Love!</b></span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">One year ago today our Mom passed away. &nbsp;As we remember the most wonderful person that has come&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">into our lives this is a rough attempt to recreate the (off the cuff) toast that I gave in her honor one year&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">ago...</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">I'm sure that you've all seen the LIVE, LAUGH, LOVE signs and pictures that are in every home décor&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">section of a local retailer. &nbsp;Until recently I hadn't given these items much more than a glancing thought. &nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">I liked them enough and even thought that it is a nice moniker to live by. &nbsp;But by the time I left the isle&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">or clicked on the next website that was the end of it.</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">That was until I started reflecting on our Mom's time with us. &nbsp;I believe that the very simple phrase&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">LIVE, LAUGH, LOVE describes exactly how she wanted us all to approach our lives and I like to&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">think that she was the epitome of the slogan.</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">LIVE!</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">Mom struggled for 35+ years with a rare condition called pulmonary hypertension (PH). &nbsp;At the time&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">she was diagnosed, the medical community didn't know too much about the disease and only a few&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">years earlier the World Health Organization made a first attempt to classify the affliction. &nbsp;In the '70s&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">the survival rate for patients with PH was about 2 years. &nbsp;Anything greater than that and you were&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">lucky. &nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">However, if you spent any time with our Mom over the course of the past 3 decades you would not&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">know that she struggled to catch her breath almost daily. &nbsp;You would not know that the strain of&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">climbing the stairs was something that stressed her out. &nbsp;You would not know that simply lifting her&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">grandsons was something that worried her because she was concerned that she wouldn't have the&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">strength to hold them too long.</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">She wouldn't let you see these things because she was too strong willed to let you. &nbsp;She wasn't about&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">making excuses for not doing something because of her condition. &nbsp;PH didn't control her life she&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">OWNED her PH. &nbsp;She fought it every single day. &nbsp;Some days it got the best of her. &nbsp;But over the course&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">of the many (wonderful) years, she KICKED ITS ASS!</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">She LIVED! &nbsp;She enjoyed time with her family, friends, kids, grandsons, and adoring &amp; loving&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">husband. &nbsp;Together our Parents would travel, entertain, go out to dinner, watch Red Sox games, or just&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">about anything that the rest of us do. &nbsp;She fought for every single experience, dream, and goal. &nbsp;And she&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">did it all! &nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">I'm sure that she had some regrets. &nbsp;Our Dad could tell you better than I. &nbsp;But let's face it, what's better&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">than spending each day with your best friend and love of your life? &nbsp;THEY LIVED each and everyday&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">in sickness and in health! THEY LIVED!</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">LAUGH!</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">Some people say that laughter is the best medicine. &nbsp;Mom loved to laugh! &nbsp;She laughed with great joy&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">when her grandson's blurted out something silly. &nbsp;She laughed anytime she got together with friends or&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">family. &nbsp;Her smile was enough to melt the frost off your windshield and warm your heart on a bad day. &nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">She laughed until she coughed. &nbsp;On bad days she would have to be careful not to get too excited, be too&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">strenuous, and overall take it easy. &nbsp;But she always laughed. &nbsp;She knew that laughing would cause a&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">coughing fit or make it difficult to catch her breath. &nbsp;Laughter was her great indulgence especially when&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">it came to her grandson's. &nbsp;She didn't want to miss a second with them and cherished each moment too&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">much not to be there even if they did make her laugh.&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">Every day our Parents would dream. &nbsp;They'd dream about the family that they were going to have, or&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">the house they were going to live in, or the vacations that they would have, or that someday I would&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">move out (for good), or that they would spend a month in Florida, or buy a second home for their&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">retirement. &nbsp;Each day they dreamed. &nbsp;They dreamed big, they dreamed small. &nbsp;But each dream was built&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">on their love and fueled by laughter. &nbsp;They smiled, winked, held hands, pinched each other's butt, but&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">they laughed and laughed. &nbsp;THEY LOVED, THEY LAUGHED.</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">LOVE!</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">Mom had love in her heart. &nbsp;It didn't matter if you were someone that she'd just met or had been life&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">long friends you felt loved. &nbsp;She had a gentleness about her that could put you at ease with a look, a&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">smile or a kind word. &nbsp;I believe that she truly cared for everyone because she loved her life so much&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">and felt so blessed that she wanted to share that love with everyone.</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">She was the best remedy for a bad day. &nbsp;She was warmth when your soul was cold. &nbsp;She had love to&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">share with you even when you were so down that you had little to share back.</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">In her eyes, she had so much to be thankful for. &nbsp;We've been blessed with a great big family and she&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">made everyone feel loved no matter how much time passed since you last saw or spoke with her. &nbsp;That&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">same love was given to her closest friends whom she considered family.</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">Her greatest love was for Dad. &nbsp;They were practically kids when they first met. &nbsp;When they were&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">married their love was young, infections, and strong. &nbsp;As time passed, their bond became stronger and&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">their love grew to be the most powerful force I've seen. &nbsp;She loved and adored Dad each and every day! &nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">Sure they had their struggles. &nbsp;Mom was diagnosed with a life threatening disease when she was 25! &nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">They had a young son and a daughter on the way. &nbsp;But never once was their love for each in doubt. &nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">Love was the emergency generator that kept them going through the rough days. &nbsp;It was the reason that&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">they did so much for those around them. &nbsp;Their love conquered all! &nbsp;They knew that each moment was&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">precious and few and that they should not be wasted.</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">Mom was strong. &nbsp;She was a fighter. &nbsp;She had a giant heart. &nbsp;She was beautiful. &nbsp;She was generous. &nbsp;She&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">was so many things...</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">Very early on Mom knew what was going to cause her pain, struggles, and take her life. &nbsp;She never&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">knew when her last moment would be. &nbsp;So in every moment she would LIVE, LAUGH, and LOVE! &nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">As you go about your daily life don't forget to LIVE it to its fullest, LAUGH often and fiercely, and&nbsp;</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">LOVE unconditionally. &nbsp;With any luck, you could be as blessed as Deborah Kavanagh was.</span><br />
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;"><br /></span>
<span style="font-family: Georgia, Times New Roman, serif;">We love you Mom and miss you each and everyday!</span>
