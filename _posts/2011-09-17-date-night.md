Tonight was the first time that we left Evan alone with a babysitter. &nbsp;We have left him before with either family or with a babysitter for Danny, Sean and Evan but never alone, one-on-one. &nbsp;We went out to celebrate our 8th anniversary and had a very nice night. &nbsp;We were out for about an hour when Katy, our babysitter and a former student of mine, sent me a text message to let me know that Evan was ok but was crying every 15 minutes or so. &nbsp;From her messages, I knew he was fine and that Katy had enough experience to handle it. &nbsp;We continued with our dinner and headed home when we were ready. &nbsp;We got home to find Evan running around the living room having fun, with no pants on - he wouldn't let Katy change him. &nbsp;Truth be told, I was actually kind of glad he was still up...I missed him! &nbsp;Not bad for the first time with a sitter!<br />
