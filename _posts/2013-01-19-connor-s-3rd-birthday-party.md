What started as a disaster of a day, ended up being a fun one. &nbsp;All before 11 o'clock we had a 2 AM wake up feeding, a crooked hair cut and a cut ear, a blow out diaper, a bloody nose, a stained carpet and no gift for a sweet little boy for his birthday. (I ordered it but it didn't arrive in time and of course it was on our front steps when we arrived home.) &nbsp;Thankfully I knew the Cenotti's wouldn't care if we showed up without a gift today and so we went to the party anyway. &nbsp;A disaster of a day immediately turned into a great day as we were able to spend the afternoon with some of our favorite people, those crazy Gilbreath's, celebrating Connor's third birthday. &nbsp;Plus this way, we will have to get together again soon so that we can get Connor's gift to him. &nbsp;Evan had a great time playing the XBOX Kinect and all the kids loved Logan. &nbsp;Happy 3rd birthday, Connor!<br />
