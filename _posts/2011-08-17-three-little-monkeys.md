Three little monkeys went to Roger Williams Park Zoo today.  Their names are Keaney, Kingston and Evan!  We saw some of Evan's favorite animals...elephants, giraffes and monkeys (gibbons).  The giraffes were so close, it seemed as though you could reach out and touch them!  After the zoo, we had a nice picnic lunch in the park.  These little monkeys had a fun day!  So did their moms!
