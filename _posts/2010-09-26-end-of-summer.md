It has been a pretty low key week since we returned from Myrtle Beach.  We have been spending time at home, getting a few things done and enjoying the last few days of summer.<br />
Evan has his 4 month visit to the pediatrician (a little late seeing as he is 4 ½ months).  He continues to be a growing boy.  He is 18 lbs and 9 ounces (93rd percentile), 27.5 inches long (98th percentile) and has a 17.5 in head circumference (91st percentile).  The doctor continues to be thrilled by his growth and progress.  Evan had his second round of shots and while he had the slightest fever you would never have known it from his demeanor.  He was just as happy as ever!<br />
<br />
We also visited two more (our final two) daycare facilities this past week and have narrowed down our choices to either of the first two that we visited.  Funny how we ended up liking those two the best…just like we did when we were planning our wedding.  So now we will have to see who has openings for January.<br />
<br />
Yesterday we went to an engagement party for Mike and Jill.  It was a great party as it always is at the Pullano’s and it was great for the Kavanagh side of the family to finally meet Evan.  Unfortunately we don’t have any photos of the day.  I guess there was just too much going on…at least 50 people and that’s not even counting all the little kids.  It was a typical Kavanagh gathering…lots of fun and great food with wonderful people!  Congrats, Mike and Jill!  Can’t wait until the wedding!!<br />
