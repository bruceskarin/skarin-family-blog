During the planning of this surprise birthday trip, we had talked about how much fun it would be to rent a pontoon boat and cruise around on the canal for a while. &nbsp;We had decided that today was our best option so we made reservations, got packed up and heading out on the water. &nbsp;Dan had said "Keep in mind, last time we did this it was October, not January, so it might be chilly." &nbsp;And boy was he right!! Thankfully we had run back in at the last minute to grab sweatshirts and long sleeves, otherwise we would have frozen! &nbsp;We spent 4 hours cruising around, taking in the sights and having fun. &nbsp;The boys, both big and small, took turns driving the boat, sometimes getting us stuck! &nbsp;We saw tons of pelicans resting in trees, lots of dream homes and a pirate ship. &nbsp;What a great way to end a wonderful trip!<br />
