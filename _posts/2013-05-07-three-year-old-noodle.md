When I was three I told my parents that I had a "three year old noodle (brain)" and "helping hands"...I guess that was my way of telling them that I was a big girl who could do things on my own. &nbsp;Evan has been saying things like "I think I'm ready for that (insert big boy toy/activity/anything here) now" and "I think I'm a bigger boy now". &nbsp;I guess this is his way of telling us that he is getting big and doesn't need our help as much anymore. &nbsp;This is true in many ways but also not in many ways (Thankfully! &nbsp;If he didn't need me anymore by the age of three, I'd be crushed!). &nbsp;We have watched him grow and change so quickly in the last few months and weeks. &nbsp;It has been quite amazing! &nbsp;He really is a "bigger boy" now who is gaining more and more independence with each new day that passes. &nbsp;I love seeing each new stage and know that he won't be little forever. &nbsp;I try to cherish our time together and am so glad that he still likes to snuggle and watch TV or read with me...I hope that never ends! &nbsp;Happy birthday, pal!<br />
