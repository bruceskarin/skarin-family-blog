A couple of weeks ago while at the playground at Auburn High School, I got to talking with a woman who was asking about other playgrounds that we visit. &nbsp;She asked if we had been to Woolie World. &nbsp;I didn't think we had but then after talking with her more, I realized that it was the playground that we walk to in Millbury. &nbsp;She said they had redone it, painted it and added on so we decided to check it out again (we hadn't been there since this spring). &nbsp;We went last week and were so impressed that we decided to meet Isabelle and TJ there again today for a play date and picnic lunch. &nbsp;The big kids had so much fun playing with the rubber mulch and going down the twisty slide over and over again. &nbsp;Nice to take advantage of Jenna's extended maternity leave while we can!<br />
