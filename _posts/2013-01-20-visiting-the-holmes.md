Today we finally did something that we have been talking about doing for years...We went to visit Ethan and Stacey and the girls. &nbsp;We planned to get together a few weeks ago but we were sick with the stomach bug so we had to reschedule. &nbsp;It was wonderful to see where they live and to see Sofia, who is getting so big, and to meet Emily, who is just as cute as her big sister. &nbsp;Evan loved playing with all of Sofia's toys, it isn't very often that he gets to play with many girly things, and Sofia loved being "Mother Hen" to Evan...it was quite cute! &nbsp;We didn't get to see Emily too much because she was napping for most of our visit. &nbsp;Guess we'll just have to do it again soon, especially seeing as we left a bit early to get home for the Pats game (which they lost)!<br />
