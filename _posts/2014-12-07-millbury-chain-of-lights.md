Each year the town of Millbury hosts the Chain of Lights. &nbsp;It is an event where many local businesses host holiday activities for families to enjoy as they stroll through town on a Sunday early in December. &nbsp;In the 8+ years that we have lived in Millbury, we had never attended until today. &nbsp;We started with lunch at the Pizza Chef where they had a live band playing and singing Christmas carols. &nbsp;Next we stopped by to see the Boy Scouts on the common who were making s'mores and giving away hot chocolate which was much appreciated on this cold day. &nbsp;After taking a horse drawn sleigh ride through town, we stopped in at a local dentist's office for some popcorn and a balloon. &nbsp;Next we went to the Asa Waters Mansion to visit with Santa and Mrs. Claus and to hear the reading of <i>The Night Before Christmas. </i>&nbsp;We finished up our day with a stop at one of our favorite places in town, the library. &nbsp;We only stopped in at a few of the many places but we had a great day!<br />
