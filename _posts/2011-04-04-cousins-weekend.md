This was a cousins filled weekend!  We hosted a Kavanagh cousins Appetizer Throw-Down on Saturday night and had a sleepover with Uncle Dan, Auntie Karin, Danny and Sean.  The appetizer throw-down was full of fun, great company and wonderful food.  Congratulations to Christi who took home the first place prize with her fancy deviled eggs, with a second place finish going to Scotty and his fried ravioli.  On Sunday, we went to the Ecotarium in Worcester.  I had been wanting to go for a while now and thought it was a great thing to do on an early spring day with Danny, Sean and Evan.
