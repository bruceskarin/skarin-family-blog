We have had some spectacular fall weather this year. &nbsp;Mild temperatures, lots of sun, little rain. &nbsp;So much so that when we were on vacation, I checked a few times to see if my weather icon was showing temperatures for Millbury or the Florida Keys. &nbsp;Today was another beautiful day! &nbsp;We played outside for a while after running to the grocery store, mostly riding bikes and just enjoying the day! &nbsp;Cooler, rainy weather is on its way later this week...I wish we could just hang on to this weather until spring...guess we'll just have to enjoy it while we can!<br />
