Over the last year, Logan has changed so much. &nbsp;I don't feel like I remember Evan changing so quickly or so much but I'm sure he did too. &nbsp;Maybe I'm just more focused on it because I don't have a little one coming along behind Logan and because I know he is the last one. &nbsp;Here are a few of the gems that he has come out with lately.<br />
<br />
- "Mommy, thank you so much! &nbsp;You made it perfect!" in reference to his ham and cheese sandwich that I made him for lunch. &nbsp;He was so excited about it that he got down off of his stool at the island and gave me a great big hug.<br />
<br />
- "It's hard to think about, right Mom?" when he couldn't remember his music teachers name.<br />
<br />
- "Logan, do you have the moves like Jaggar?" "Nope, they aren't in my brain anymore."<br />
<br />
- "Mommy, are these my eyebrowns?"<br />
<br />
- "So Elsa and I were talking. &nbsp;She's going to come over to our house, play Paw Patrol, and sleep in my room, OK? &nbsp;That sounds like a good plan, huh?" when getting in the car after school last Thursday.<br />
<br />
Logan is such a sweet, sweet boy with so much personality. &nbsp;He is silly, spunky, goofy, and so appreciative of everything. &nbsp;Clean clothes, washing his blankets, getting him a sweatshirt, getting him a snack. &nbsp;Each time I get the biggest hug and most sincere thank you for helping him.<br />
<br />
I love you, little guy! &nbsp;Don't get too big too fast, ok?<br />
