We all know that Evan loves to sing and dance. &nbsp;Twice on our trip to Florida, Evan put on his dancing shoes and entertained us all. <br />
<div style="text-align: left;">
The first time was Friday night. &nbsp;We went to the Gulf Coast Town Center after dinner to hear some live music. &nbsp;We weren't really sure who was going to be there performing but Gramma knew that Evan would love it. &nbsp;It turned out to be Richie C. doing classic oldies and Papa and Gramma knew the words to every song. &nbsp;It took Evan a little while to warm up and at first he only wanted to dance with Daddy but eventually he came around and was quite the little entertainer.</div>
<div style="text-align: left;">
Saturday night Evan put on his dancing shoes again and had a great time dancing at the Beachwalk end of the year celebration. &nbsp;He danced and danced and danced. &nbsp;A couple staying at Beachwalk even recognized him from the night before at the Gulf Coast Town Center! &nbsp;The female performer gave Evan a tambourine to play and he loved it! &nbsp;He kept going back for more. &nbsp;Evan was, as he generally is, the life of the party! &nbsp;I think he made all the grandparents at Beachwalk miss their grandkids!<br />
