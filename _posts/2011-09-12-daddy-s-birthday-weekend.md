This past weekend we celebrated Bruce's birthday with not one but two parties. &nbsp;Friday night we had many of our WPI friends over. &nbsp;It was a little cool but still nice enough to be outside on the deck and playing cornhole, life sized Jenga and beer pong in the grass. &nbsp;We had a fire and a few people used the hot tub. &nbsp;Evan was his usual self, entertaining everyone with his animal noises and jumping jacks. &nbsp;I guess we were having too much fun though because we didn't take any pictures. &nbsp;Oh well...next time!<br />
Sunday we hosted a second party, this time with my family. &nbsp;It was again a beautiful day so we spent most of it on the deck. &nbsp;Gotta soak up every minute we can because we all know what is coming...winter! &nbsp;Yuck! &nbsp;It was a relaxing day and we all enjoyed watching the boys play together. <br />
Tonight we planned to have a special salmon dinner just the three of us but Evan had other ideas! &nbsp;He was getting into everything as we tried to cook dinner, including the wine glasses off of the wine rack. &nbsp;Hard to believe that he was playing with glasses again seeing as he broke one yesterday with a Weeble. &nbsp;While we were in the kitchen, Evan managed to grab a glass, hit it against another glass and bottle of wine, and then try to get it into his mouth. &nbsp;It seemed that the more Bruce and I yelled "No!", the faster Evan moved. &nbsp;Luckily I got to him just before he put the glass in his mouth. &nbsp;He did end up with a small scrape on his chin but it could have been so much worse. &nbsp;Needless to say, this set the tone for the rest of the evening and Bruce and I ended up eating our salmon dinner in shifts. &nbsp;Not quite how we planned but still a memorable night!<br />
