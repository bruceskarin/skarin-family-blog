For the last few years, we have been participating in the St. Pat's 5K road races in some capacity. &nbsp;Sometimes both Bruce and I run, sometimes just Bruce (I was pregnant with Evan) and sometimes just me. &nbsp;Today I ran the Celtic 5K in Pawtucket with my girlfriend, Nicole. &nbsp;I thought I was just going to go, run and come home but then the boys decided to come and cheer us on. &nbsp;It was a chilly day and not my best run but still nice to see some familiar, smiling and super cute faces as I neared the finish line. <br />
