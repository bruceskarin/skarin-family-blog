<div style="text-align: left;">Today we had a big day and spent the day with Jon and Allie!!  We first met them for lunch at Arizona pizza and then went to Lakes Park after that.  It was a mostly cloudy day but perfect weather for walking around the park.  We have driven by the park hundreds of times but have never gone...until today.  It is huge!  There are quite a few playgrounds, both wet and dry, a miniature train, lots of boats and bikes for rent and a nice boardwalk.  Evan had his first swing ride and his first train ride too.  So much to do...we will definitely have to go back again when Evan is older!  We will also be sure to let Uncle Dan and Auntie Karin know about it too...Danny and Sean would love it!</div><div style="text-align: center;"><iframe allowfullscreen="" frameborder="0" height="390" src="http://www.youtube.com/embed/uTN1rsfpf8I" title="YouTube video player" width="640"></iframe></div><br />
After the park, we went back to Jon and Allie's house for salmon on Sunday.  Bruce went to return the car because it smelled musty and Evan had a great time playing with Frankie and Fergie, John and Allie's dogs.  We had a nice relaxing evening enjoying each others company and the warm Florida air.  It was great to see Jon and Allie, as it always is.  We are so glad that they were able to meet Evan and are looking forward to spending more time with them on our future annual visits to Fort Myers!<br />
