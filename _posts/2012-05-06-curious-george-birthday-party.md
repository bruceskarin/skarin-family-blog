Today we celebrated our little monkey about to turn two with a party themed by another little monkey, Curious George. &nbsp;Curious George and Evan have been best buddies for the last two years and have shared lots of experiences together. &nbsp;Evan would take George everywhere he went if we let him. &nbsp;Even if George isn't there "in person" to share an experience, Evan tells him all about it. &nbsp;When planning his second birthday party, I was originally thinking of a Cars and Lightening McQueen theme but then I thought if we were ever going to have a Curious George party, this was the year. &nbsp;Who knows...next year Evan might be too old for George. <br />
We tried to keep it to a reasonable size so as not to overwhelm our little guy. &nbsp;We had eight little ones, ages 5 and under, as well as 13 adults. &nbsp;Thankfully the weather was nice enough that the kids could spend most of the afternoon outside. &nbsp;Evan was tired from all the playing and needed to nap for a couple of hours in the middle of his party so that he could enjoy his monkey cupcakes and open his presents. &nbsp;Thank you to everyone who attended and shared Evan's special day!<br />
