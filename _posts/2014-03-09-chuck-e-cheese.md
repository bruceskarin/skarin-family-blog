Today we celebrated Danny's 7th and Sean's 4th birthdays at Chuck E Cheese with 30 of their friends. &nbsp;It was our first Chuck E Cheese experience with the boys and while it was a bit of sensory overload from all the lights, noises, games, music, etc, we all had a great time. &nbsp;Logan was happy to just sit on quite a few of the rides and even played a few games. &nbsp;Evan tried out most of the games and liked the pirate one the best (so did Daddy!). &nbsp;The birthday boys were running around all afternoon, having fun with their friends and cousins. &nbsp;I don't think I even saw Sean until we had been there for at least 20 minutes! &nbsp; &nbsp;After the party, we went back to Danny and Sean's house to watch them open their gifts. &nbsp;It looked like Christmas all over again...lucky boys! &nbsp;A fun day celebrating two very special little guys who are getting big really fast! &nbsp;(Unfortunately, I didn't get a ton of photos of everyone but there are a few sprinkled in there.)<br />
