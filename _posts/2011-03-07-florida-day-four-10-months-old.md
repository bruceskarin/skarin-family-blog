Is it me or does every month seem to go by faster and faster than the last?  Evan is ten months old today!  And what a way to celebrate...with another beautiful and fun day in Florida.  We spent most of the morning and early afternoon by the pool again (gotta take advantage of this beautiful weather!) and then went to Pincher's Crab Shack for dinner.  After vacationing in Fort Myers so many times, we have quite a few favorite restaurants that we always visit.  This trip I especially wanted to go to the Pincher's on the way to the beach because I knew Evan would love it.  There are all sorts of things hanging from the ceiling and I knew he would enjoy looking at everything.  Sure enough, I was right.  Papa even taught him how to say "Wow"!
