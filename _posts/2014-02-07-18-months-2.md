Logan turned 18 months old today! &nbsp;One and a half already!?!? &nbsp;When and how did that happen?? &nbsp;Too quickly that's for sure!! It's hard to believe that 18 months ago we were just getting to know our little guy. &nbsp;Now I can't imagine my life without him. &nbsp;He has brought so much love and joy into our life and filled each day with laughter, love and smiles. &nbsp;I haven't been as good as keeping up with milestones for him like I was for Evan but here are a few.<br />
<br />
Started crawling: 11 months<br />
Started walking: 14 months<br />
Started talking: Around 13-14 months, Logan had a few words...maybe 7-10 total, and mostly things that I could understand but that strangers wouldn't be able to decipher. &nbsp;Today I made a list of 60+ words (see below) that he has and uses on a daily basis. &nbsp;Some are clear as day and others still only mommy and daddy can understand but he can certainly communicate quite easily. &nbsp;He will be talking up a storm before we know it.<br />
Clothes: Logan is outgrowing most of his 18 month clothing and getting into 24 months and 2T. We go to the pediatrician in 2 weeks and I will be interested to see how he is growing and if he is still neck and neck with Evan stats from the same age. <br />
Likes: Some of Logan's favorite games are peek-a-boo (which never gets old); knock, knock jokes; ABCs; counting, especially gold doubloons; singing and dancing; and doing "Cheers" at meal time. Evan and Barky are still Logan's best pals even when Evan doesn't let him play quite the way he wants to. <br />
