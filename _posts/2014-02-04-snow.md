We've had a good amount of snow this year but it has been a bit difficult to get out and play in it very much. &nbsp;It has either been bitter cold or there has been too much snow for Logan to be able to walk and therefore enjoy playing in the snow. &nbsp;Today we was a day between storms. &nbsp;About 3-4 inches fell yesterday and tomorrow there is another 10 or so coming. &nbsp;We took advantage of the warm (almost above freezing) weather to get out and enjoy for a while today. <br />
