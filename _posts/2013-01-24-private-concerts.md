Evan has been putting on performances for us recently. &nbsp;He is always entertaining us but here are two of his more "formal" performances. <br />
<br />
This first one was one night just before we were ready to have dinner. &nbsp;Evan insisted that we go into the living room so that he could put on a show for us. &nbsp;He instructed us where to sit and then began his karate/dance show. &nbsp;Later on that night we all had a dance party, dancing to "Daddy's song", Some Nights by Fun.<br />
