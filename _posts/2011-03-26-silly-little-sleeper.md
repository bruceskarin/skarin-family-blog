Before we left for Florida, Evan had just started crawling and wasn't pulling himself up on furniture except for once or twice when he was highly motivated.  That all changed quickly over the last few weeks.  We knew that we needed to do some baby-proofing including dropping the mattress in his crib and were planning to do that once we came home.  Friday night (the 18th) we had put Evan to bed and he was settling down for a while but then started getting worked up again.  Bruce went upstairs to see if he could calm him down and found him standing (yes, standing!) at the railing of his crib closest to the bedroom door.  We had not yet dropped the mattress so the railing was about waist height!  Yikes!!  So needless to say we immediately dropped the mattress and put him back to bed.  Since that night we have been finding him in a variety of new positions at night and in the morning.
