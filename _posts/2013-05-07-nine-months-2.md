Dear Logan,<br />
You are 9 months old today! &nbsp;You are sharing this big day with your brother who turned 3 today! &nbsp;It seems like just yesterday that we were meeting you both for the first time and now so much time has passed. &nbsp;You are already such great friends and I cannot wait to continue watching that special bond grow. &nbsp;My hope for you both is that you will always remember who your very first best friend was and that you remain close throughout your entire lives. <br />
<br />
You are not crawling quite yet but you are schooching more and more everyday. &nbsp;I bet you will be just like Evan and crawl just before you turn 10 months old. &nbsp;Your top two teeth are slowly coming in but you don't have any more on the bottom quite yet. &nbsp;You are always smiley and flirty and have the cutest little side bend/ head twist when you are really turning on the charm. &nbsp;You switch from rolling over like a champ to forgetting how and just kicking and screaming everyday. &nbsp;It is hard to believe that you will be one in just three months!<br />
I love you, little one!<br />
Love, Momma.<br />
