The Smucker's arrived from Montana on Thursday and with them they brought what will hopefully be the last snow storm of the 2013 winter...a 2-day, 18+ inch storm! &nbsp;It started snowing on Wednesday afternoon and didn't stop until late in the day on Friday! &nbsp;We kept thinking we would wait for the snow to stop before taking the kids out to play but it quickly became apparent that it wasn't going to stop anytime soon. &nbsp;So Friday afternoon, we bundled them up and sent them out side with Daddy/Uncle Bruce and Aunt Kristina/Mama. &nbsp;They went sledding across the street and started building a fort. &nbsp;Once they had had their fill, they kids came in while Daddy cleaned up the driveway and Aunt Kristina finished the fort. <br />
Saturday was a fun filled day in the snow. &nbsp;It was a beautiful, warm, sunny day so the snow was quickly melting but it stuck around long enough so that the kids could have lunch in the fort, build a snowman and play all afternoon. &nbsp;But we have had our fun so as far as I'm concerned it can all melt now and not come back until next winter!<br />
