Today was my first attempt at making baby food for Evan.  Until now he hasn’t had anything besides formula, rice cereal and bananas.  When we saw Dr. Gonroff last week she told us we could start fruits and orange veggies so we decided to start with carrots.  Plus we already had plenty of them in the fridge.  As you can tell from the photos, we can add carrots to the list of foods that Evan likes!<br />
