Today we celebrated a very special first birthday for the little love bug that we call Logameister! &nbsp;Happy first birthday, little guy! &nbsp;How is it that time seems to pass faster and faster with each week, month, year?? &nbsp;We wanted to keep the party small and simple but still special for Logan so we only invited immediate family and didn't go crazy with a theme. &nbsp;When I think about Logan and what he likes...well, anything that Evan likes! &nbsp;But he does have something special all his own, his "welcome to the world" gift from his big brother and best snuggle buddy, Barky! &nbsp;For this reason, I decided to make a Barky cake. &nbsp;I'm no Cake Boss but I think it turned out pretty good. &nbsp;We had a wonderful day, enjoyed the beautiful weather, made it inside before the rain, and celebrated our little boy who is getting too big too fast! &nbsp;Happy birthday, Logan! &nbsp;We love you!!<br />
