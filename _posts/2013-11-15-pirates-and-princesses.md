Back in August, my friend Jenna told me about a Disney Jr. show that was coming to the Hanover Theater in Worcester. &nbsp;It was Jake and the Neverland Pirates and Sophia the First. &nbsp;We all know how much Evan loves Jake and he is also a big fan of Sophia. &nbsp;Bruce and I decided that we wanted to take him but knew that Logan would never sit through the show. &nbsp;We decided to purchase 4 tickets and take Evan and Sean while Grammy got some one-on-one time with Logan. <br />
Well today was the show. &nbsp;Neither Sean nor Evan knew where we were going. &nbsp;We went to the 99 for lunch before the show and then went to the theater. &nbsp;We told them when we were parked that we were going to a Jake show. &nbsp;They were both really excited!<br />
It was a great show! &nbsp;Complete with Doc McStuffins, Minnie and Mickey, Cinderella and Peter Pan, all of whom Evan adores. &nbsp;Sean took a little bit to warm up but once he did, both boys really enjoyed the show. &nbsp;It was as much fun as I had hoped it would be. &nbsp;I just loved watching their faces as they watched the show. &nbsp;What a special day spent with two special three year olds!! &nbsp;This is what being a mom and auntie is all about...making memories!!<br />
