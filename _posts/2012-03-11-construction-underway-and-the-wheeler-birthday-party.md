In order to make room for our growing family, we will be moving Evan into a big boy room, hopefully sometime around his 2nd birthday. &nbsp;As a result, we will be moving the "office" into the basement, which is something we have talked about doing for a while. &nbsp;Bruce is currently working two days a week from home so not having an office is not an option. &nbsp;Plus if we can finish the basement, it will not only add value to the house in the long run but also make that a more usable space for us. &nbsp;We had two contractors come and give us a quote for the work we are planning to do and unfortunately the quotes were a bit higher than we would have liked. &nbsp;Thankfully, Bruce is really handy and is able to do most of the work by himself...or should I say with the help of friends and family! <br />
Before getting started on finishing the basement, we (and I use the term very loosely) needed to fix the crack in the foundation that occasionally allows water to seep in. &nbsp;Bruce started on this, not so fun project this weekend. &nbsp;It worked out nicely that Evan and I could get out of the house for the day and go to the birthday party for the Wheeler's so that we could just let Bruce work. &nbsp;Evan loves to "help" daddy but unfortunately he is not much help right now. &nbsp;Wait a few years, kiddo, there will be plenty to help daddy with! &nbsp;Stay tuned for more updates and photos on our (really Bruce's) progress! &nbsp;(Please ignore the mess in the basement. &nbsp;We have already started making room and moving things around.)<br />
