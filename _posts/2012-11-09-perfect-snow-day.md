In my opinion, today was the perfect snow day. &nbsp;The sun was shining brightly and felt warm. &nbsp;There was enough snow to play in but not so much that you were up to your knees when walking through it. &nbsp;It was warm enough to stay outside for a while making putting on all of the snow gear worth the time and effort. &nbsp;We built a small snowman, a snow castle, made snow angels and had a snowball fight! &nbsp;Logan even came out to join us for a few minutes. &nbsp;The snow was melting as we played so it was a little wet for Evan's little knit gloves but all in all, it was a great day to play in the seasons first snowfall!<br />
