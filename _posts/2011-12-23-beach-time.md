If you are from the Northeast, you probably don't think much about going to the beach in December. &nbsp;Unless of course you are boarding a plane before you go or are longing for the days of summer to return as you watch the snow fall. &nbsp;Well I know two toddlers who had tons of fun exploring the beach on the Puget Sound in a different sort of way...one that required rubber boots (thanks to Omi!), winter coats, mittens and digging tools. <br />
