I knew it was going to hit me and hit me hard, I just didn't know when. <br />
<div>
<br /></div>
<div>
The days before Mom passed were long and hard, filled with worry, tears, heartache and sadness. Strangely enough, however, the days after weren't as hard as I expected them to be. &nbsp;We made the arrangements for Mom's funeral rather quickly and for the next few days we were fully in planning and organizing mode. &nbsp;The day of the funeral came and I was dreading it. &nbsp;I had visions of myself being a total mess all day. &nbsp;Instead, I was overcome with such love and support that I could never have imagined. &nbsp;So, so many people came to pay their respects to Mom and to express their sympathy to us. &nbsp;Childhood friends, former co-workers, extended family, in-laws, high school and college friends, family and life long friends. &nbsp;It was amazing to see and experience such love! &nbsp;Everyone telling stories of Mom or saying how sweet, kind, beautiful, loving, caring...the list goes on and on...she was. &nbsp;It really made the day much easier than I expected it to be. &nbsp;Even Sunday and Monday, after the service, after we'd said goodbye for the last time, were easier than I expected. &nbsp;We watched the Patriots win the AFC championship game together at Dan and Karin's on Sunday. &nbsp;Then Monday, I helped Dad sort through some bills, receipts and paperwork, before having late lunch with Dan. &nbsp;We were all together, remembering, crying, laughing and smiling.</div>
<div>
<br /></div>
<div>
Then it came...Monday night, Tuesday, Wednesday.... &nbsp;Things were returning to "normal". &nbsp;People were heading back to work and school, there was nothing left to plan, life was moving on. &nbsp;No way! &nbsp;Not in my book! &nbsp;How could I be expected to just pick up and move on? &nbsp;No one was asking me to pretend that this was all just a bad dream, although I wish it was, but it was time to start moving forward a little bit and accepting this "new reality". &nbsp;Well it sucked! &nbsp;I spent much of last week in a fog. &nbsp;Wandering around aimlessly, having so many things to do but not being able to accomplish any of them. &nbsp;Simple tasks were seemingly impossible. &nbsp;I was still experiencing a huge outpouring of love from so many people which helped but the days and nights were quite challenging. &nbsp;</div>
<div>
<br /></div>
<div>
After spending part or all of everyday for nearly three weeks in Rhode Island with Mom, Dad and Dan, I was home with the boys and Leni on Tuesday and Wednesday. &nbsp;Thursday was Leni's last day in MA so I went to RI to help Dad a bit more without distractions. &nbsp;Just being with Dad helped me so much. &nbsp;There are times when I'm not sure if we are good or bad for each other because we are both missing Mom so much but I also know that's just what we need right now. &nbsp;He's the closest thing I have to Mom right now so I'm holding on tightly!</div>
<div>
<br /></div>
<div>
Thursday night when Bruce took Leni to the airport was extremely difficult for me. &nbsp;Leni had been here for two weeks and was a godsend! &nbsp;She took care of so many things...cooking, cleaning, laundry, bathing...Not to mention taking such great care of Evan and Logan in our absence. &nbsp;I don't know what we would have done without her help. &nbsp;But now that she was heading home to WA, it was like the last thing that was returning to "normal". &nbsp;</div>
<div>
<br /></div>
<div>
Friday was ok. &nbsp;Evan had school and Bruce dropped him off so I could have time to get the day started a bit slower. &nbsp;When I picked him up, we headed to Papa &amp; Gramma's house for the afternoon and evening. &nbsp;We had a nice visit with Glenn and I think it helped Papa to have the boys there for a while. &nbsp;</div>
<div>
<br /></div>
<div>
The weekend was tough again. &nbsp; With a to-do list miles long and an inability to focus on anything, the time slipped away. &nbsp;I was missing Mom so much and just couldn't bring myself to continue as if nothing had changed. &nbsp;</div>
<div>
<br /></div>
<div>
Monday, Mom's birthday, was easier again. &nbsp;Dad came up to help out with the boys so that I could attend Evan's teacher conferences. &nbsp;In the afternoon, I helped Dad with some online banking, something that is totally new to him. &nbsp;Monday would have been Mom's 63rd birthday and while I was sad that she wasn't there with us, the day wasn't as hard as I would have thought it would be. &nbsp;</div>
<div>
<br /></div>
<div>
I guess that is the lesson in all of this. &nbsp;There will be good times and bad, there will be happy times and sad. &nbsp;Day by day, step by step, we will all find a way to carry on. &nbsp;We will never quite be whole but we will never forget for one minute the love that we shared. &nbsp;</div>
