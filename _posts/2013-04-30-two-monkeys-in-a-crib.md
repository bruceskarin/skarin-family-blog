Today when Logan woke up from his nap, Evan and I went up to his room to get him up, as we always do. &nbsp;But today I picked Evan up and had him give Logan kisses while Logan was still lying in his crib. &nbsp;Then I dropped (gently placed actually) Evan in the crib with Logan. &nbsp;The boys had fun just being silly in the crib for a while. &nbsp;Logan loved having his big brother in his bed and it is hard to believe that Evan was sleeping in that same crib just one year ago. &nbsp;He has gotten so big! &nbsp;Silly boys!<br />
