This month was all about having fun and being silly.  Evan started walking a few weeks ago and hardly ever crawls anymore.  He is cutting more teeth, his molars, which isn't too much fun but Evan still spends the majority of his time being a goof ball.  We have so much fun together and are really enjoying the nice weather.  Evan is talking up a storm and loves to make animal noises.  He repeats just about everything we say.  Guess we better make sure our language is baby appropriate from now on!
