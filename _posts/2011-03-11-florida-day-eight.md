Well after one day of cool weather and rain, we are back to sunshine and blue skies today.  It was still a bit cooler than it had been earlier in the week but nice enough to head back to the pool to test out Evan's new boat and turtle.  After spending the majority of yet another day in the sun, Bruce and I went out on our second date night in one week!  We went to Doc Ford's for dinner and then to The Cottage.  Both places had live music that we enjoyed singing and dancing along to.  Thanks for another night of baby sitting, Papa and Gramma!  We sure could get used to this!!<br />
