If you are familiar with the children's book <i>Blueberries for Sal, </i>then you know what it was like picking blueberries with Evan today. &nbsp;We had been waiting for a cooler day so that we could go blueberry picking with Gramma. &nbsp;Well today was that cooler day that we needed. &nbsp;Evan carried his own bucket and once he had about 10 berries in his bucket, he ate them all. &nbsp;Then he started picking them and eating them before ever even putting them in his bucket, feeding some to Gramma and Mommy too! &nbsp;And finally he started eating them right off of the bushes! &nbsp;I know I shouldn't have been laughing and encouraging him but it was just too cute!<br />
