And boy was she busy!! &nbsp;We went to Franklin Park Zoo today because we heard that Doc McStuffin's and the Doc Mobile were going to be there. &nbsp;We arrived at the zoo a little later than planned for a few reasons...a late start due to sleeping in a bit, road closures and traffic jams due to the Dominican Parade in Jamaica Plain, and every toddler in MA wanting to see Doc. &nbsp;We had a quick lunch before locating the Doc Mobile. &nbsp;Once we found it, it was clear that there was no way we were getting in! &nbsp;She was only there until 4 pm and by 1:30 they had already closed the line because it was over 3 hours long! &nbsp;I thought there was going to be a major meltdown when we told Evan that we couldn't see Doc but he was totally fine with it. &nbsp;Instead of waiting in line for 3 hours, we enjoyed the zoo, the playground and even had a ride on the carousel. &nbsp;In general, we weren't super impressed with the zoo. &nbsp;There were many animals off exhibit but the gorillas were really cool and the boys LOVED the playground! <br />
