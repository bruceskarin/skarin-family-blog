Christmas was, as it always is, busy and full of good food, great company and lots of spoiling! &nbsp;We always try not to get too crazy but without fail it always happens. &nbsp;I think the best sign of this was about halfway through gift opening on Christmas Day when Sean said "No, I'm good" when we told him it was his turn to open a gift next. &nbsp;Shortly there after Evan, Sean and Daddy/Uncle Bruce were watching Curious George on the droid. <br />
We did Christmas in three parts this year...Christmas Eve, Christmas Day and the day after. &nbsp;We couldn't fit everything in with our schedule of trips back and forth to RI but it was actually nice to have a low key day on Wednesday to leisurely open the rest of our gifts. &nbsp;I think it's safe to say that we thoroughly enjoyed Logan's first and Evan's third Christmas!<br />
Before Christmas, Evan was really excited about a Batman costume and a Batman car. &nbsp;Santa brought a Batman costume and a small Batman car but the big hit so far seems to be the trumpet. &nbsp;Evan's response to the small Batman car was "Um, I think Santa forgot to bring me a big Batman car. &nbsp;Silly Santa!" &nbsp;Guess Santa will need to do better next year!<br />
