Today a surprise, months in the making, finally happened and it was perfect!! Tomorrow is Gramma's 60th birthday and we all (Bruce, Evan and I and Uncle Dan, Auntie Karin, Danny and Sean) flew down to Florida to surprise her! &nbsp;And boy was she surprised!!<br />
I had told Bruce that I wanted to go to Florida for my mom's birthday last spring and then mentioned it to Dan as well. &nbsp;When Dan and Karin decided to make that the time for their family trip to Fort Myers, it only made sense for Bruce and Evan to join me as well. &nbsp;We had been sending emails and IM's and making secret phone calls for months and all the planning paid off!<br />
Dan, Karin and the boys flew from PVD to TPA and then drove to RSW to pick us up. &nbsp;When we arrived at the condo, the ladies (Mom, Marsha and Jan, who was also visiting) were out and on their way home. &nbsp;We hung out at the condo with Papa waiting for them to arrive and to reveal the big surprise! &nbsp;And it was perfect!! &nbsp;Gramma was thrilled to have us all there and was secretly hoping we would all be there to help her celebrate her birthday. &nbsp;This was definitely the best birthday present we could have given her! &nbsp;Can't wait for the next 5 days of fun in the Florida sun!<br />
