It's that time of year again...the holidays are here! &nbsp;Thanksgiving was early but we decided to get a tree and start decorating this weekend anyway. &nbsp;The beginning of December is busy for us with a bachelor party, Bruce traveling, and then a weekend away at Mohegan for Mommy and Daddy. &nbsp;For our tree this year, we decided to cut it fresh from a local tree farm in hopes that it would last a bit longer than normal and not lose so many needles. &nbsp;It was a chilly day but we found a tree before we were too cold. &nbsp;I think this may be a new tradition for the Skarin's.<br />
