This was the first official weekend of summer and we kicked it off in style! &nbsp;Daddy was going camping in NH with the guys so it was just Mommy and the boys for the weekend. &nbsp;Well, sort of! &nbsp;Gramma called on Friday and said it was supposed to be a beautiful, pool weather weekend and wanted to know when we were coming down. &nbsp;I figured we would go on Saturday at some point and likely stay over but we ended up heading down just after lunch on Friday. <br />
We spent Friday afternoon in the pool, enjoying the weather and drinking some Del's. &nbsp;Logan enjoyed taking a ride in the covered turtle while Evan took just about every pool toy out of the cabana to test out. &nbsp;Saturday morning, the boys and I met up with Uncle Dan, Auntie Karin, Danny and Sean. &nbsp;We started the day at the Build &amp; Grow workshop at Lowe's and then went to the summer reading kickoff at the Warwick Public Library. &nbsp;It was a fun event with lots of activities for the kids. &nbsp;After that we went back to Papa &amp; Gramma's for lunch, naps and more time in the pool! &nbsp;We spent Sunday by the pool with everyone, including Grammy, Bumpa and even Daddy! &nbsp;Bill dropped him off in RI instead of at home on the way back from camping. &nbsp;And to round off the weekend, we stopped for ice cream on the way home! &nbsp;What a great start to the summer!!<br />
