For the past couple of years, Uncle Dan, Auntie Karin, Danny and Sean have participated in the <a href="http://www.riparks.com/RIOutdoorsPursuit/">Great Outdoors Pursuit</a> in RI. &nbsp;It sounded like something that would be fun for Evan and so today we decided to join them and check it out. &nbsp;We didn't have anything else going on and it was a nice day to spend outside at Lincoln Park. &nbsp;The actual event wasn't quite what I expected and was quite crowded but we still enjoyed ourselves. &nbsp;I don't know that we will attend all events this summer but we might check out another one or two if we can. &nbsp;It is always fun to do something a little different and Evan had fun. &nbsp;After the event, we met Gramma, Papa &amp; Grammy for lunch. &nbsp;It was the first time all 11 of us had been together since Christmas!!<br />
