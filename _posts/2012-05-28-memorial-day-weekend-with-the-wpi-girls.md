We had a busy weekend with some of mommy's good friends from college. &nbsp;The Green's had planned a trip to MA from England and so we convinced (it wasn't really that hard to do) the Kempler's to come for a visit from DC. &nbsp;Evan and I spent last Monday at the Green's beach house on Plum Island with Nicole. &nbsp;It wasn't your typical beautiful beach day but it was still nice enough to be outside and for the kids to play in the sand and the water. &nbsp;Evan had a great time playing with Henry and Charlie, who is just a bit too little to really play with the bigger boys.<br />
On Friday, we hosted a BBQ for 7 little boys and their parents, as well as Adria, Avi and Nicole. All of the boys played well together even if it was tough at times for Evan to understand that not everyone plays with his toys the same way as he does. &nbsp;Once the sun came out and it was warm enough, we filled up the water table, which all the boys enjoyed. &nbsp;Adria, Avi, Nicole, Mommy, Daddy and Evan enjoyed a nice relaxing evening by the fire that included s'mores! &nbsp;It was the first time we had a fire when Evan was still awake and while it made me a little nervous, he had a great time!<br />
Saturday, we and our house guests had a lazy start to the day before heading back to Plum Island for another gathering at the beach house. &nbsp;It was a hot and humid day, perfect for spending the evening on the beach. &nbsp;Again, Evan enjoyed playing with Henry who has the cutest little English accent. (Charlie was already in bed this time.) &nbsp;After dinner, the boys had fun playing on the deck, running around with toys and wearing silly hats! &nbsp;We really wish that the Green's and Kempler's lived closer to us because it is always so much fun when we can see them! &nbsp;Such a wonderful weekend with great friends!<br />
