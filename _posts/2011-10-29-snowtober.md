This year we had a freak snow storm in October, before Halloween! &nbsp;We were inside all day on Saturday watching the storm until later in the afternoon when we decided to take Evan out to play in the snow. &nbsp;We had record amounts of snow last winter but Evan was really too young to enjoy it much and then there was too much to even really play in it, at least for a 9 month old. &nbsp;We all got suited up...not a quick event...and headed out to play. &nbsp;We pulled Evan along in the <a href="http://youtu.be/0tHtQBz_WMo">sled</a>&nbsp;(listen for his laugh around 6 seconds), <a href="http://youtu.be/8EfKOp8VpUU">ate some snow</a>&nbsp;(I know, I know...I'm a terrible mother to laugh...but come on, it was funny!) and <a href="http://youtu.be/j34zgNLTc7c">built a snowman</a>...everything all in half an hour! &nbsp;As far as I'm concerned, we've had winter and we can go back to 70's and sunny but I think Evan would beg to differ!<br />
