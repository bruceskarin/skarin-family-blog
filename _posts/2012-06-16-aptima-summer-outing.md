It was 75 and sunny with a beautiful blue sky when we were preparing to leave our house today and head to the Aptima summer outing in Georgetown, MA. &nbsp;We drove about halfway there and the clouds began to increase as the temperature dropped. &nbsp;When we arrived, it was completely cloudy, with no promise of seeing the sun for a while, and in the low 60's. &nbsp;Thankfully I remembered that it is generally pretty breezy where the event is held and I packed sweatshirts and windbreakers. &nbsp;We were really bummed because the facility has a nice pool and spray park for the kids that Evan would have absolutely loved but it was much too chilly for that today. &nbsp;Instead, after having lunch, we made use of the other fun things...basketball (Bruce and I actually played a game of PIG - I couldn't tell you the last time I shot a basketball), volleyball, the bounce house, and the playground. &nbsp;Evan loved the big curvy slide! &nbsp;He wasn't interested in getting a temporary tatoo...until we were already on our way home...and we did play in the water for a few minutes before heading home for the day. &nbsp;Even though I think the weather kept some Aptimists away, we had a nice day!<br />
