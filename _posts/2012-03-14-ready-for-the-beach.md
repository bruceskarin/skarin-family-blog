I think Evan is getting really excited about our upcoming trip to Florida to see Gramma and Papa. &nbsp;He already has a to-do list for his vacation.<br />
1. Build a sand castle with Papa<br />
2. Go to the beach with Gramma<br />
<br />
This morning&nbsp;when I asked him what he wanted to do today,&nbsp;one of the first things he said to me was "Go to Fla-di-da?" Shortly there after he picked up a little bucket and shovel and said "Dig sand with Papa." &nbsp;I think he's ready!<br />
