When we moved into this house 7+ years ago, we had great visions for the back yard. &nbsp;Much of the immediate yard was already in great shape but the jungle behind the house was more than we wanted. &nbsp;We have made some progress over the last 7 years but not nearly as much as we would have liked. &nbsp;This year I finally put my foot down and decided there would be enough work to do once the land was cleared and that it made sense to have someone come and do the clearing for us. &nbsp;Yesterday the machine was dropped off and today the work was completed. &nbsp;Evan, as you can see, loved sitting in the excavator and both boys enjoyed watching it work! &nbsp;Now the fun of getting the land ready and actually planting begins...what have we gotten ourselves into??? &nbsp;Maybe it was better just being a jungle?? &nbsp;Stay tuned for after pictures...coming...who knows when!!<br />
