If you are a loyal reader, you are probably familiar with our holiday traditions. &nbsp;This year we stayed in MA instead of going to WA so that means that tonight we were at Aunt Linda and Uncle Louie's in Cranston, RI. &nbsp;It is an amazing celebration with food galore and people every where you turn. &nbsp;This year we were missing one very special lady, Gigi! &nbsp;While it was difficult to know that she wasn't there, it is comforting to know that she is at peace and is back with Papa, celebrating together and looking down at the wonderful legacy that they left behind. &nbsp;I think I'll let the photos speak for themselves mostly but as always it was a great night with so many of our loved ones!<br />
