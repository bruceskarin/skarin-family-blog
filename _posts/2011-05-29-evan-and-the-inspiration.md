On the way home from visiting with Chuck, Clare and Savanna, we took advantage of being in upstate New York and stopped in to visit more friends.  This time we were visiting the Kiblers - Kodi and Stella (Jeff was sleeping from his overnight working!).  Kodi is the inspiration for Evan's blog.  While I have only met Stella once, I have been able to watch her grow up through Kodi's blog and I love it!  I hope some day her creativity will rub off on me a bit more!  We had a great afternoon visiting with them.  Stella was a wonderful hostess, sharing all of her toys with Evan.  She also made sure that Evan had his milk and his shoes at all times!  So cute!  Thanks for a great visit (and lunch), Kodi and Stella!
