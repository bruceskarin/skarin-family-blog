<div class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent: .5in;">As a result of needing a C-section, our hospital stay was extended until Tuesday.&nbsp; Luckily for Bruce, the bed in the maternity room was an actual cot not one of those convertible chair-beds.&nbsp; We spent 4 nights and 3 ½ days at the hospital recovering and figuring out how to take care of Evan.&nbsp; Neither Bruce nor I had had much experience with newborns prior to Evan but we seemed to figure things out pretty quickly.&nbsp; Especially Bruce, as much of Evan’s care was his responsibility for the first couple of days due to my limited mobility.&nbsp; Bruce, as I knew he would, fell quite naturally into the role of Daddy and was changing diapers and feeding Evan as if he had been doing it for years.&nbsp; I think it certainly helped that Evan was so good and relatively easy to take care of!&nbsp; </div>

<div class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent: .5in;">On Saturday, I was in quite a bit of pain and wasn’t feeling up to having many visitors so I asked my parents to let everyone know that we would prefer people wait until Sunday to visit.&nbsp; We did get a visit from Uncle Dan and Aunt Karin, who were so excited to meet their first “official” nephew and also from Gramma and Papa.&nbsp; Also, thanks to technology, Evan was able to meet Omi and Grandpa Bob who were at home in <st1:place><st1:placename>Washington</st1:placename>  <st1:placetype>State</st1:placetype></st1:place>.&nbsp;</div>

<div class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent: .5in;">Sunday was Mother’s Day and I had a wonderful day with Evan.&nbsp; After feeding him in the morning, he took a long nap in my arms.&nbsp; He was quite alert at other times throughout the day and I explained to him that nothing could ever top this Mother’s day!&nbsp; He set the bar quite high and I know that each Mother’s day will be special as I will always remember becoming a family of three just two days before my first Mother’s day.&nbsp; In the evening, while I was holding Evan, he picked up his head, turned towards me and gave me a look that just melted my heart.&nbsp; It was then that I truly knew just how much I already loved our little man!&nbsp; </div>

<div class="MsoNormal" style="text-indent: .5in;">On Sunday afternoon, Bumpa, Aunt Chris, Uncle Glenn, Aunt Chris and Uncle Bob all came to meet our special man.&nbsp; We also video conferenced with Aunt Kristina, Uncle Ty and Cousin Hannah.&nbsp; Hannah seemed excited to meet Evan and kept saying “Baby, baby.”&nbsp; It was rather cute!&nbsp; We also received many congratulatory phone calls, text messages and emails.&nbsp; It is wonderful to know that we have so much support and that Evan is so loved already!!</div>
