With the mild winter and the warm early spring weather that we had, it is hard to believe that this week was the first time that we spent anytime by Papa &amp; Gramma's pool. &nbsp;Evan and I spent the day poolside on Thursday while Bruce was in DC. &nbsp;It was a perfect day and Evan, while a little cautious, enjoyed the water.<br />
Today was a typical summer Sunday - everyone at Gramma &amp; Papa's for a fun afternoon by the pool with lots to eat. &nbsp;Evan was already more adventurous in the water today than he was on Thursday. &nbsp;He was even going down the slide, from the top, all by himself! &nbsp;I, thankfully, was in the house during this because it would have made me too nervous. &nbsp;We are hoping that this nice weather is here to stay so we can have many more fun summer days at the pool!<br />
