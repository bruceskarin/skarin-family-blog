After a weeks worth of projects around the house, I decided that this weekend was going to be a fun one. &nbsp;To kick it off, today we went to Roger Williams Park Zoo. &nbsp;Evan and I had been last summer with <a href="http://evan.skarin.us/2011/08/three-little-monkeys.html">Amy, Keaney and Kingston</a> but Daddy, Omi and Grandpa had never been. &nbsp;It was a beautiful day and it wasn't too crowded, despite the fact that mom's were admitted for free. &nbsp;There were a few extra events in honor of Mother's Day, including a DJ near the food court and some games for the kids. &nbsp;Evan loved dancing, trying to hula hoop and also played the bean bag toss game. &nbsp;Mommy found some money, which was exciting and will make date night tonight even more fun!<br />
