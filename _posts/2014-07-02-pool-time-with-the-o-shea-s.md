After 4 straight beach days, we needed a little change of pace. &nbsp;We took this opportunity to visit the O'Shea's, who happened to be in Mashpee this week as well. &nbsp;We went to their house for lunch, pool time and lots of fun! &nbsp;They boys had a great time with the O'Shea kids. &nbsp;Evan especially loved playing hide and seek with Zoe, who was like a little mom to Evan while we were there. &nbsp;He even said that his favorite part of the day was getting hugs from Zoe before we left. &nbsp;So cute! &nbsp;Thanks for hosting us, O'Shea/Gabrielian family! &nbsp;It was great to see you all!<br />
