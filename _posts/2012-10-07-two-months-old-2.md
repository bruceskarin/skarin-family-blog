Logan is two months old today. &nbsp;He is a growing, happy boy who sleeps through the night (6-8 hours inconsistently) and eats like a champ. He is eating about 30-35 ounces per day and still naps much of the day. &nbsp;Logan is becoming more and more alert everyday and loves to watch his big brother run around the house. &nbsp;We go to the pediatrician on Thursday so check back for a size update. &nbsp;He is growing out of most of his 3 month clothes so I'm sure he will be off the charts! &nbsp;And as you can see from the pictures, he loves to talk, smile and giggle! &nbsp;We sure are lucky!<br />
