Dear Logan,<br />
Today you are 8 months old and you are the droolyist little guy ever! &nbsp;You wear a bib all the time and we are still waiting on more teeth, which must be coming soon. &nbsp;You had a month full of firsts - your first flight, your first trip to the beach, your first time in a pool, lots of new foods (all of which you enjoy), your first time meeting Uncle Ty, Auntie Kristina and cousin Hannah. &nbsp;You also had a chance to get reacquainted with Papa &amp; Gramma and Omi &amp; Grandpa (only for a short time) while we visited them and they visited us, respectively. &nbsp;You are beginning to form more of a sleep schedule with a morning nap generally between 9 and 10, an afternoon nap between 2 and 3, &nbsp;and you almost always wake up at 6:30 and go to bed at 8. &nbsp;You are still quite an easy baby to care for as long as you are fed, (relatively) dry and rested. &nbsp;Each day you discover new toys and figure out how things work. &nbsp;It is hard to believe that time is going so quickly but we love you more and more each day. &nbsp;We love the little person that you are becoming and look forward to lots of fun that is just around the corner.<br />
Love, mom.<br />
