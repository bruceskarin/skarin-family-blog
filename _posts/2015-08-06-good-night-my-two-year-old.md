Dear Logan,<br />
I just kissed you good-night for the last time as a two year old! &nbsp;It is hard to believe that tomorrow you will be three! &nbsp;Where has the time gone?!?! &nbsp;I feel like I just wrote your <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2015/05/logans-two-year-letter-almost-9-months.html">two-year old letter</a>...well, maybe because I did! &nbsp;I promise to try to do better from here on in. &nbsp;I can't promise that I will be perfect but I will certainly do the best that I can for you...always!<br />
Three years ago today, I spent the day with Evan at Southwick's Zoo. &nbsp;It was a beautiful day and the last time that I was the mother of just one little guy. &nbsp;I can't believe how much our life has changed in the last three years and how much love you have brought to our family. <br />
You are so full of love, life and spunk. &nbsp;Always smiling and being super silly while at times stubborn and stern. &nbsp;I love the little person that you are becoming. &nbsp;So smart and sweet, always with a big hug and kiss that could make anyone's heart smile, especially mine. &nbsp;I love coming home from a workout, tutoring or a 5 minute run to the store to be greeted by you with a great big smile, hug and kiss. &nbsp;You always act as if I've been gone for hours and like you missed me beyond belief. &nbsp;It makes this Momma feel very special and quite loved. <br />
You love your time with Daddy and Evan doing boy things. &nbsp;Riding the tractor, watching shuttle launches, building with Legos and getting dirty. &nbsp;While you love pushing Evan's buttons, (and you know exactly where they ALL are!), you also love playing together and are such good friends. &nbsp;I hope that never changes. &nbsp;You truly believe that you can do anything that he can do...from the "flying squirrel" at Chris &amp; Erin's wedding to swimming in Papa &amp; Gramma's pool without your Sharky to climbing the rock ledge behind our house. &nbsp;No one is going to tell you "No" or hold you back. &nbsp;I love the confidence that you have and hope you never lose it.<br />
Today, we spent the (rather windy) day at the beach and I loved watching you and Evan play in the sand together. &nbsp;I am trying to hold on to these moments, especially this summer, before Evan starts Kindergarten and you start pre-school in just a few weeks. &nbsp;You are going to miss your best buddy while he's at school and I am going to miss you both. &nbsp;5 hours all to myself...what will I do??? &nbsp;Probably just think about you and Evan the whole time. <br />
Tonight as I was putting you to bed, you gave me a great big hug and kiss and said "Thanks for being my best pal today!" &nbsp;And then when I started to cry, you had more kisses and hugs for me. &nbsp;It makes me sad to know that you will be 3 tomorrow because Gramma never knew you as a 3 year old. &nbsp;You have changed so much this year and I just know how much joy she would have had in watching you learn, grow and change. &nbsp;She would be so proud of you for so many reasons, just as we all are. &nbsp;I love you, little buddy! &nbsp;Happy birthday, pal! &nbsp;(One day early! &nbsp;Woohoo! &nbsp;Go me!)<br />
Love always,<br />
Mom<br />
