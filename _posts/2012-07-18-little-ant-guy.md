We have been having a little issue with carpenter ants this season. &nbsp;It was first bad in the spring when everything was coming alive again but Bruce set traps and sprayed and it seemed to mostly take care of the issue. &nbsp;Last Tuesday morning, however, I woke up to find ants everywhere! &nbsp;In every room, on every surface...everywhere!! &nbsp;I don't know where they were coming from and why all of a sudden but something needed to be done. <br />
Wednesday we had a pest control guy come and spray around the house. &nbsp;For a few days we only saw a few ants here and there but then it started picking up again. &nbsp;Last night, it was as if we never even did anything! &nbsp;We called our pest control guy again this morning and he came to investigate again. <br />
Both times, Evan was very curious as to what "the ant guy" was doing and where he was. &nbsp;Last week, "the ant guy" was mostly outside but today he was inside quite a bit more. &nbsp;Evan noticed that he had a bluetooth ear piece which prompted him to want one too. &nbsp;He then went around our room, on the hunt for ants!<br />
Hopefully we won't need to see "the ant guy" again!<br />
