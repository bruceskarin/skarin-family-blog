When Bruce and I went to Mohegan Sun at the beginning of December for the Dave Matthews Band show, we stumbled upon Dan's Place in West Greenwich. &nbsp;It was a pizza place where we decided to stop for lunch that is so much more than a pizza place. &nbsp;At our visit we received a coupon for a future visit that was in a sealed envelope and for anywhere from 10-50% off your total bill. &nbsp;Today, we went for lunch with Uncle Dan, Auntie Karin, Danny, Sean and Bumpa. &nbsp;Unfortunately, Evan was just getting over a cold, Logan was just coming down with it and Uncle Dan was still sore from his hernia surgery so we didn't stick around after lunch but it was still a nice afternoon with good food. &nbsp;We will go back for sure!<br />
