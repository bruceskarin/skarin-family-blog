Seeing that tomorrow is my birthday and it's a Monday and I'm tutoring from 5-8, I wanted to do something fun as a family today. &nbsp;Unfortunately, neither one of us had any really good ideas, nor did we really give it any thought until yesterday afternoon. &nbsp;While researching some options, we were leaning towards the Providence Children's Museum. &nbsp;Something fun for Evan and not too far or super expensive. &nbsp;We called Uncle Dan and Auntie Karin to see if they wanted to join us and even though they were planning to stay home and get a few things done, they did join us! &nbsp;We decided to meet at Fat Belly's for lunch and then head to the&nbsp;museum. &nbsp;We, amazingly, were a few minutes early so we took Evan to the Splash show at Jordan's Furniture. &nbsp;It was pretty cool and certainly the right price...free! Then we went to lunch and heading on to the children's museum. &nbsp;Everyone enjoyed the museum, especially the big water tables! &nbsp;Such a fun family day...just the way I wanted to spend the day!<br />
