Dear Evan,<br />
Happy second birthday! &nbsp;I can't believe how quickly the last two years have flown by and how much you have changed in the last year. &nbsp;You started walking just after you turned one and never looked back. &nbsp;Now you scamper, run, gallop, and jump everywhere you go. &nbsp;You are so full of life and energy that it often amazes me, especially on those days where you eat next to nothing. &nbsp;Where do you get your energy from? <br />
You love to play outside. &nbsp;You explore the yard, looking for rocks, branches and leaves. &nbsp;You dig in the dirt, veggie garden and rock gardens with your shovels and rakes. &nbsp;You look under the deck for hidden treasures. &nbsp;You help mom weed and help dad dig up roots. &nbsp;You love to blow bubbles and draw with chalk on the patio. &nbsp;You are daddy's little helper when he does work on the cars, in the yard and especially when he mows the lawn!<br />
You love to play with tools, both ones designed for you and Daddy's. &nbsp;Everyday you fix something with your drills, hammers, wrenches, and screwdrivers. &nbsp;You share your tools with mom, dad and George so that we can help fix too. &nbsp;Sometimes you use the nails and screws from your tool bench and toolbox. &nbsp;Other times you use pretend ones.<br />
You love to play with balls of every kind...plastic balls, ping pong balls, soccer balls, basketballs, footballs, baseballs, bouncy balls. &nbsp;You throw well enough that we are starting to limit the balls that we can play with in the house. &nbsp;You kick and run after the balls sometimes but you prefer to hit the balls with paddles (just like daddy!) or with a bat. &nbsp;You can catch a ball when one is thrown to you and you hit quite well. You love to play golf at Papa &amp; Gramma's house. <br />
You also love to sing, dance and entertain! &nbsp;You have two guitars, a keyboard piano and two microphones that you play with every day. &nbsp;You often turn other objects into microphones as well. &nbsp;You like to drum on the floor, buckets and your legs while you sing and dance around. &nbsp;Your favorite songs are Wheels on the Bus; ABCs; Twinkle, Twinkle; Itsy Spider; and Happy Birthday but you know quite a few others as well. &nbsp;When we drive in the car, if the radio is off, you ask us to turn it on. &nbsp;Or if there is a commercial, you request music. &nbsp;You often sing along (with your own song) to whatever we have on at home or in the car. &nbsp;You love, love, love to dance and jump around when music is playing, always entertaining everyone around.<br />
You like Mickey Mouse Clubhouse, Winnie the Pooh, Lightening McQueen and some other Disney movies but you LOVE Curious George! &nbsp;He is your best pal! &nbsp;You snuggle him when you sleep and at various other times during the day. &nbsp;You feed him, play ball with him and take care of him like we take care of you. &nbsp;You give him good night kisses and read to him before you put him to bed.<br />
You are a pretty good eater, although you don't always like to take the time to sit and eat. &nbsp;You eat better in the morning and at lunch than you do for dinner. &nbsp; You love fruit of all kinds, especially raspberries. &nbsp;You like veggies - frozen peas and carrots, broccoil, green beans, cucumbers and tomatoes are your favorites. &nbsp;You eat a fair amount of dairy including yogurt, cheese and milk. &nbsp;You drink a gallon of milk every 3-4 days by yourself!<br />
You are a very sweet little boy, with a great big brain and heart. &nbsp;You show great concern for others when they are upset or hurt by sharing the things that make you feel better...a tissue, a blanket, and often even George. &nbsp;You give wonderful kisses and squeezes to most anyone who asks, and even often when they do not. &nbsp;Your&nbsp;vocabulary is quite advanced for a two year old which makes communicating with you often quite easy, even if there are still times when you cry and get upset rather than simply asking or telling us what you need. &nbsp;You say some of the cutest things and make amazing connections between things you see and hear. &nbsp;You can count to ten, although you prefer 1, 2, 3, 9, 10 blast off! And can recite your ABC's from A to Z with just a little help. &nbsp;You remember so much of what you see and hear, even if you only see or hear it one time. &nbsp;We try to teach you as much as we can and explain things to you in a way that you can understand. You amaze us every day!<br />
You have changed our lives in so many ways that I can't even begin to explain. &nbsp;(You will understand when you have children of your own.) I told dad just the other day that this is such a fun age for you that I almost wish we could stop time for a little while. &nbsp;I am so proud of all the things you can do and hope that I am taking good enough mental notes so that my memories don't fade over time. &nbsp;I love to watch you learn and figure things out with that big brain of yours. &nbsp;I hope you never lose your desire for knowledge. <br />
While part of me wants to stop time for a little while and keep you this age, part of me is so excited for all that is to come. &nbsp;I know that with every new day, there will also be a new learning experience. &nbsp;I know that this summer will be full of fun and excitement. &nbsp;I know that you will continue to be a wonderful friend and cousin and that you will soon be the best big brother to your little brother. &nbsp;I see in you so many wonderful things for your future and while part of me is a little sad that you are already growing up and gaining independence so quickly, another part of me is so excited for all that is to come! We love you more than you will ever know and will always be here for whatever you need...always!<br />
Happy birthday, sweet boy! &nbsp;Thank you for the most amazing two years! &nbsp;Can't wait to see what the next one brings!<br />
I love you!<br />
Love always,<br />
Mom.<br />
