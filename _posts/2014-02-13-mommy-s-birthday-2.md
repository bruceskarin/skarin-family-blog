When I heard that we were going to be getting yet another snow storm on my birthday, I was not happy! &nbsp;Partly because I don't want anymore snow and partly because that would mean we were stuck in the house all day. &nbsp;I wanted to take Evan to school, take Logan to music class, maybe go out for lunch or dinner. &nbsp;Do something a little special to "celebrate". &nbsp;Well none of that happened and I'm glad it didn't! &nbsp;We had such a fun day being snowed in together, doing not much of anything other than enjoying our time together. &nbsp;Who needs to celebrate when you have two amazing little boys who think you are the best mom in the world? &nbsp;Thank you, Mother Nature, for "ruining" my birthday plans!<br />
