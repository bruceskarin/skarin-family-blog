Hi Mom! &nbsp;It's me. &nbsp;We're here! &nbsp;We made it to Florida and we are missing you like crazy already! &nbsp;We had a bit of excitement this morning when Bruce realized that he left his license on the scanner at home accidentally. &nbsp;Of all things to leave at home...of all days! &nbsp;Bruce was going to go home and get his license and take a later flight but then we were told that he could still get through security without it. &nbsp;Who knew?!? &nbsp;So he turned around where he was on 295, came back to the airport with a little help from Bumpa and made it just in time, with literally minutes to spare!<br />
<br />
The boys were so, so, so good on the plane. &nbsp;Evan watched Planes - Fire and Rescue and Logan played on the tablet. &nbsp;They couldn't have been any better! &nbsp;It was a long day but we got to your lake house between 5 and 5:30. <br />
<br />
I've had a hard time getting excited about this trip. &nbsp;I was excited and anxious to see Dad but I knew it was going to be a tough trip without you here. &nbsp;I need to get my nails done tomorrow...wanna come? &nbsp;We could go to lunch and then shopping at Beall's after. &nbsp;Oh how I wish we could! &nbsp;I know we will have a nice time here...we always do...but it won't be the same, nothing is the same, without you!!<br />
<br />
We'll take good care of Dad (always, but especially) this week. &nbsp;The photo that you guys had taken for the Indian Creek directory is beautiful! &nbsp;Probably one of the best photos you've ever had taken! &nbsp;You look so beautiful, so healthy, so full of love and life...always a woman to me!<br />
