Today we met Papa, Gramma and Gramma's friend Marsha at Jerry Remy's Sports Bar &amp; Grill behind Fenway park for lunch. &nbsp;Marsha is here visiting for a few days and is a big Red Sox fan so Gramma thought it would be a fun place to go and she was right. &nbsp;The food was good and the restaurant itself is really nice. &nbsp;It wasn't too crowded on a Thursday afternoon and Evan had fun sitting like a big boy in the booth between Mommy and Gramma. &nbsp;After lunch, we took a quick walk over to Fenway. &nbsp;Even though the 2011 season ended on a sour note, we are still faithful fans!<br />
