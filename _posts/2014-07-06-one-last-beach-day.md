We had such a great week at the Cape that we didn't want it to end...so we made sure it didn't...At least for today! &nbsp;We spent one last day at the beach. &nbsp;We started with a stop at Allie's Donuts on the way to meet Nicole at Roger Wheeler state beach aka Sand Hill Cove. &nbsp;We got a bit of a late start and she was there early so we missed her which was just as well because by the time we got there, the lot was closed. &nbsp;We doubled back to Scarborough and had a great afternoon at the beach. &nbsp;Evan had a bit of an incident with the waves and got knocked under and lost his sunglasses but he was fine. &nbsp;After the beach, we picked up chowder and clamcakes for dinner and brought them to Papa &amp; Gramma's. &nbsp;We ate poolside and then enjoyed a bit of a firework show from their family room before heading home. &nbsp;What a great way to end a wonderful vacation!!<br />
