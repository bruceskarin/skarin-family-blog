Anytime we leave our house and go north, we pass a ball field and a playground.  I don't know why but for some reason just the other day it dawned on me that we could probably walk to it.  We looked it up on Google and sure enough, it is just over a mile from our house.  A short little walk to a nice big playground!  Today we decided to go check it out.  It was a little chilly and windy but still nice to get out and enjoy the fresh air.  I don't think Evan knew quite what to make of it and I think Bruce and I may have had more fun.
