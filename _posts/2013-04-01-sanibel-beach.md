Today we took Logan on his first trip to the beach. &nbsp;We drove over to Sanibel, planning to go to the beach for a few hours and then go to the Mucky Duck for lunch. &nbsp;We were having so much fun that we decided to just stay at the beach and eat the few snacks that we brought with us. &nbsp;It was a bit of a rocky start as Logan was hungry, tired and fussy and Evan was overly excited and a bit impatient but once we were settled, it was just about as perfect as a beach day can be with a two year old and an infant. &nbsp;Evan made about 300 trips from the sand in front of our chairs to the water, singing and collecting shells the entire time. I think we may have spoiled Logan a bit...it's going to be tough to out do the beauty of Sanibel!<br />
