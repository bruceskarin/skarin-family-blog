After a fun day in the sun, by the pool, everyone went to the Nauti Turtle for Gramma's birthday celebration. &nbsp;Papa had done quite a bit of research to find a restaurant where there was something for everyone, especially a place that was good for the boys. &nbsp;The Nauti Turtle was perfect! &nbsp;A menu with a lot of variety, a place where the boys could get up and run around a little bit by checking out the boats by the docks or dancing to the live music, and a place willing to let us take our time and have a wonderful celebration of a very special lady! &nbsp;Pat, Kenny and Mary-Anne (Gramma and Papa's friends from Florida) and Marsha (Gramma's friend from elementary school!) joined us in the celebration. &nbsp;After dinner, Danny invited everyone back to our condo to keep the party going...such a little host in the making! &nbsp;We played a family game of Uno and had lots of laughs. Happy birthday, Mom! &nbsp;You had made such an impact on our family in so many ways. &nbsp;We are grateful and thankful to have you as our mom, gramma and friend, everyday! &nbsp;We love you!<br />
