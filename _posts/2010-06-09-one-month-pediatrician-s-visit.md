We went back to the pediatrician today for Evan’s  one month visit. Dr. Gonroff was very pleased in his growth over  the last two weeks and was thrilled with how well behaved and cute Evan was  today. At the end of the visit, she thanked him for being her  “gift” today. Lucky for us…He is our gift everyday!!  Even on the cranky/more difficult days!! Some stats from  the visit – Weight: 11 pounds 8 ounces (81<sup>st</sup> Percentile), Length: 23  inches (85<sup>th</sup> Percentile), Head circumference: 15.35 inches  (61<sup>st</sup> Percentile). Needless to say our little man is  thriving!!<br />
