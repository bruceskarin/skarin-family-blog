We've had a couple of crazy weeks with Bruce resuming his campaign so it was nice to have a relatively quiet, relaxing day, just the four of us today for Mother's day. &nbsp;It started with french toast and bacon (and bloody mary's for me!) made by Evan and Bruce. &nbsp;Not quite as fancy as the omelettes we have had in years past but I could taste a little extra "love" from Evan's helping hands. &nbsp;After breakfast, the boys played outside for a while and then we went to lunch. &nbsp;We wanted somewhere to eat outside and enjoy this beautiful day and we ended up at Vintage Grill on Shrewsbury Street. &nbsp;A great lunch on an absolutely gorgeous day! &nbsp;After lunch, we went to Home Depot to pick up some plants for the yard. <br />
Logan fell asleep in the car on the way home so I brought him in, changed his diaper and put him down to nap around 4 pm. &nbsp;We didn't see him again (aside from me checking on him before I went to bed) until 6:30 the next morning. &nbsp;I guess my little guy was tired!! <br />
Bruce and Evan spent the early part of the evening working in the yard while I relaxed a bit and then worked on some music for boot camp. &nbsp;A very special "nothing special" kind of mother's day!<br />
