We have significantly scaled back our Christmas cookie operation. &nbsp;So much so that Bruce and Evan were making the sugar cookies on Christmas Eve, as if there weren't enough things going on already! (which will hopefully never happen again!) &nbsp;We still found time to make a few different varieties and had a very good helper along the way. &nbsp;I know everyone understands the reason for the scaling back but I also know that everyone looks forward to and enjoys the cookies so it's nice to be able to do a few kinds, especially with such a cute helper!<br />
