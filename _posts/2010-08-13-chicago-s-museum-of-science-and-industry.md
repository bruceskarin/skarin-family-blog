Due to the extreme heat that Chicago is having, Bruce and I decided to opt out of the architectural boat tour of Chicago.  While it sounded like an exciting and informative way to see the city, we weren’t sure if we would be able to escape the heat and keep Evan cool enough.  Instead, the three of us drove into the city, had lunch and went to the Museum of Science and Industry.  Evan slept in the stroller for about half of the museum tour and was then wide awake to take in all the sights and sounds.  It was a nice museum and surely a place that we will visit again when Evan is a little older.<br />
