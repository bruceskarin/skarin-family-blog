Since Logan was born, we have been asked over and over "How is Evan doing? &nbsp;Is he a great big brother?" I have to say Evan is a wonderful big brother! &nbsp;He is very helpful and willing to get things for me when I need them...a bib, a burp cloth, a bottle, a binkie...and he often gets things without being asked. &nbsp;Evan is also very loving and sweet with Logan. &nbsp;He gives him lots of kisses, often times on his own, talks to him, snuggles with him and wants to hold and feed Logan all the time. &nbsp;This is not to say that we haven't had difficult moments as well but I think (or at least I like to believe) that these are typical two-year old moments more so than they are attributed to having a new baby in the house. <br />
Logan also loves his big brother. &nbsp;He responds to his voice and laughter and watches Evan play and dance around. &nbsp;Now that Logan is smiling more, he often smiles at Evan. &nbsp;It is so wonderful to watch this bond between brothers begin to develop...just as I'd hoped it would. &nbsp;I know they will have their moments when they fight but overall I believe they will be best friends.<br />
