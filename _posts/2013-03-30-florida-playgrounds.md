Two other things that have become somewhat of a tradition on our trips to Fort Myers are a trip to Lakes Park and "salmon on Sunday" with Uncle Jon and Allie. <br />
Our trip to Lakes Park was Wednesday when the weather was still a bit too cool for the pool and beach. &nbsp;It is funny though because I thought we would be hot, hot, hot due to the fact that our trip was so late this year. &nbsp;It started out quite the opposite which is ok seeing as it allowed us to do a few other things on the cooler days. &nbsp;We had some fun at the swings and playground, took a break for some lunch, caught the last train ride of the day, had some ice cream and took a walk through the botanical garden. &nbsp;Evan was pretty excited about the bikes and paddle boats too. &nbsp;I see a rental coming in our future!<br />

Our tradition with Uncle Jon and Allie was a bit different this year. &nbsp;We are generally in Florida for two weekends but this year we only had one, which was also Easter. &nbsp;For this reason, we got together with Uncle Jon and Allie on Saturday for lunch at their house and then headed to Estero Park. &nbsp;They have such a lovely home and I always love visiting. &nbsp;I love how open and airy it is with beautiful plants and trees everywhere you look! &nbsp;Allie had made some special lunch "creations" for Evan and Evan enjoyed playing with Fergie. &nbsp;After lunch we went to Estero Park where Uncle Jon and Allie could really see Evan in action! &nbsp;As always, it was a (too short but) nice visit!<br />
