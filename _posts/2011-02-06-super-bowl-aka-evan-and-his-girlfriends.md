To celebrate the Super Bowl, we went to Tim and Chris's annual Super Bowl party.  This year it was a little different than past years as there were almost an equal number of guys and girls there.  This is very unusual for most WPI parties.  Evan enjoyed the party and especially being passed from one girlfriend to the next.  I think he had 7 girlfriends there...Evan was in heaven!  I didn't get pictures of everyone holding him but it was quite cute how everyone wanted a turn.  And really, why wouldn't you??
