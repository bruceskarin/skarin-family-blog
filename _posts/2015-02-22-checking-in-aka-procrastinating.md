Hi Mom. &nbsp;It's me. &nbsp;Just checking in. &nbsp;We haven't "spoken" for a few days so I wanted to "call" to see how you've been and let you know what we've been up to. &nbsp;I know you would have called on Friday to see how Logan made out at the doctor. &nbsp;No bronchitis, no pneumonia. &nbsp;Just a lingering cough that won't go away. &nbsp;I'm hoping that the Florida fresh air and sunshine will take care of that next week.<br />
<br />
Not too much else going on here. &nbsp;We spent Friday with Em, Annalia and Jonah. &nbsp;We had a fun day with lots of playtime for the kids and a few crafts. &nbsp;Em and I had a bit of a chance to catch up but never as much as we'd like. &nbsp;Such is life!<br />
<br />
Yesterday, I finally gave the boys their Valentine's gifts. &nbsp;We were in RI last Saturday and I kept forgetting all week. &nbsp;They didn't seem to mind the fact that they were a week late. &nbsp;Logan loved his Baymax/Big Hero Six shirt and Evan was super excited about his new Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtle sunglasses. &nbsp;He was wearing them much of the day, even inside!<br />
<br />
We got a few more inches of snow yesterday, 4 total. &nbsp;I'm ready for spring! &nbsp;We are looking forward to heading to Fort Myers next week to see Dad. &nbsp;The boys both got haircuts...FINALLY...so they'll be handsome and ready to go. &nbsp;Logan's was so long!! &nbsp;Wish you were going to be there too. &nbsp;You will be in our hearts and thoughts, as you always are!<br />
<br />
Right now the boys are at Quaker meeting with Bruce and I am supposed to be working on my assignment for my thesis class but I'm having a hard time getting started. &nbsp;I am hoping that once I get into it, it won't be too bad but it's that getting started that's the trouble right now. &nbsp;I just have to remember that I will be so glad once it is done and how quickly the quarters at WPI went by. &nbsp;It's only about 11 weeks with spring break in there too. &nbsp;I know I can do it...just got to get started!!<br />
<br />
Nothing much else to report. &nbsp;I miss you and love you so much!<br />
