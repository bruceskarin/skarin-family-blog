A few weeks ago a former co-worker of mine posted a picture of her daughter with Clifford the Big Red Dog. Clifford had come to the Barnes &amp; Noble in Millbury for story time. &nbsp;We didn't know about this event but we checked out the website for future events and found out that Curious George would be there on March 3rd. &nbsp;Curious George! &nbsp;THE Curious George who is Evan's best buddy! &nbsp;Well we couldn't miss it!<br />
We actually had plans to take Evan to RI in the late morning for his first sleepover with Danny and Sean while Bruce and I attended Laurel and Kyle's wedding in CT. &nbsp;Had it been any other character, except for maybe Pooh Bear, we would have skipped it and stuck to our original plan but we couldn't skip George! &nbsp;Thankfully Evan and I had met Amelia and Connor for story time the day before and saw how crowded it was. &nbsp;We knew it would be more so on a weekend, especially with a character visiting so we made sure to get there early.<br />
They read two stories,&nbsp;<i>Curious George Makes Pancakes</i> and <i>Curious George at the Aquarium,</i>&nbsp;and then George came out for photos. &nbsp;We waited a little while for the crowd to die down and then Evan got his turn to meet George.<br />
I asked Evan if he wanted to go with mommy to meet George or on his own and he said "With Mommy." &nbsp;So we both went up and said hello to George while Bruce took pictures. &nbsp;Evan said hi to George, gave him a kiss and then said "Thank you, George" as our turn was ending. &nbsp;After all the kids had had a turn, the woman from the library said that kids could come up again for one more photo before George had to leave. &nbsp;This time, our big boy decided he wanted to go up on his own. &nbsp;Evan marched right up on the little stage and stood next to George, all by himself, then gave him a high-five. &nbsp;We were so proud of our little guy!<br />
