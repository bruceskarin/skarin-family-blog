Evan has always loved to ride on the tractor with Daddy while he mows the lawn and until recently he hasn't had any real competition. &nbsp;In the spring, Logan was content to sit with me and watch Daddy and Evan ride the tractor. &nbsp;Then sometime mid-summer, Logan became more and more interested in the tractor...pointing, talking and squawking at it. &nbsp;Today he got his first ride. &nbsp;He was a little nervous at first and was holding on for dear life but by the end he really liked it. &nbsp;Next year I think we might need a "ride with daddy mowing schedule"!<br />
