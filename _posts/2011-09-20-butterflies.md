Thanks to Groupon we enjoyed a fun, rainy day and picnic lunch at The Butterfly Place with Abby and Connor. &nbsp;I didn't realize know what to expect but there were tons of butterflies, flying all around us. &nbsp;A couple were close to landing on Abby and one landed on my head as we were leaving. &nbsp;We had planned a picnic lunch which we didn't think would be possible because of the rain but they had a big tent out back so it was perfect. &nbsp;A good way to spend an otherwise dreary day!
