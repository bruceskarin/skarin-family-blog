Today we took a trip to West Hill Dam with Isabelle and TJ. &nbsp;We stopped to see their new home in Whitinsville first and then spent a beautiful day at the dam. &nbsp;We had never been before but it was perfect. &nbsp;Quiet, clean, peaceful and small enough to keep an eye on the kids from one place. &nbsp;The kids had so much fun swimming and playing in the sand and actually gave Jenna and I a bit of a chance to catch up and chat...amazing! &nbsp;And TJ was my little pal, such a little love bug. &nbsp;It was a great place, close-by to spend a hot day without traveling all the way to the beach. &nbsp;Thanks for the fun, Belle &amp; TJ!<br />
