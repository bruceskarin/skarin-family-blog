What's better than apple picking on a beautiful late summer, fallish day? &nbsp;Apple picking and a corn maze! &nbsp;We had a fun family day at Schartner Farm in Bolton today. &nbsp;We wandered through the corn maze for a while, got a little lost (isn't that the point?) and made our way out. &nbsp;Then we took a hayride out to the apple orchard to pick some apples. &nbsp;The trees were loaded with lots of good, juicy (we had to taste a few!) apples. &nbsp;There were even quite a few low enough for Evan to reach. &nbsp;We stopped to grab a bite to eat on the way home and were home in time for mommy and daddy to get ready for date night. <br />
