Now that the weather has been getting nicer we have been trying to take advantage of it and spend <a href="http://youtu.be/kI1VpZrEYB0">time outside</a> whenever we can.  It has been a little difficult trying to find a good place for Evan to play.  Our top deck (as you can see from the pictures) needs to be painted, the bottom deck is sagging from the snow load this winter and gave Evan splinters the other day, and the lawn has been really wet.  But even though we have been a little limited, we are still getting out and having fun!  Evan doesn't really like the feel of the grass too much and it turns out that Evan likes to crawl in the rock garden the best...Ouch!
