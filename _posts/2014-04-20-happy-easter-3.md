Easter traditions have always been a bit varied from year to year in my memories. &nbsp;The Easter bunny always came but some years we were on vacation, some years we would go to my Great Aunt Bet and Uncle Joe's for a big family egg hunt and dinner, and some years our day was a bit more low key. <br />
We, unintentionally, have started a similar non-traditional tradition. &nbsp;The Easter bunny comes and brings small gifts and a bit of candy. &nbsp;Nothing crazy. &nbsp;This year the Easter bunny "hid" some candy filled eggs for the boys to find in addition to the gifts and candy that he left in their Easter buckets. &nbsp;It was a fun way to start the day!<br />
After our egg hunt and breakfast, we went to RI to spend the afternoon with the Dowdings. &nbsp;It was great to see Uncle Glenn and Aunt Chris's new house. &nbsp;It's an adorable house in a really cute neighborhood, great for running! &nbsp;We had a delicious meal and then enjoyed watching the 6 little ones play together. <br />
