I have always thought of March 1st as the unofficial start to Spring even though the equinox is not until the 20th. &nbsp;With the mild winter we have had, I thought this would be more true this year than ever before. &nbsp;How wrong I was! &nbsp;We had one of the biggest snow storms of the season today and have about 6-7 inches of fresh snow on the ground. &nbsp;Hopefully it will melt quickly! &nbsp;In the meantime, Evan will have fun playing in it! &nbsp;Hard to believe that today was our first (and hopefully our last) weekday snow day of the season!<br />
