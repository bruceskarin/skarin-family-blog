Monday morning we were up, dressed, fed and out of the house just after 11 am. &nbsp;I know this doesn't seem like much of an accomplishment but seeing as Logan isn't even three weeks old yet and therefore not sleeping through the night, I felt like this was pretty good. &nbsp;The plan was to go to the playground and then grocery shopping. &nbsp;However, when we arrived at the playground both boys were sound asleep! &nbsp;I tried a few times to wake up Evan but he was out cold! &nbsp;So I turned around and went back home. &nbsp;After carrying Evan upstairs to continue his nap, he woke up wondering when we were going to go to the playground and the store. &nbsp;Needless to say he was not happy when I told him we went and he fell asleep in the car. &nbsp;Thankfully though he did fall back asleep for a while.<br />
Today I decided to try it again. &nbsp;We were once again up, dressed, fed and out of the house right around 11. &nbsp;The outcome was much different this time though. &nbsp;When we arrived, Logan was fast asleep which actually made things much easier and Evan was ready to play. &nbsp;We had the playground to ourselves and played for about an hour. &nbsp;Take two was a success...including the grocery shopping...but mommy was beat when we got back home!<br />
