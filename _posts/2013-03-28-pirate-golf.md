Today after going to see Papa &amp; Gramma's new place at Indian Creek (more on that in another post), we took the boys to play mini golf - pirate mini golf! &nbsp;Unfortunately, the course wasn't stroller friendly so we couldn't bring Logan. &nbsp;Thankfully, Papa agreed to watch Logan so that Daddy and I could golf with Evan. &nbsp;Good thing too because this is what Evan has been talking about for weeks. &nbsp;Swimming in Papa's Florida pool (which we have yet to do thanks to unseasonably cool weather) and playing pirate golf. <br />
So after I brought Logan back to the condo to stay with Papa, we started out on our pirate golf adventure. &nbsp;First stop, checking out the baby crocodiles and waiting our turn for the first hole in the pirate ship. &nbsp;The course was pretty crowded but Evan was a trooper. &nbsp;We played all 18 holes and enjoyed the beautiful day. &nbsp;There was a large group which should have been two separate groups ahead of us who allowed us to play through on the back nine. &nbsp;I'm not sure any of us would have made it otherwise!<br />
