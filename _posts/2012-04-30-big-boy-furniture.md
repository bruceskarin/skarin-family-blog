From the time that we found out we were expecting another little one later this year, Evan has been pretty excited about being a big brother and getting a new big boy room. &nbsp;He has helped with every stage of the process of turning the office and spare room into his big boy room. &nbsp;From choosing new carpeting to shopping for furniture to prepping and painting the room, Evan has been excited and helpful along the way. &nbsp;Today his new big boy furniture arrived! &nbsp;He watched as the guys from Cardi's unloaded the truck and then once they left, he raced upstairs to check out his new furniture. &nbsp;I can't wait to see the look on his face once the room is completely ready!<br />
