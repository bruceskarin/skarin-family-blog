Spring is on it's way...At least according to the calendar! &nbsp;And today it actually felt like it! &nbsp;We spent much of the day outside in Boston with many others who are ready for spring to hurry up and get here. &nbsp;We started at Thornton's for breakfast where we met Daddy's cousin Matt who was up from Philly for the weekend. &nbsp;We hadn't seen him in 12 years and it was good to catch up for at least a little bit while he was visiting. &nbsp;After breakfast, the boys ran around the Christian Science Plaza while we figured out what else we would do for the day. &nbsp;We decided to keep it low key as it was already getting a bit late in the day. &nbsp;We found a playground at Titus Sparrow Park where the boys had fun for quite a while. &nbsp;We then headed to Harvard Square to visit the <a href="http://thecuriousgeorgestore.com/">World's Only Curious George Store</a>, which is really just a book store with a few unique George things, and then had lunch before heading home. &nbsp;We spent a beautiful day in the city...something we certainly need to do more often!<br />
