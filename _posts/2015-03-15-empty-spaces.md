Hi Mom. &nbsp;It's me. &nbsp;I'm just checking in to say "Hi!" &nbsp;I'm having a bit of a rough day today...missing you terribly. &nbsp;I took down your cards today and now there is just an empty space where they once were. &nbsp;Kind of like the empty feeling that I have without you here. &nbsp;I know you are still with me in my heart and in my memories but it's just not the same. <br />
<br />
I find that the days I expect to be hard, like yesterday, aren't but the days that I don't expect to be hard, are. &nbsp;It's such a roller coaster. &nbsp;There are so many things that I want to tell you but then I try and I can't find the words or remember what it was. <br />
<br />
Today while I was having lunch with the boys, I just started to cry. &nbsp;We were having grilled cheese sandwiches on white/italian bread and it reminded me of you. &nbsp;Simple things like memories of grilled cheese sandwiches bring a smile to my face and a tear to my cheek. &nbsp;Logan came right over and gave me a big, long hug. &nbsp;He has been really sweet and sensitive when he knows I am sad. &nbsp;Evan asked why I was crying and I told him that I am missing you and that I wish I could pick up the phone and call you. &nbsp;He said "You know what I wish, Mom? &nbsp;I wish that Papa and Gramma were together." &nbsp;Me too, buddy, me too!<br />
<br />
I wish you were in Florida with Dad, enjoying a nice visit with Uncle Rick and Aunt Donna. &nbsp;I know they are all missing you and I am glad that they are there together. &nbsp;I wish you were there so that I didn't have to worry so much. &nbsp;I talked to Dad every day for 70 days and on the 70th day, when I didn't think I was going to talk to him, I was really upset. &nbsp;I ended up talking to him that night and it made it easier the next day when I didn't talk to him (at least on the phone, we chatted on Facebook). &nbsp;I know he is doing ok but I still worry about him. &nbsp;I remembered you busting my chops about not coming to visit you when you were in the hospital for those 3 days just after Christmas. &nbsp;I remembered you saying that Dan came to visit "because he cares". &nbsp;I know you were only joking and that both you and Dad know how much I care and love you.<br />
<br />
I wish you could talk to Logan and he could tell you about his new sneakers. &nbsp;They aren't anything super special, no lights, no super heroes, but he is excited about them and they are fast! &nbsp;I wish you could hear him talk and be amazed like I am at how much he has changed since Christmas. &nbsp;It is unbelievable. &nbsp;He sits at the dining room table with us now, not in his high chair but just in a regular chair with a booster. &nbsp;He's getting so big.<br />
<br />
I wish you could talk to Evan and listen to his amazing stories where he uses his imagination to think of so many interesting adventures. &nbsp;I wish you could see how excited he is about his new Big Papi shirt. &nbsp;I wish you could see some of the drawings Evan does and how well he is writing his letters. &nbsp;I wish you could see him try to sound out words and begin to read. &nbsp;I wish you could see how well Evan and Logan have been getting along and how nicely they have been playing together. &nbsp;They are just so special.<br />
<br />
Most of all I wish that I could hear your voice and that you could listen to all that I want to tell you. &nbsp;All the things about finishing my Master's and the mix up with my registration for one of my courses. &nbsp;I wish I could tell you about my data analysis and what I learned in excel as I was doing it. &nbsp;I wish I could tell you about going back to boot camp. &nbsp;I was supposed to go last Monday but I just couldn't. &nbsp;I had told myself for weeks that after Florida would be the time but putting one foot in front of the other was so hard last week. &nbsp;But I am going back tomorrow. &nbsp;I have to do it for me...for you. &nbsp;I wish I could tell you all about Bruce's job search and opportunities and how things are with us. <br />
<br />
I wish I could tell you how sorry I am that I wasn't more supportive when Gramma died. &nbsp;I know she had been sick and that her life was quite restricted and that it probably was for the best. &nbsp;But now I understand better what it feels like to lose your Mom and that it isn't easy at any age. &nbsp;I know our relationship is different than yours was with her but I still don't feel like I was supportive enough. &nbsp;For that I am sorry. <br />
<br />
And then all of the little things that don't really matter too much in the grand scheme of things but the things that I would tell you...about my races, getting together with friends...just the little things. <br />
<br />
But I guess that is what I am doing by writing though, right? &nbsp;I am talking to you in the only way I know how right now. &nbsp;I wish I could know for sure that you are ok. &nbsp;That you can breathe. &nbsp;That you can see us and watch out for us. &nbsp;I wish I could know for sure that I will see you again someday and that I will hear your voice and see your smile. &nbsp;I wish...I wish...I wish...you were still here. &nbsp;I love you!
