Today Evan went on his longest car  ride yet and visited his third state. Boy, this kid really gets  around considering he isn’t even one month old yet. We went to  New  Hampshire to  help Jess and Jeff celebrate at their BBQ baby bash for baby boy Costa who will  be arriving at the end of August or beginning of September.  As  always, Evan was great in the car and slept most of the way to  Kensington. Once we arrived, after Bruce and I ate, Evan woke up  hungry. Good timing though! After eating, he was  alert for a while, making some of his many faces at us. I  couldn’t get over how many other kids and babies were there. I  guess we are at that age though. It was a nice day and fun to help  Jess and Jeff get ready to join the world of parenthood. We hope  they experience the same joy that we have over the last month. It  will be fun to have another little boy so close to Evan’s age.  Good luck Costa’s!!<br />
