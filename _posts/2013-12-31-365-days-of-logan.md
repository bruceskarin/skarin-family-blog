(Note: The most recent pictures will be at the end. &nbsp;You may want to flip through using the left arrow instead of the right. I have tried to only use pictures that haven't appeared in another post. &nbsp;Those with captions have been used twice...some are just too cute to pass up!)<br />
<br />
Towards the end of 2012, I started to feel like I wasn't taking many pictures of Logan. &nbsp;So this year, I am attempting to take a picture of Logan EVERY.SINGLE.DAY. &nbsp;A friend of mine who has her own amazing blog inspired this idea when she did the same thing with her daughter a couple of years ago. &nbsp;I think it will be amazing to see how much he changes this year...starting 2013 just shy of 5 months old and ending just shy of 17 months. &nbsp;My plan is to choose the photo of the day at the end of each month and to limit it to one photo per day (hopefully one that has not appeared in another post), no captions (this will be tough!). &nbsp;Some days there are just so many cute ones I know it will be hard to choose but I will. &nbsp;I can't wait to see how it turns out! &nbsp;Thanks for the inspiration, Kodi!<br />
<br />
(edit 3/31) When I started the 365 days of Logan at the beginning of the year, I felt that captioning (is that even a word?) every photo for the year was a bit <strike>OCD</strike> out of control so I decided not to. &nbsp;But then for many photos I found that there was a cute moment or story being lost because I wasn't captioning each photo. &nbsp;I'm not saying that every photo will have a caption but I am going to start adding some in hopes of capturing those little moments without creating a new post entirely. &nbsp;(If I ever get caught up and have nothing else to do...not likely with two little monkeys keeping me busy...I will go back and caption January and February.) I will indicate if the photos have been used twice. <br />
<br />
(edit 4/30) There are two things I can take away from April's photos. &nbsp;1. &nbsp;Logan must be part Italian. &nbsp;He is always "talking" with his hands. &nbsp;2. Logan is quite possibly the happiest baby...ever!<br />
<br />
(edit May/June) So many photos without captions because what more can you say that his beautiful smile doesn't already say?<br />
<br />
(edit July) Less sitting, more moving...still always smiling!!<br />
<br />
(edit August) Nothing much to say other than we are still having a great summer and don't want to see it end! Lots of photos that have already appeared in other posts even though I started the year trying to avoid that. &nbsp;There are too many photos and not enough time to pick unique ones all the time. <br />
<br />
(edit September &amp; October) The theme of these two months is busy, busy, busy! &nbsp;Logan is always on the move, crawling in September and walking in October. &nbsp;He wants to do anything his big brother can do and is quite often successful. &nbsp;He is living up to his nickname "Trouble Bubble" these days. &nbsp;Thanks to Auntie Karin and Uncle Dan for filling in for us while we were on our anniversary trip. <br />
<br />
(edit November &amp; December) Logan was sick for a bit in November but still mostly smiley. &nbsp;Such a trooper! &nbsp;We enjoyed every minute of the holidays, including a visit from Omi and Grandpa and some extra time with cousins!
