For at least five years, I have been wanting to run the Blessing of the Fleet in Narragansett. &nbsp;I intended to run it a couple of times in the past but due to injury or pregnancy I was never able to. &nbsp;Until tonight! &nbsp;The weather was a little shaky to start but really the rain felt good. &nbsp;75+, rainy and super humid was still better than the 95+ we have had the last few weeks. &nbsp;It was an amazing run with so many athletes and spectators...a fun route for the entire 10 miles. &nbsp;The carnival and festivities at the end were great too! &nbsp;I saw so many people I knew too (only in RI, right?). &nbsp;This is definitely a race I will do again! &nbsp;Thanks to my boys for coming to cheer me on...it is for them that I run these races! &nbsp;Love you all!<br />
