Today was Evan's holiday program at Here We Grow Preschool. &nbsp;Picture this...twenty-eight three year olds singing and performing seven holiday songs and poems. &nbsp;Can you say adorable?? &nbsp;They were all so cute in their holiday outfits. &nbsp;Evan's class wasn't quite as into it as the other class but they still did a great job. &nbsp;Most of the girls were paying attention and following along. &nbsp;The boys...well...not as much! &nbsp;It was a sweet little performance and I am so glad they do this for the parents. &nbsp;He loved it! &nbsp;Enjoy the show!<br />
