It seemed to take a long time for Logan's umbilical cord to fall off. &nbsp;When I went back to check to see when <a href="http://evan.skarin.us/2010/05/evans-first-bath.html">Evan's first bath</a> was I found out both boys had their first bath at 3 weeks and 1 day old...funny! &nbsp;I wonder how many other firsts will match up like this. &nbsp;And so it begins...comparing the boys. &nbsp;Don't worry though, we will be sure to let each develop his own personality. <br />
I learned two things today. &nbsp;1. Logan is a firehose! &nbsp;He peed on the bathroom floor not once, not twice but three times! And 2. Evan is huge! &nbsp;I put him on the changing table to get him dressed right after Logan and the difference in their size was amazing. &nbsp;Hard to believe that Evan was as small as Logan just 27 months ago! <br />
