Thanks to a Groupon deal and me feeling the need to occasionally organize group outings, 19 of us attended the Pawtucket Red Sox game tonight.  We were undecided during the day as to whether or not we should go because Evan wasn't feeling well.  We thought due to his new teeth coming in so we decided to go anyway.  We had a pre-game BBQ and then stayed for the game.  It was Evan's first live baseball game and also the first time Bruce had been to the Paw Sox.  We tried to take a pre-game photo with Paws but Evan was a little afraid.  I should have taken my cue from Danny, who, at four years old, didn't want to go near Paws either.  It was a cool but nice evening for the game.  We ended up leaving before the post-game fireworks so I think we will have to go another time when we can stay for them.
