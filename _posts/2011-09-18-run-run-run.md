Today was a beautiful day for a fall 5K...the CVS Downtown 5K. &nbsp;Bruce was planning to once again run with Evan in the stroller but Uncle Dan volunteered to push him while Auntie Karin pushed Danny and Sean so that both Bruce and I could run. &nbsp;It was a great course with local bands playing all along the route. &nbsp;Bruce ran a great time, especially seeing as he hadn't done much training, and I ran my second fastest. &nbsp;We followed up a fun morning with an afternoon of football at Uncle Dan's and Auntie Karin's and lots of fun cousin time!
