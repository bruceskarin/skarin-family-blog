This winter has been tough...just ask any New Englander and they'll tell you. &nbsp;(They'll probably tell you if you don't ask too!) &nbsp;It has been snowy and cold! &nbsp;So cold that the snow never melts, it just piles up and up and up. &nbsp;If you are 18 months old (or any age in my opinion) that isn't a good thing. &nbsp;You want to go out and play in the snow but the minute you get out there and realize how deep it is and that you can't walk in it thanks to the depth and your abominable snowman snowsuit, you are miserable. &nbsp;Welcome to Logan's life! &nbsp;Poor little guy! &nbsp;He wants to go outside and play with his big brother more than anything but he is so frustrated that he can't do anything that he spends most of his time in the driveway. &nbsp;I'm sure he's thinking "How come Evan and do it but I can't?" &nbsp;Next year, kiddo, next year! &nbsp;We have still managed to have some fun anyway. <br />
