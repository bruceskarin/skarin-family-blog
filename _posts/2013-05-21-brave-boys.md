I took the boys to the pediatrician today. &nbsp;Evan for his 3-year check-up and Logan for his 9-month. &nbsp;Both boys passed with flying colors and as always impressed Dr. Gonroff. &nbsp;She was asking Evan all sorts of questions about shapes, letters and numbers. &nbsp;He answered each one perfectly. &nbsp;She also asked him to run, hop, jump, sing and touch his toes. &nbsp;He was again a super star! &nbsp;Dr. Gonroff was also impressed with Logan and how he is growing, developing and learning. &nbsp;Their check-ups were great! &nbsp;Both boys needed to have a finger prick for a lead test and Evan needed a Hep A vaccine. &nbsp;The nurse came in and did what she needed to do and neither Evan nor Logan made a peep! &nbsp;They were both so brave! &nbsp;It was a "proud momma" moment for sure!<br />
<br />
Evan's 3 year stats: Height - 38.25 inches (90th percentile), Weight - 31 lbs (63rd percentile)<br />
Logan's 9 month stats: Height - 29.5 inches (81st percentile), Weight - 22 lbs 10 oz (76th percentile), Head Circumference - 48 cm (97th percentile)<br />
<br />
Evan was exactly the same height at 9 months, weighed 4 oz more and had a HC of 47.5 cm. &nbsp;The boys are still matching up almost identically. &nbsp;Can't wait to see what happens when Logan starts moving!!<br />
