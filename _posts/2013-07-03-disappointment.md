The last couple of days were tough ones around here. &nbsp;For whatever reason Evan was in a mood...and not a very nice one! &nbsp;He was being fresh to mom and dad, mean to Logan and just not the sweet boy that he generally is. &nbsp;I don't know what the cause was but I hope tomorrow is better!<br />
Tonight we were supposed to go to the carnival and fireworks with Danny, Sean, Uncle Dan and Auntie Karin. &nbsp;This is something Evan has been talking about doing ever since we went last year. &nbsp;With his challenging behavior over the last few days, we kept warning him that we weren't going to go if he kept it up. &nbsp;This usually works but not today! &nbsp;So after a difficult morning and early afternoon, I called Auntie Karin to tell her that we weren't going to go. &nbsp;I hated to do it but sometimes the tough decisions need to be made. &nbsp;I think in the end Bruce and I were more disappointed than Evan...we understand exactly what we were missing. <br />
Regardless of the difficult times, when all is said and done at the end of the day, Evan is still our little angel! &nbsp;Let's just hope his behavior is angelic tomorrow!<br />
