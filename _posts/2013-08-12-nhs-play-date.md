It has been quite some time since we last got together with some of my former co-workers for a play date. &nbsp;We saw Jenna and Belle in February but they have had a new addition, TJ, since then. &nbsp;We hadn't seen Mindy and Jayten in over a year and they too have had a new addition since then, Camren. &nbsp;Funny how we each had our first babies in 2010 and now the second children have all been born around the same time too. &nbsp;We had a great day at the Stanley's house playing with all of Isabelle's toys, swimming, and swinging...what more could 6 children 3 and younger want!! &nbsp;Great to see some old NHS pals and catch up!<br />
