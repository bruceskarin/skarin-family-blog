We had a fun, action pack Halloween weekend. &nbsp;It started out Friday when I took Evan to the Millbury Public Library for story time and <a href="http://www.thedailymillbury.com/gallery/halloween-parade?pic=12">parading</a> to trick-or-treating at Town Hall. &nbsp;All the kids were so excited and cute in their costumes. &nbsp;It was a nice day so we decided to take a ride to see Daddy at work. &nbsp;I knew Bruce didn't have any lunch meetings because of the Halloween potluck. &nbsp;What I didn't know was that everyone eats in a big conference room together! &nbsp;Everyone was thrilled to see Evan and it was fitting because Aptima's "mascot" is a sock monkey so Evan fit right in. <br />
Saturday was low key which was good because on Sunday, we took Evan practice trick-or-treating at some of my relatives houses. &nbsp;We were able to make five stops: Uncle Glenn &amp; Aunt Chris (who we almost missed while they were out), Bumpa, Aunt Donna &amp; Uncle Rick, Gigi and Gramma &amp; Papa. &nbsp;It was a busy day but nice to be able to visit a little bit with out too many other people around. <br />
Today was Halloween. &nbsp;We took Evan trick-or-treating to four of our neighbors houses. &nbsp;He was really quiet at first but by the end he had figured out what to do and say. &nbsp;And of course, once we were back home Evan said "Trick-treat" and "Thank you" all night long! &nbsp;After dinner, we video conferenced with Grandpa Bob, Omi, Auntie Kristina and Hannah. &nbsp;It was fun to be able to see Hannah in her dinosaur costume and to show off our little monkey!<br />
