With all the snow that has fallen this year, it is hard to believe that this is our first snowman. &nbsp;But then again it has been so cold that all of the snow has been too fluffy...Until today. &nbsp;Today was perfect "play in the snow" weather...high 30s/low 40s, a little sunny and not windy. &nbsp;The boys enjoyed playing for a while and Logan had fun building his first snowman. &nbsp;Hard to believe that he was too little last year...time is flying!!<br />
