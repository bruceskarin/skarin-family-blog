This afternoon, I decided to change that. &nbsp;(Truth be told, the boys had already watched a bit of TV this morning while I took care of a few other things.) <br />
We had lunch, bundled up and headed outside to play in the snow. &nbsp;We were out for a bit over an hour, which is a long time these days. &nbsp;Once we came back in and after having a snack (these boys do nothing but eat these days!), the boys worked in their workbooks for a while. &nbsp;Evan was working on identifying beginning sounds and identifying capital and lower case letter pairs. &nbsp;Logan was searching for all of the similar objects and then counting them. <br />
Then it was 4:30 pm...Logan wasn't going to take a nap, we had already watched a decent amount of TV this morning...what were we going to do until dinner??? &nbsp;That is when I decided it was time to execute! &nbsp;<a href="http://www.funathomewithkids.com/2013/08/rainbow-soap-foam-bubbles-sensory-play.html">This</a>...we were going to do this! &nbsp;Our rainbow foam didn't look quite as fluffy and cool but the boys still had fun. &nbsp;Who knows maybe I'll have enough motivation to do another similar activity in the coming days.<br />
