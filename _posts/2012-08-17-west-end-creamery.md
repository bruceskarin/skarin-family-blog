After 5 days at the hospital and 6 more being at home, I needed to get out of the house! &nbsp;I think knowing that Bruce was only going to be home for two more days and then I would be on my own with the boys and still not cleared to drive only made this desire worse. &nbsp;For the last week and a half, Evan has been so patient and relatively understanding that Mommy needs to rest and that caring for Logan takes a lot of Mommy and Daddy's time. &nbsp;While Daddy and Evan have had lots of fun time playing in the yard, I thought it was important for all of us to get out for a while and have some fun. &nbsp;We decided to go to West End Creamery for some ice cream, mini golf and burn some energy at the Barnyard Jump. &nbsp;Boy was this a hit with Evan...and Daddy too!! &nbsp;After an ice cream lunch, we took a quick walk through the barn to look at the animals. &nbsp;Then, Evan and Daddy jumped on the Barnyard Jump pillow for about 30 minutes. Just long enough to take 116...yes, 116 photos! &nbsp;We decided we should play mini-golf before Logan woke up ready to eat and before Evan crashed. &nbsp;We started on the course and it was not pretty! &nbsp;Bruce and I are generally pretty good mini-golfers but running interference with a two-year old, making sure that Logan wasn't in the sun and me being just 10 days post-surgery had both of us off our game. &nbsp;We decided to throw away the score card and just have fun! &nbsp;After about 6 holes, Logan needed to eat. &nbsp;This worked out perfectly as we found a nice shady spot in the grass under a tree to give us all a break from the hot sun. &nbsp;Evan had a snack, Logan had a bottle and tired Mommy was able to sit and rest for a few minutes. &nbsp;When Logan finished eating, we play another 3 holes of mini-golf before returning to the Barnyard Jump for a few more jumps...almost another 30 minutes...before heading home...tired! &nbsp;Today was the first of many adventures we will have with our two boys!<br />
