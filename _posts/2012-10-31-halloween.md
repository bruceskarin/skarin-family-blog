Halloween this year was a four day long celebration. &nbsp;It started on Sunday at Gramma &amp; Papa's. &nbsp;We all got together to watch the Patriots game and to talk about the anniversary party from Saturday night. &nbsp;While we were there, Gramma gave Halloween bags to the three older boys (Logan is still a bit too little for candy). &nbsp;They had lots of fun digging through their bags and finding all of their goodies. &nbsp;They especially loved the fangs! <br />
Then on Tuesday, I brought the boys to RI for some family trick-or-treating. &nbsp;We stopped at Uncle Glenn and Aunt Chris's first but no one was home so we headed to Bumpa's house. &nbsp;We visited with him for a while while Logan had a bottle and Evan entertained us all. &nbsp;Our next stop was Uncle Rick's and Aunt Donna's. &nbsp;We stayed there for a while before everyone started getting tired and heading to Gramma &amp; Papa's so that the fire chief could take a nap. &nbsp;We stayed for dinner at Governor's and everyone got a kick out of seeing the fire chief and his little monster.<br />
Today, we did the same thing we did <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2011_10_01_archive.html">last year</a>...story time at the library, trick-or-treating at town hall, and then lunch with Daddy at Aptima. &nbsp;After a nap and dinner we had planned to take Evan (and Logan) to a few of the neighbors houses. &nbsp;However, our plans changed when we had a fresh two-year old on our hands this evening. &nbsp;We gave Evan multiple chances to change his behavior but he just didn't seem to care that he wasn't going to be able to go trick-or-treating. &nbsp;I was certainly more upset about it than he was. &nbsp;It was a busy couple of days and maybe Evan had decided that he already had enough candy. &nbsp;Either way we had a fun Halloween with our little fire chief and our monster/pumpkin!<br />
