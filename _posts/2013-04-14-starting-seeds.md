Last summer the plants in our garden yielded a really good crop, especially the tomatoes, which we were picking by the quart. &nbsp;We didn't give it a ton of attention because we were a little busy, especially at the end of the summer, but it still did quite well. &nbsp;We saved seeds from a few different veggies - 3 varieties of tomatoes, broccoli and green beans. &nbsp;Tonight Daddy and Evan started some seeds that will hopefully grow into our starter seedlings to be transferred into the garden in a few weeks.</div>
