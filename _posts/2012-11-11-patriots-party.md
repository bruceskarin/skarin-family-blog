We love having parties and hosting get togethers. &nbsp;We used to get stressed about it and argue a little while trying to get ready but now we seem to have a good system worked out and we can put together a party for 30 people with relatively little effort. &nbsp;Most of the parties we host are during the summer when people can be outside. &nbsp;Our house is a decent size but not huge and not very open...making indoor parties more of a challenge. <br />
Today we had a small Patriots party. &nbsp;We invited Uncle Dan, Auntie Karin, Danny &amp; Sean, Gramma &amp; Papa and Grammy. &nbsp;What we thought was going to be a party for 11 turned into a party for 8. &nbsp;Sean was sick so Auntie Karin stayed home with him and Uncle Dan went to the game. &nbsp;Danny still came with Gramma, Papa and Grammy which was great because Evan was really excited to see his cousins. &nbsp;Logan had lots of smiles and stories for everyone. &nbsp;We had a nice afternoon and watched the Pats beat the Bills.<br />
