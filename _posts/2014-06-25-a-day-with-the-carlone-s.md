Today we had a wonderful, water-filled day with the Carlone's! &nbsp;Ben is home from Thailand visiting for a few weeks this summer and Kristen just arrived in RI from Minnesota last night. &nbsp;It was a beautiful, warm summer day - perfect for water squirters, a couple of (freezing cold) dips in the pool and running through the sprinkler. &nbsp;It's always a bit tough to completely catch up with little ones running around but we managed to do ok. &nbsp;As always, it was wonderful to see all of the Carlone's! &nbsp;As Evan said when we got in the car to come home, "That was a really fun party, Mom!" &nbsp;Thanks for having us!!<br />
