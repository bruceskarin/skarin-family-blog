Well this was certainly the Christmas season that kept going and going and going! &nbsp;Today is finally Christmas day and we have been opening gifts everyday for a week! &nbsp;I think Evan might be disappointed next year when the gift giving is all done within two days. &nbsp;We had a fun day, most of which was spent opening gifts and stockings and eating. &nbsp;Evan and Hannah had fun playing with all the new toys and were both extremely well-behaved considering how overwhelming Christmas can be, especially after a week long celebration. &nbsp;Christmas is definitely much more fun when there are little ones to share it with!<br />
