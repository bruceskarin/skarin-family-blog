Everyday I live with two little turkeys...today was a day celebrating them...well sort of. &nbsp;We started the day with pumpkin bread watching the Macy's Day Parade. &nbsp;Evan was pretty excited to see some of his favorite characters and was especially excited to see Santa. &nbsp;We then began the annual RI tour starting at Uncle Bob &amp; Aunt Chris's for dinner and then heading to Uncle Rick &amp; Aunt Donna's for dessert (where I guess we had our hands too full to take pictures). &nbsp;It was a long, busy day but it is always nice to see everyone. &nbsp;Once we were back at Papa &amp; Gramma's and watching the Pats game, Logan discovered his voice. &nbsp;(Videos to come at a later date.)<br />
