Logan went to the pediatrician for his 18 month well visit today. &nbsp;He has changed so much over the last few months...walking then running and talking more and more everyday. &nbsp;He is running around so much that he hasn't gained any weight! &nbsp;He's still just over 25 pounds in the 37th percentile which is funny seeing as he started out in the 90+ percentile. &nbsp;He grew almost 2 inches since November and is 34.5 inches tall (94th percentile). &nbsp;His HC is about 50 cm (95th percentile). &nbsp;I guess this explains why he fit into 18 month clothes for so long! &nbsp;I don't have Evan's 18 month stats but I will have to get them to do another comparison because up until now they are pretty much growing at the exact same rate! &nbsp;No shots today because Logan was sick earlier in the week which is probably a good thing because he cried through his entire exam. &nbsp;Poor little guy still isn't quite himself but he still continues to impress Dr. Gonroff, especially with his ever growing vocabulary. &nbsp;Hard to believe that his next appointment will be his two year check up!<br />
