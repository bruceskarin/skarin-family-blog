With each new stage comes new challenges and new rewards. &nbsp;Now that Logan is sitting up (mostly unassisted) and playing more, he and Evan have been having more fun together. &nbsp;Evan has been requesting to have Logan play with him in a particular room or with a certain toy. &nbsp;Just in the last two days, Evan has requested that Logan sit with him in his "little house", a place that he usually doesn't want anyone. &nbsp;Evan has also been learning to share toys with Logan and trying to understand that Logan's attention span is quite short and he will only play with something for a few minutes. &nbsp;Evan also played a special song for Logan..."playing a king song to the beat" and singing in his "super microphone." &nbsp;What a great big brother! &nbsp;It is clear to me that these two special little boys are already forming quite a special bond. &nbsp;One which I hope will continue throughout their lifetime together!<br />
