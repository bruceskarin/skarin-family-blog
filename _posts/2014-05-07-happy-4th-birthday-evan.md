Four years old?!?! &nbsp;What?!?! &nbsp;Already!?!?! Yup, it's true. &nbsp;Today we celebrated Evan's fourth birthday! &nbsp;It was a beautiful day. &nbsp;The perfect day to play outside with his new baseball tee. &nbsp;He opened his gifts from us in the morning and was truly excited about EVERYTHING...even the clothes! &nbsp;After playing with his new "big boy Legos, just like Danny" for a while, we headed outside to try out his new baseball tee. &nbsp;I have been wanting to get him one for a while now and it is just the perfect size for him. &nbsp;With every swing, Evan kept saying, "Step and swing". &nbsp;After a little bit, we packed up, stopped at the Queen's cups to pick up some birthday cupcakes and then went to RI to meet Gramma and Papa for lunch at Evan''s favorite place, the 99! &nbsp;After lunch, we went back to their house to play so that Evan could show them his new gifts. &nbsp;We had fun playing other games too. &nbsp;Evan kept saying "This birthday is awesome!" so much so that by the end of the night Logan was saying "Awesome, awesome!" &nbsp;Happy birthday, big guy! &nbsp;We all love you so much!<br />
