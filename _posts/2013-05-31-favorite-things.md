After Evan woke up from his nap today we went outside to enjoy some more of the early summer weather we have been experiencing the last two days. &nbsp;I asked Evan what he wanted to do outside...ride his bike, blow bubbles, color with chalk...He didn't answer me, he just kept looking around. &nbsp;Once he saw Daddy come around the corner on his tractor he looked at me and said "I found what I wanted to do! Ride with Daddy!" &nbsp;So sweet! &nbsp;<span style="text-align: center;">I love the way Evan sits with his arms rested on Daddy's legs. &nbsp;I love that they both have a sort of crooked smile...crooked on the same side!</span><br />
