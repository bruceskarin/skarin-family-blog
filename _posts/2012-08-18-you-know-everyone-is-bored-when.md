...Mommy suggests that we do Play-Doh!<br />
<br />
I think we were all feeling a little house bound on this rainy Saturday. &nbsp;We had a fun family day yesterday at West End Creamery but this morning everyone, kids and parents included, was a little cranky. &nbsp;Daddy decided to take a nap at the same time as Logan and I asked Evan if he wanted to do Play-Doh. &nbsp;You know times are tough when I willingly ask my child to play something where I know he will make a mess to clean up later...but we had lots of fun and all felt better afterwards! &nbsp;I think having a few "emergency" toys that don't get used all the time is key to beating the rainy day blues!<br />
