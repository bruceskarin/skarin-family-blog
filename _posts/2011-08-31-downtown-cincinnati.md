Early this morning, Papa dropped us off at T.F Green and we made our way to&nbsp;Cincinnati, Ohio. &nbsp;We are in town until Sunday to celebrate Uncle Gary's 60th birthday (ssshhhh....he doesn't know we are here!) and to have a Brunner family reunion...the first since before our wedding! &nbsp;We checked in, got settled, took naps and then headed out to explore downtown a bit. <br />
We had appetizers in the bar at the hotel which Evan loved because he could stand right by our table and look out the window at the bustling city. &nbsp;After that we walked down to a couple of parks and playgrounds by the Ohio river. &nbsp;It was a beautiful day to explore the city. &nbsp;The playgrounds that we found were some of the nicest I have ever been to and what was even better...we were the only ones there! &nbsp;At least at first. &nbsp;Evan had such fun exploring around, swinging and sliding. &nbsp;He even made some music on the big xylophone. &nbsp;Before heading back to our hotel we stopped at Walgreen's to get a few things and let's just say that was an interesting experience! &nbsp;We ordered some (bad) sandwiches and called it a night. &nbsp;Not a bad first day in Cincinnati!<br />
