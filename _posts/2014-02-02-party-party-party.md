This weekend was one full of parties. &nbsp;First an 85th birthday party for Bumpa at Twin Oaks on Saturday night. &nbsp;It was a long night for everyone but the kids were so well behaved and it was nice to see all of the Dowdings. &nbsp;Sunday after spending the morning at Danny and Sean's, we went to Annalia's 3rd birthday party. &nbsp;The kids had fun making paper plate crowns, opening the piñata, and playing in the transformed yoga studio. &nbsp;What a great idea for a children's party in the winter! After Lia's party, we headed home to get ready for our Super Bowl party. &nbsp;It was a small gathering, just 10 people including us, but it was fun to watch the game with some friends. &nbsp;I think it is safe to say, we are just about partied out!<br />
