When I asked Evan what type of birthday party he wanted, I thought for sure that he would say superheros. &nbsp;Much to my surprise, he didn't! &nbsp;He instead wanted to have a sports party. &nbsp;Not a baseball, basketball or soccer party but ALL sports. &nbsp;He was quite insistent on that. &nbsp;So today we had a party to celebrate his 4th birthday with friends and family and played sports...You name it, we played it...Soccer, baseball, golf, ladder golf, cornhole, biking, ring toss, jai lai, paddle ball, lacrosse, and football! &nbsp;What a great day for our little sport! &nbsp;Happy birthday, Evan!<br />
