Happy Mother's Day!  As my friend Jess said, it's almost like I had two first mother's days.  Last year I was in the hospital and on pain meds and while it was a very special day of getting to know our special guy, we didn't really do too much.  Hard to believe that a year has passed already!  <div>After the party yesterday, we decided on a pretty low-key day today.  We were going to go out for brunch but then opted for having brunch at home instead, complete with delicious omelette's made to order and mimosas!  It was wonderful.  Then we all took at nap when Evan did...it was a good day for it.  This afternoon after the weather improved a bit, we took a stroll on the bike path to get out and stretch our legs some.  When we came home, Evan tried out the Hocus Pocus chair that was Daddy's when he was a little boy.  He loved it!
