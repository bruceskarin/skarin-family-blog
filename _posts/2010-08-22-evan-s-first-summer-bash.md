This weekend we hosted our annual summer party in Evan’s honor.  While we still had the beer pong table going, this year was a little different than years past as there were quite a few children under five years old and it was a mix of friends and family.  We started a cornhole tournament (that we never finished) and introduced Evan to quite a few new people…some friends and some Kavanagh cousins.  Thank you to all who came and made it a success.  We will surely have more parties in years to come!  It was nice to have Uncle Dan, Auntie Karin, Danny and Sean sleep over and spend a lazy Sunday afternoon with us.  A preview to our trip to Myrtle Beach next month!  Can’t wait!<br />
