Today was the 44th Annual Kavanagh Family Picnic (or the Family Kavanagh Picnic as Evan likes to call it). &nbsp;We have had fun over the last few weeks talking about last year's picnic and Evan was really excited for the races. &nbsp;He remembers that last year he was too little but that he watched Danny and Daddy participate. &nbsp;It is a wonderful tradition and gathering of people who all value the importance of family. &nbsp;We had a wonderful time catching up with some we hadn't seen in a while and there were still so many others who we hardly had a chance to say "hello" too. &nbsp;I guess that is what happens in a family of this size! &nbsp;Thanks to all those who work so hard to put this even together year after year! &nbsp;Another fun day with amazing people!<br />
