Most of my blog posts are all about the boys or adventures we have with the boys but we do still have a life that doesn't always include them. &nbsp;Tonight we attended the wedding of two of our good friends, Ray and Emily. &nbsp;The Roger Williams Botanical Garden was a beautiful setting for the marriage ceremony, complete with a Carl Sagan quote. &nbsp;The relaxing reception at the Iron Works Tavern was just the right setting for everyone to mingle and chat with good friends, some of whom I hadn't seen in 4+ years. &nbsp;It was such a great night to have everyone together to celebrate with Ray and Emily. &nbsp;Congratulations to the bride and groom!! &nbsp;May you have many, many happy, healthy years together!<br />
