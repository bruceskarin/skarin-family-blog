As I think back about February, I think it has been a relatively low key month.  But as I look back at pictures, they indicate otherwise.  It started with Evan pulling himself up to standing for the first time on his exersaucer and being so proud that he was able to do so.  Then we had a play date with the Cenotti's which was fun as always.  Evan learned how to open his George in the Box without turning the crank.  There was a lot of <a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=apieC3InDas">time spent</a> trying to crawl and a couple of warm spring-like days.  There were <a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9EPKMfmtJl4">raspberries</a> in the <a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=9_l1MMZh1LE">high chair</a>, funny faces on the horsey, time spent <a href="http://www.youtube.com/watch?v=O66HKuevDq8&amp;feature=mfu_in_order&amp;list=UL">reading Mommy's birthday cards</a> and fun in the rocking chair.  And then just yesterday we had a lesson in baby-proofing from Isabelle.  And these are just the things we did that didn't have their own blog post...no wonder we need a vacation!
