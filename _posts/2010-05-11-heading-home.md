<div style="text-indent: 0.5in;">Each day at the hospital became a little easier  and on Tuesday I think we all felt ready to head home.&nbsp; We took our  time getting ready and packed up and were ready to go home around 3.&nbsp;  We got Evan loaded into his car seat, which he didn’t mind nearly as much  as I thought he would and were on our way.&nbsp; Evan slept through the  car ride home and the quick stop at CVS.&nbsp; While we were at CVS, I  called Gramma and Papa to let them know we were almost home so that they could  come and visit.&nbsp; We arrived at home around 3:30 and that is when  things got interesting!!</div>
