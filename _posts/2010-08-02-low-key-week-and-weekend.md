Wow!  It seems like I haven’t posted in forever, not just a little over a week.  (I wrote this before the 7/27, 7/28, 7/30, and 8/1 posts.)  We have had a low key stretch of about ten days or so.  There has been lots of time spent at home with Evan learning new things and perfecting old things.  I don’t feel as though I have taken as many pictures and I guess there isn’t too much to report.  Has the novelty worn off already?  It has only been just over 12 weeks!!  No way!  Maybe we are just at a stage where the changes are more subtle and we are getting used to getting so many smiles and little giggles that I don’t feel the need to write about it so much?  Or maybe we are just too busy enjoying our time to stop and take pictures?  Either way, here are a few pictures from this past week.<br />
