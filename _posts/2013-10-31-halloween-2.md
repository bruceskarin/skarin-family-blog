At the beginning of September, Evan had decided that he wanted to be a firefighter for Halloween again this year. &nbsp;It was mostly because of the fireman rain boots that Omi sent but it was fine with me because we already had everything we needed. &nbsp;We had even thought about asking Auntie Karin if she still had the dalmatian costume that Sean wore when Danny was a fireman in 2010. &nbsp;However, a couple of weeks ago, Evan decided that he wanted to be Batman. &nbsp;This was pretty easy too because Santa had brought him a cape and mask last year for Christmas. &nbsp;We needed a shirt, pants, and Batman belt and we were all set. &nbsp;I found a shirt and pants at Target (that he will be able to wear for a while...win, win! And wants to wear everyday!) and purchased what we needed to make a Batman belt. &nbsp;Once Evan decided to be Batman, we decided that Logan would be a monkey...quite fitting seeing as he is a monkey everyday!!<br />
Today, Evan wore his costume to school and had a Halloween party and parade at school. &nbsp;Logan and I went to see the parade. &nbsp;Too cute!! &nbsp;40+ 3 year olds, dressed in costume, <strike>wandering</strike>&nbsp;walking through the parking lot at Here We Grow, stopping to wave to mom or dad and trick or treat...too funny! &nbsp;The boys and I had lunch at 99, came home to examine Evan's goodies from school and rest up before going trick-or-treating tonight. &nbsp;It was a warm night and thankfully the rain from earlier had stopped. &nbsp;We went to 10 houses, got more candy than a 3 year old and a 1 year old need, and called it a night. &nbsp;I was a little disappointed that we couldn't continue our tradition of going to the Millbury library, trick-or-treating at town hall and then to see Daddy at work but today was a really fun day too! &nbsp;I guess maybe some traditions don't last too long but new ones are just as nice. <br />
