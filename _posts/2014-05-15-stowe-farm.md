Today Evan's preschool had a field trip to Stowe Farm. &nbsp;It is right here in Millbury and yet we have never been. &nbsp;The weather looked a bit iffy but the rain held off and the kids had a great time. &nbsp;There were horses, goats, pigs, chickens, a bounce house, a playground, among other things. &nbsp;The kids all planted their own seeds in cups to bring home; Evan chose a sunflower. &nbsp;I hate that I wasn't able to attend because I was with Logan and siblings weren't allowed. &nbsp;I'll have to remember to see about getting someone to stay with Logan next year so that I can go on a field trip with Evan. <br />
