Happy 11 months, little man! &nbsp;Your personality continues to be sunny and cheerful most of the time. &nbsp;You are pulling up on more and more things - the hassock, the coffee table, the play table. &nbsp;You aren't quite walking around them but that will come quickly I'm sure. &nbsp;You are feeding yourself more and more which is quite helpful to daddy and I at mealtimes. &nbsp;You are getting really good at going from your belly back to your knees and sitting even though you still aren't rolling much from your back to your belly. &nbsp;You are speaking up for yourself more often, especially if Evan does something you aren't thrilled about like takes a toy away from you. &nbsp;Your teeth are quite slow to come in. &nbsp;You still have just four but a couple more are coming. &nbsp;You are growing bigger and bigger by the day and I have been taking clothes out of Evan's drawers and putting them directly into yours. &nbsp;I can't believe that you will be one next month! &nbsp;Time is flying but I am really enjoying you grow and change. &nbsp;I love you more and more everyday!<br />
