After a low key, relaxing day yesterday, we wanted to get out and enjoy the beautiful Labor Day. &nbsp;It wasn't really warm enough to sit by the pool so we met Papa and Gramma at Chelo's by the water for lunch. &nbsp;It was slammed! &nbsp;We were told it would be over an hour to get a table outside but something opened up after only about 20 minutes. &nbsp;After a nice lunch, complete with a delicious mudslide, Evan took over the dance floor! &nbsp;The band was Good Will and Them Apples (cute name) and they were great! &nbsp;We will definitely try to see them again. &nbsp;What a great (unofficial) end to a great summer!<br />
