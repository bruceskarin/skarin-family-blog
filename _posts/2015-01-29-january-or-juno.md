I made the mistake last week of telling my Dad that I had heard on the news that Worcester had only had 9 inches of snow so far for this winter instead of the average of about 36 inches. &nbsp;Well, that all changed yesterday! Over the last couple of days, Worcester got more snow than it ever has on record before in one storm...34.5 inches. &nbsp;Guess we are up to and beyond that average now!! &nbsp;There is so much snow that it is hard for the boys to even play in but we did get out to enjoy it for a little while today.<br />
