Has it really been almost four weeks since I last posted already?  How can that be??  I guess we have been too busy having lots of fun to spend time in front of the computer putting together blog posts.  I will go back and post older updates but wanted to at least get something up so that I don’t feel so far behind.<br />
So let’s see…what’s new?  Lots!  Evan is now 16 weeks old and has met lots of new people…friends and family.  We took a trip to Chicago so Evan had his first plane ride.  We have spent lots of time with Gramma and Papa and had a fun weekend with Uncle Dan, Aunt Karin, Danny and Sean.  He is an eating machine having 8 ounces, 4-5 times per day.  He is starting to outgrow some of his clothes, especially those items that are short in the torso.  Evan’s movements and facial expressions are becoming much more deliberate and he is getting very close to rolling over.  He is beginning to outgrow his bouncy seat as he wants to be able to sit up and gets frustrated that he can’t.  Evan’s laughter, smiles and generally happy disposition are the highlight of our days!  <br />
