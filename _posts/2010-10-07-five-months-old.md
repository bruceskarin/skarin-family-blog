Evan is five months old today!!  And he is full of surprises these days.  It seems like everyday he does something new.  His hand-eye coordination improves everyday and he reaches out and grabs everything he sees, including our drinks and French fries off of my plate!  Some of Evan’s latest tricks are getting his toes into his mouth (something he has been working at for some time now); sitting up mostly on his own with only occasional help from pillows; and panting like a dog (check out the <a style="font-weight: bold;" href="http://www.youtube.com/user/dskarin213#p/u/0/NMJkoXrXZTI">video</a>, it’s funny!)  Evan has also added another food…applesauce made from the apples we picked on Sunday.  We are having so much fun as he continues to develop into a little man with a wonderful personality!  There were so many great pictures, it was hard to choose just a few.  You can find them all <a href="http://picasaweb.google.com/bruceskarin/FiveMonthPhotoShoot?authkey=Gv1sRgCKru0Nj1j6nHUA&amp;feat=email#"><span style="font-weight: bold;">here</span></a>.  Enjoy the photos!<br />
