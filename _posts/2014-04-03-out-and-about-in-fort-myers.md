This trip to Fort Myers was like most others. &nbsp;As much time in the sun and by the pool as we could get with some other outings and events mixed in for fun with the boys, meals out and our annual visit with Jon and Allie. &nbsp;(Next year we have to do a better job of getting photos of the boys with both Jon and Allie!) &nbsp;This year we also went to the beach with Papa &amp; Gramma and took the boys to Zoomers. &nbsp;Logan was a bit young for most things but Evan loved it! &nbsp;(As I am posting this in July...better late than never...Evan has already mentioned going to Zoomers again next year a few times.) &nbsp;I took the boys to Lakes Park, Bruce took Evan to pirate mini golf and Bruce and I caught the last home Red Sox spring training game of the year. &nbsp;Lots of activity packed into one vacation...Good thing Papa &amp; Gramma don't mind that we were there for more than a week!!<br />
