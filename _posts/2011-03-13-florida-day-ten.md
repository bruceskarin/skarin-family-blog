Our last full day!!  How can that be?  This week has flown by so quickly!  Today we took a ride out to Sanibel and Captiva Islands.  It was the kind of day where the sky is that perfect, I-Want-To-Bottle-It-and-Paint-A-Room-That-Color shade of blue.  We were planning to go to the Mucky Duck for lunch, a spot that Papa and Gramma really like but when we got there they were closed!  Closed on Sundays!  Bummer!  But we did take a short walk out on the beach, which was fun.  And we went to a different place, The Green Flash, which was also good and somewhere none of us had been before. <br />
