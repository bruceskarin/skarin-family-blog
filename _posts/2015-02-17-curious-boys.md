Hi Mom. &nbsp;Just wanted to tell you a couple of cute stories about the boys.<br />
<br />
I took the boys to get haircuts today. &nbsp;We went to the place in town, Matt's at the Buzzer, and they have 2 candy machines - a gumball machine and an M&amp;M dispenser. &nbsp;Logan really wanted to go across the room and look at them but didn't really want to go alone. &nbsp;He asked me and Evan to go with him but we didn't want to. &nbsp;A few minutes later, moving ever so slowly, he took one little step at a time and started moving a bit faster the closer he got to the candy. &nbsp;He was so excited once he got there and checked out the candy. &nbsp;He counted the M&amp;M's...there were 7...well according to him anyway. After that he kept bouncing back and forth across the room. &nbsp;We never did end up getting haircuts though. &nbsp;The wait was too long. &nbsp;At this rate, Papa might have to try and do it in Florida! &nbsp;:-)<br />
<br />
Then after we had lunch today, Evan asked me what the fastest bird besides the peregrine falcon is. &nbsp;He knows peregrine falcon from watching Wild Kratts with you. &nbsp;We then spent about half an hour looking up different animals...eagles, manatees, animals that are "almost extinct"...it was great! &nbsp;He is so curious about the world and loves animals and exploring. &nbsp;This also did lead to watching 3 videos of "What does the fox say?", which is Evan's new favorite song because Danny likes it too! &nbsp;But for a while he was really interested in all the different animals.<br />
<br />
Ok, gotta run, Evan just asked me to look up some facts about hawks. &nbsp;He wants to know if there is a white winged hawk and if they blend in to the snowy trees so that their predators can't see them...He's amazing! &nbsp;Talk soon! &nbsp;Love you!
