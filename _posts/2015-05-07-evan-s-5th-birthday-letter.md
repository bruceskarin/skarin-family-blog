Dear Evan,<br />
Happy 5th birthday, buddy! &nbsp;You are asleep in bed and have been for about an hour after a very busy day and week of fun and celebration. &nbsp;Today we spent the day at the New England Aquarium with Daddy, Logan, Omi and Grandpa Bob. &nbsp;It was Omi and Grandpa's last day here before heading back to Washington. &nbsp;After the aquarium and a nap in the car on the way home, we went pretty much straight to your t-ball game. &nbsp;You say you are having fun playing which I hope you are. &nbsp;I wish I could see it a little more though. <br />
You are an amazing little boy with a vivid imagination. &nbsp;You love to learn and are right on the cusp of being able to read. &nbsp;I think if we worked at it a little bit, you would be reading in no time. &nbsp;You are a sensitive little boy, sometimes a bit too sensitive when things don't go your way, especially when Logan only wants my help and not yours. &nbsp;You love to play outside, exploring and riding your bike, which you just learned to do last week! &nbsp;You love the Teenage Mutant Ninja Turtles, Raphael is your favorite. &nbsp;You love to create with Legos and are getting really good at creating your own original things. &nbsp;I think you'll be conquering that Erector set in no time. &nbsp;You adore your brother, usually, and are generally very nice to him. &nbsp;You guys have lots of fun together, especially when you add in Danny and Sean.<br />
This last year was a bit of a stressful one for you and I am seeing that reflected in your sensitivity, anxiety about new situations, and "OCD" habits. &nbsp;I hope that this is a short phase for you and that you learn to be more confident and comfortable in the amazing little boy that you are. <br />
I know that I am not always as nice or as patient as I could be and want to be...I am working on that though. &nbsp;Regardless of that, I hope you know just how much Daddy and I love you. &nbsp;We have high expectations of you because we know what an incredible boy you are. &nbsp;You are destined for great things!<br />
I am excited for the end of the school year with so many fun things coming up including your birthday party and pre-school graduation. &nbsp;I am excited for the summer and a little bit sad that you will be starting school in the fall. &nbsp;I will miss you when you are at school but I am excited to see and learn all that you do, through your eyes.<br />
I hope that you know how special you are to so many people, but especially to Daddy and I. &nbsp;I hope that you always love to learn and create and imagine wonderful things. &nbsp;I hope that you continue to grow and achieve and make us proud. &nbsp;We are so proud of you and couldn't ask for anything more...well maybe a bit better listening sometimes... &nbsp;:-)<br />
Happy 5th birthday, pal! &nbsp;I love you to the moon and back!<br />
Love,<br />
Mommy
