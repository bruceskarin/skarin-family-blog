We have a point and shoot camera that generally just sits on my dresser these days. &nbsp;Evan uses it if he wants to take pictures and I used to take it along on "outings" and play dates with me before I had my MotoX. &nbsp;Now that my cell phone takes such great photos, the little lumix mainly collects dust...Except for at night when I check on the boys. &nbsp;The are just so sweet and precious when they are asleep. &nbsp;I wish I could pause time and have them stay so sweet and innocent forever! &nbsp;But I can't so instead, I try to capture this sweet innocence on camera. &nbsp;The photos on the camera never quite do it justice but the photos in my mind will never forget!<br />
