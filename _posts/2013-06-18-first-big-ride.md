Ever since Evan got his balance bike for his birthday, we have been talking about taking him over to the bike path to ride. &nbsp;Today we finally went! &nbsp;It was a warm but cloudy day and we had a great time! &nbsp;Evan rode for about 40 minutes and based on the time it took me to walk back with the boys in the stroller I figure he covered about a mile in that time. &nbsp;Not bad for his little 3-year old legs, especially considering that most of the ride was uphill. &nbsp;We even saw a train go by and blow his horn as we waved! &nbsp;Evan didn't have much of a chance to glide so maybe next time he can catch a ride in the stroller on the way out so that he can glide on the way back. &nbsp;Such a fun morning along the Blackstone!<br />
