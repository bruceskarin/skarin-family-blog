...Everywhere you go...Including our house! &nbsp;We went to Sleigh Bell Farm to cut a fresh tree again this year. &nbsp;It's a fun family outing and we don't have so many needles on the floor at the end of the Christmas season. &nbsp;Evan and Logan were excited to help pick out the tree and even more so to test out all of the "face in a hole"s at the farm. &nbsp;We tried to get a photo for the Christmas card but...we'll you can see for yourself how that worked out. <br />
We have had a lot of fun decorating the entire house, little by little. &nbsp;For some reason doing things with a three year old and a 16 month old takes a lot longer! &nbsp;The boys have been excited and interested in helping with everything but we have had to be a bit careful where we put some of the decorations. &nbsp;There are some curious little fingers running around these days! &nbsp;We even decided to put our tree in the back corner of the house so that it was a bit more out of the way. &nbsp;It's been a great start to the holiday season continuing some old traditions from each of our families (making Oma's cookies and putting up a village like Papa's) while starting some new ones of our own. <br />
