We decided to take advantage of the beautiful day today and go to the concert on the common. &nbsp;We hadn't been to one yet this year because Logan is often napping around noon time but today with Daddy home, we made it work. &nbsp;Evan, Omi and I went to the concert with a picnic lunch to listen to <a href="http://www.pendragoncelticmusic.com/home.html">Pendragon</a>. &nbsp;This is one of my favorite groups that does these summer concerts because one of their members step dances to a few songs. &nbsp;Today, Evan was eager to dance but then shy when he was in front of people. &nbsp;Towards the end of the show though he did go on "stage" and step dance.<br />
When the concert was over, Daddy and Logan came to join us so that we could go for a walk/bike ride on the bike path. &nbsp;Evan hadn't been riding his bike a ton lately but his balance was certainly improved since the last time on the path. &nbsp;What a great way to enjoy a beautiful day!<br />
