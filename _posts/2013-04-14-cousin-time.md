Last night we had our hands full (well not really) with four of the best little boys on the planet. &nbsp;It was the night of the Boys &amp; Girls Club Auction and so we babysat for Danny and Sean. &nbsp;It has been a while since the cousins had much time to play together and they all had fun. &nbsp;Danny had a great time playing with Logan and making him laugh. &nbsp;We ordered Domino's for dinner and then Danny read to all the boys before bedtime! &nbsp;Can't believe our (not so) little nephew is reading! &nbsp;<br />
Today we went to Mozzarella's for lunch to celebrate Grammy's 70th birthday. &nbsp;Happy birthday, Grammy!<br />
