Anyone who knows Bruce knows that he is a bit of a science nerd. (I mean that in a good way! It's one of the things I love about him.) &nbsp;He understands all kinds of processes from simply to extremely complex and has already begun sharing that knowledge with Evan. &nbsp;Bruce gets pretty excited about space discoveries and new, "green" technologies. &nbsp;His carrot in life is to some day own a <a href="http://www.teslamotors.com/models/gallery#8">Tesla Model S</a>. &nbsp;For some time now, Bruce has been following the developments of Tesla Motors and found out that there was a Model S on display at the Lars Anderson Car Museum in Brookline this week. &nbsp;Bruce thought it would be a fun family outing go and check it out. &nbsp;So on Friday afternoon we drove to Brookline to check it out. &nbsp;Evan loved sitting in it, especially the back seats, specially designed for little ones. &nbsp;He also got a chance to "drive" it and didn't want Daddy to sit with him. &nbsp;It was also interesting to see the other cars they had on display, especially some of the earliest cars from the early 1900s. &nbsp;The Model S is certainly a beautiful car and I hope that someday Bruce's dream comes true. &nbsp;He works really hard for our family and he deserves it! (And maybe a <a href="http://www.teslamotors.com/modelx">Model X</a> for me!)<br />
