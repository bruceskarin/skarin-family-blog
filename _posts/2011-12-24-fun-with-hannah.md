Most of our time spent in Washington for Christmas revolved around watching Hannah and Evan play together. &nbsp;The last time they were together in the same room (not on video chat) was in August of 2010 when Evan was only 4 months old. &nbsp;Needless to say, he wasn't much fun to play with then! &nbsp;This time, however, things were different. &nbsp;The kids had so much fun playing with each other, with the exception of the occasional meltdown by either Evan or Hannah. &nbsp;But in general I think they both would say that they love their cousin!<br />
