We had a very small (3 guests), low-key Patriots party today for their first playoff game. &nbsp;We were expecting three people but two of the three who were here were different than we expected. &nbsp;We were happy to see Tara, who is the ultimate trooper having just gotten back from Hawaii last night, and for her to finally meet Logan. &nbsp;I am fully aware that Logan is the real reason she made the trek! &nbsp;Either way, it's nice to have smaller gatherings sometimes so that you have more of a chance to catch up...and always nice to have a Pats win!<br />
