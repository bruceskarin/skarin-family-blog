The last couple of day have been rough ones in our house. &nbsp;Thursday night Evan and I came down with the stomach bug just before 9 pm. &nbsp;I wasn't feeling well while I was making dinner but I thought maybe I was just too hungry. &nbsp;Then Evan didn't feel like eating and I thought it was because he was really tired from not napping that day. &nbsp;Well soon enough we found out what was really going on. &nbsp;Thursday night and Friday were really rough but thankfully Bruce wasn't sick so he could take care of us and Logan dodged the bullet for the most part too. <br />
There was a lot of TV and movie watching, loads and loads of laundry, and lots of sleeping. &nbsp;We were all feeling a bit better last night and had a special treat...popsicles! &nbsp;Not generally something you eat in the winter but certainly just what the doctor ordered for today. &nbsp;Earlier today, Evan was certain that Logan wanted to try some pear from his baby safe feeder so we let him try it. &nbsp;Did he like it? &nbsp;I'll let you be the judge. <br />
