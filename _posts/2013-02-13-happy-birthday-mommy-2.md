Today was a wonderful day! &nbsp;Evan, Logan and I finally made it out to see Jackie, Evalyn and Genevieve. &nbsp;We had been planning this play date since the beginning of December but due to busy schedules, the holidays, illness and my trip to Florida, we kept having to reschedule. &nbsp;Today we finally made it and had a wonderful visit, complete with delicious cupcakes from the Queen's Cups. &nbsp;After nap time, we went to Northworks for dinner and had a very nice evening. &nbsp;My boys then surprised me with homemade flowers and some new perfume. &nbsp;All in all, a great way to turn 34! 34...really!?!?!<br />
