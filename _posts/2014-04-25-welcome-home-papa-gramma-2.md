This is always one of our favorite days of the year...the day Gramma and Papa come home from Florida!! &nbsp;They arrived home last night around 5 pm and today we showed up around noon. &nbsp;What?? &nbsp;We gave them 12+ hours before invading! &nbsp;In all honesty, we would have been there sooner except that Evan had a dentist appointment. &nbsp;We love that they go to Florida to escape the cold, snowy winter (and that we get to go too!) but we love even more when they are just a quick drive away! &nbsp;We had a nice afternoon with them and stayed for dinner at Chelo's with Uncle Dan, Auntie Karin, Danny and Sean. &nbsp;Great to have everyone back together again!<br />
