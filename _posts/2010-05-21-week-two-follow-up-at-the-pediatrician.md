Our second week at home proved to be much less  eventful than the first.&nbsp; We had begun to figure each other out a  bit more and were falling into a nice rhythm.&nbsp; We celebrated  Grandpa Bob’s birthday on Monday the 17<sup>th</sup>, spent some more quality  time with Grandpa Bob and Omi before saying goodbye to them on Wednesday the  19<sup>th</sup>, had another visit from Papa and Gramma (a dry one this time!),  and went to a few stores to run a few errands.&nbsp; It was nice to be  out of the house!!&nbsp; A bit more complicated than it used to be but  still nice!<br />
