Daddy took today off from work to spend his birthday with his two favorite little pals, Omi and me. &nbsp;It was a beautiful day and we had lunch outside at the Vintage Grille in Worcester. &nbsp;We had never been there before and were pleasantly surprised with the selection and quality of the food. &nbsp;We had planned to spend part of the afternoon at a corn maze only to find out that most of them are only open on weekends. &nbsp;Instead we went to the playground near our house so that Evan could run around with Daddy and Omi. &nbsp;It wasn't as much fun as a corn maze would have been but it was still fun. &nbsp;We had a nice dinner and finished the night with Daddy's favorite, peaches 'n cream pie! &nbsp;Happy birthday, Daddy!<br />
