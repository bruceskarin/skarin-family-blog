Recently in the mail we received a picture of Auntie Kristina and Hannah inside a wolf den, howling like and calling the wolves. &nbsp;While Evan and Daddy were playing in the snow today, they did the snowy version. &nbsp;After playing in the snow for almost two hours, it was time for Evan's first mug of hot chocolate in the Santa mug that I used to drink hot chocolate from after I played in the snow as a kid. &nbsp;Evan wasn't a big fan of the hot chocolate but he sure liked the Santa mug and I'm sure he will grow to love the hot chocolate soon! &nbsp;What a great way to end a fabulous year!<br />
