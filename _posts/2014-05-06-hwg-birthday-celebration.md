Today Evan celebrated his birthday with his friends at Here We Grow Preschool. &nbsp;It is so nice to see how far he has come this year and how much he has learned. &nbsp;I love that he has made his first friends, well ones that aren't his cousins or children of our friends. &nbsp;He was prince for the day and brought in a special snack of trail mix "but this time with fruit loops!" to share with his friends. &nbsp;Hard to believe that my little guy will be 4 tomorrow! &nbsp;I love to watch him grow and learn but also wish I could slow things down. &nbsp;Can't wait to see what the next year will bring!<br />
