We stayed over and on Saturday had a gathering with the usual suspects – Bumpa, Grammy, Uncle Dan Aunt Karin, Danny, Sean, and of course Gramma and Papa.  We were at their house after all.  The boys were all so good and Danny was extremely excited to swim in the pool with his Daddy and Uncle Bruce.  He is a great swimmer especially considering that he is only 3 years old.  He has “super power” and no fear – swimming in the deep end, jumping in like crazy and going down the slide all by himself.  We had a great day and Danny was entertaining us all late into the night.  I can’t wait until all three boys are saying the cute things that he does.<br />
