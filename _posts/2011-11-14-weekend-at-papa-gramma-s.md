This past weekend was our first weekend away from Evan since he was born. &nbsp;Bruce has taken business trips or been places over night like camping but I have not had a single night away from Evan in a little over 18 months. <br />
We went to Luray, Virginia to celebrate the wedding of Adria and Avi and it was a much needed weekend! &nbsp;It was nice to be able to come and go as we pleased without considering nap time or whether or not Evan would enjoy what we were doing. &nbsp;We both love our little guy but it is nice to have some "us" time too. <br />
We had a wonderful weekend celebrating a special couple and were so glad to hear that Evan was an absolute angel for Papa and Gramma. &nbsp;They had so much fun together that I don't think Evan really missed us at all...which is just fine! &nbsp;Sorry, no pictures of Evan's weekend this time...just pictures of ours. <br />
