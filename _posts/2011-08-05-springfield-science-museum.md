Today Evan and I took advantage of the <a href="http://www.highlandstreet.org/index.php?id=79&amp;option=com_content&amp;view=article">Free Fun Friday's</a> summer program and went to the Springfield Museums.  We went to the science museum and walked through the Dr. Seuss Sculpture Garden then had a picnic lunch.  Evan enjoyed the science museum especially seeing all of the animals from the African plains and the life-sized replica of the T-Rex.  As you can tell from the photos, Evan was more interested in looking around than looking at the camera.  That's ok though...that is after all, why we went!
