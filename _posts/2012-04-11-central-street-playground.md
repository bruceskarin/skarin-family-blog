Today we met the Wheeler's at Fresh City for lunch and then went to the Central Street playground in Southboro. &nbsp;We had never been to a Fresh City before and we were pleasantly surprised. &nbsp;They have quite a large selection of interesting salads and sandwiches. &nbsp;After attempting (four times) to schedule some open jump time at Jump Around in Framingham, we decided some outdoor fun would be better anyway. &nbsp;The kids had a great time playing in the sand and on the swings. &nbsp;Dylan and Evan had tons of fun playing together. &nbsp;Always nice to check out a different playground, especially with friends!<br />
