Today, after going out to breakfast with everyone, Bruce and I left Evan with Gramma and Papa for the day and headed to jetBlue Park aka Fenway South for a Red Sox game. &nbsp;It was a beautiful day for a game and we were excited to see the new park. &nbsp;They modeled many aspects of the park after Fenway but it also reminded me quite a bit of City of Palms. &nbsp;The game was a blowout...and not in the Sox favor but we still had a great time anyway!<br />
