We haven't had as many cousins get togethers lately. &nbsp;In fact the last Kavanagh cousins get together was almost one year ago. &nbsp;Everyone is so busy and getting sitters is always tough so today we hosted a family one. &nbsp;It was a beautiful day and so fun to have all the kids running around and playing together. &nbsp;A bit different than the typical get together, especially the last one, but still a great day spent with wonderful people! &nbsp;Thanks to all those who made the trek to Millbury! &nbsp;Let's try to do this a bit more often, ok?<br />
