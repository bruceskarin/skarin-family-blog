Our Thanksgiving celebration was a bit quieter this year than in years passed. &nbsp;We started the day with the Macy's parade as usual. &nbsp;The boys weren't super interested and I have to say I don't blame them. &nbsp;The entire first hour was just NBC promoting their own shows and Broadway musicals. &nbsp;Unfortunately by the time we were able to see many of the floats, balloons and bands, they had mostly lost interest. <br />
When the parade was over, we quickly video conferenced with Omi and Grandpa Bob, who were visiting the Smuckers in Montana. &nbsp;It is nice to be able to "see" them even though we can't be with them today. &nbsp;Then we headed to RI for our Thanksgiving feast at Uncle Bob and Aunt Chris's. &nbsp;It was a smaller gathering this year, only 21 people. &nbsp;Still plenty big for such an involved meal!<br />
Normally after the big Dowding feast, we go to have dessert with the Kavanagh's but unfortunately Aunt Donna and Uncle Rick weren't feeling up to it this year. &nbsp;It is a huge undertaking and they always host a fabulous day but we understand that it just wasn't in the cards for this year. &nbsp;We did, however, stop by to drop off some pumpkin bread for them to enjoy over the weekend. &nbsp;Wishing you lots of love and good health to all of our family today, especially Uncle Rick and Aunt Donna! &nbsp;Happy Thanksgiving!<br />
