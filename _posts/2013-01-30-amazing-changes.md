Over the last three and a half weeks, we have seen amazing changes in Logan. &nbsp;He has started talking a lot and found a new, non-screechy voice with lots of ma-ma's and da-da's and tongue flipping. &nbsp;His movements have become much more deliberate. &nbsp;You can almost watch the gears turning in his head..."I'm going to grab that ring. &nbsp;Got it. &nbsp;I'm going to put it in my mouth. &nbsp;Done." &nbsp;He studies his hands in the same way that Papa describes Danny doing when he was little. &nbsp;He loves to grab his toes and try to get them into his mouth, especially when you are changing his diaper and he has no pants on. &nbsp;Logan has started sitting up more and more on his own. &nbsp;He is still quite wobbly and falls over quite often but he can sit for a minute or two unassisted. &nbsp;He is also, finally, getting the hang of eating from a spoon a bit better. &nbsp;Then, just tonight he rolled over twice from his back to his belly. &nbsp;He has been rolling from his belly to his back, when he feels like it, since the beginning of December but not the other direction. &nbsp;Although I must say, I have not actually seen him roll from his back to his belly. &nbsp;Twice tonight he was on his back when I left the room to go to the kitchen for something and when I came back he was either on his belly or no where near where he was when I left, leading me to assume that he rolled. &nbsp;He has been close for a few days so I guess he finally put it all together. &nbsp;Growing up so fast already!<br />
