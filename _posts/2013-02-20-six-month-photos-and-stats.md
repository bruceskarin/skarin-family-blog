I left for Florida on the day that Logan turned 6 months old. &nbsp;He was all smiles when we woke him up to go to the airport but later that day, after a trip to the pediatrician, we found out that he had pink eye. &nbsp;Poor little guy! &nbsp;Thankfully, he kept this pink eye to himself and miraculously only one eye and after a little ointment, he was better in no time.<br />
Today we went back to the pediatrician. &nbsp;This time for a well visit. &nbsp;Dr. Gonroff was quite pleased to see how he is developing...gaining weight and growing like a champ, sitting up, clapping, talking, etc. &nbsp;His six month stats once again measure up quite closely to Evan's and are as follows: height 28.5 inches, weight 20 lbs, 8 oz, head circumference 46.5 cm. &nbsp;Evan's and Logan's stats have been so close that I had to do the nerdy thing and graph it. After all, a picture is worth a thousand words, right? &nbsp;All of the measurements are really close but the weight simply amazes me. &nbsp;I wonder what the 9 month stats will show...will Evan pull ahead or will Logan stay on track now that he is eating more??? &nbsp;Stay tuned!<br />
