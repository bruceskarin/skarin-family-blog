Logan, today you are three months old! &nbsp;You are smiley, giggly, nosey, chatty, fidgety, wiggly and drooly. &nbsp;You weigh 16 pounds, 13 ounces and are wearing 6 month clothes and size two (soon to be size three) diapers. &nbsp;You have lived through some hot, humid, end of summer weather as well as tropical storm/hurricane Sandy (which was really pretty uneventful in Millbury) and as I type this we are getting our first snowfall of the season. &nbsp;Yesterday, you came with me while I went to vote and Barack Obama was elected for a second term as the 44th president of the United States of America. &nbsp;A lot has happened in your little lifetime. <br />
More importantly, you are a thriving, growing, learning little man. &nbsp;You are still eating about 30 ounces per day and are more consistently sleeping through the night for longer and longer stretches of time. &nbsp;You rarely wake up in the middle of the night anymore but you often do like to say "good morning" between 5 and 6. &nbsp;Although the last two nights you have slept for almost 12 hours with the exception of one 9 or 10 pm feeding for which you barely woke up. If your big brother is in the room, you watch him where ever he goes, running, playing, talking and singing. &nbsp;You love to suck on your fingers and thumb, especially when you are hungry or tired. &nbsp;You give the best smiles and have this sweet, bashful little look that often follows your smiles. <br />
I am excited to see what the next three months (and beyond) brings and to see what your little personality will be like. &nbsp;Will you be silly and entertaining like Evan or more laid back like Sean? &nbsp;Will you play by the rules like Danny or be an independent adventurer like Hannah? &nbsp;Will you continue to be built like a Kavanagh or become long and lean like Daddy? &nbsp;Only time will tell. &nbsp;For now, just keep growing, smiling, learning, eating and sleeping like you do and I know you will do amazing things! &nbsp;I love you, little one. &nbsp;Love, mom.<br />
