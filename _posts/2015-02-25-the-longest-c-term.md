Hi Mom. &nbsp;It's me. &nbsp;Just checking in while all the boys, big and small, are over at the pool. &nbsp;Remember when we used to talk about how quickly the 7 week terms at WPI went by. Well not this one! &nbsp;This has been the longest "C term" of my life. &nbsp;It's been 6 weeks since I last held your warm hand and kissed your warm cheek. &nbsp;It's been 7 and a half weeks since we last spoke. &nbsp;Amazing that 7 weeks used to fly by and this has been an eternity!<br />
<div>
<br /></div>
<div>
I think about you all the time. &nbsp;I hope you can feel it. &nbsp;There are so many little things that I miss. &nbsp;Your laugh, your smile. &nbsp;The way you always had special little plates for the boys when we came to Florida. &nbsp;The way you always went out of your way to be sure that we had everything we needed. &nbsp;The way you would watch the morning news in bed and let the little monkeys join you to snuggle or watch their own show. &nbsp;(Logan snuggled with Dad for a while this morning...I think they both enjoyed it!) &nbsp;The way you would appreciate the fact that Evan's new crocs are bright green just like his Ninja Turtle bathing suit...and I didn't even plan it! &nbsp;The way your face would light up when Logan would have something new to show you. &nbsp;Your consistency, your kindness, your love...YOU!!!</div>
