We have a Christmas tradition that started a few years ago of making cookies and giving away tins to family members. &nbsp;This is something Bruce used to do as a child and it has been fun for us to continue the tradition. &nbsp;Some years get quite out of hand...making six or seven different kinds and multiple dozens of each. &nbsp;This year, for a number of reasons, we scaled back our production. &nbsp;I thought about skipping them altogether but then thought that too many people would be disappointed so we chose to make less kinds and only ones that were easy. &nbsp;Evan enjoyed helping unwrap (and eating) Rolos and Kisses...especially the eating part! &nbsp;Next year we will be back to full production with our great little helper!<br />
