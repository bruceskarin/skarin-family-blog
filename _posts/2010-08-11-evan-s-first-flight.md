Evan’s first plane ride took him to Chicago for a Skarin family reunion.  I was a little nervous about traveling with him and all of the gear that accompanied us but it was much easier than I imagined.  It was nice to be able to fly out of T.F. Green in the middle of the afternoon, a relatively slow time of the day.  Getting through security was a breeze and we were able to feed Evan before boarding the plane.  He was fussy for a couple of minutes when they first closed the door and began to pressurize the cabin but as soon as we started moving he fell asleep and slept the entire way.  What a little angel!  (He was just as good on the return trip as well!)<br />
