A retelling of a conversation between Evan and Uncle Glenn from Easter<br />
<div>
<br /></div>
<div>
Evan: Were you the one who carried that box?</div>
<div>
Uncle Glenn: Which box?</div>
<div>
Evan: That box of candy</div>
<div>
Uncle Glenn: Oh you mean the robin's eggs...yes</div>
<div>
Evan: No, no, not the robin's eggs. &nbsp;The other box, of candy, the big box, on the table out there</div>
<div>
Uncle Glenn: Oh, yeah, I put them on the table</div>
<div>
Evan: Oh, ok</div>
<div>
(Long pause)</div>
<div>
Uncle Glenn: Did you want something?</div>
<div>
Evan: No, I was just wondering if you were the one who carried that box</div>
<div>
Uncle Glenn (to me): This is the way my wife asks questions too</div>
<div>
Uncle Glenn (to Evan): Did you want to see what is in the box?</div>
<div>
Evan: Oh, ok, yeah, sure!</div>
<div>
<br /></div>
<div>
Evan then proceeded out to the living room with Uncle Glenn and helped him open and eat some of the Whitman's Sampler that Evan saw Uncle Glenn place on the coffee table shortly after arriving at their house for Easter dinner. &nbsp;He waited patiently all day for this conversation and it was adorable!</div>
