Once I started taking daily pictures of Logan, I began feeling like I wasn't taking many pictures of Evan. &nbsp;I decided to start a 365 days of Evan as well. &nbsp;It's harder to get "good" pictures because he is always on the move but I love seeing all of his different activities. &nbsp;Enjoy!<br />
<br />
(edit 3/31) I added the note below to Logan's 365 days post. &nbsp;It is even more true for Evan. &nbsp;I could write 3-4 posts per day on just the things Evan says and does. &nbsp;Considering I am at any given point and time weeks behind in posting that is just not possible! &nbsp;This will have to suffice. &nbsp;It is often hard to pick just one photo! I think you will notice a theme in the pictures from March...determination and concentration. <br />
<br />
When I started the 365 days of Logan at the beginning of the year, I felt that captioning (is that even a word?) every photo for the year was a bit&nbsp;<strike>OCD</strike>&nbsp;out of control so I decided not to. &nbsp;But then for many photos I found that there was a cute moment or story being lost because I wasn't captioning each photo. &nbsp;I'm not saying that every photo will have a caption but I am going to start adding some in hopes of capturing those little moments without creating a new post entirely. &nbsp;(If I ever get caught up and have nothing else to do...not likely with two little monkeys keeping me busy...I will go back and caption January and February.) I will indicate if the photos have been used twice.<br />
<br />
(edit 4/30) Evan is always on the go, loving life and living it to the fullest. &nbsp;He loves to be outside and be silly!<br />
<br />
(edit May/June) I made it 5 full months but missed my first day for Evan on June 1st. &nbsp;I included a photo from June 1st last year...when Evan used to sit and pose for pictures. &nbsp;Now he's always on the move!<br />
<br />
(edit July) Hard to believe how quickly the summer is going and how big our little guy is getting!<br />
<br />
(edit August) Enjoying the end of the summer before Evan starts preschool next month..<br />
