Hi Mom. &nbsp;Just checking in to let you know that I thought about you a ton today...so many different reasons, so many different times. &nbsp;I was trying to do some cleaning which is quite challenging with your two littlest monkeys around. &nbsp;It's like "brushing your teeth while eating Oreo's" to quote Auntie Karin! &nbsp;I was able to get quite a bit done but I kept thinking about all the times I was "cleaning" but really I was talking to you! &nbsp;You were always my best procrastination pal!<br />
Then when I was cleaning up from lunch, I needed new yellow gloves so I opened the drawer and found 2 right handed gloves and started to cry. &nbsp;Who am I going to give my right handed gloves to now? &nbsp;I still think it's funny how you always wore out the right and I wore out the left. &nbsp;A perfect pair!<br />
After I finally had a chance to clean the living room, the boys and I decorated a bit. &nbsp;We put up some of your Easter decorations along with the few that we had. &nbsp;They just love the "rockin' down" tree! &nbsp;I wonder if Eli would remember that tree. &nbsp;He was the one who coined the phrase "rockin' down" &nbsp;and "na-night...wakin'"...probably about when he was Logan's age. &nbsp;Boy how quickly time has past!<br />
In addition to general cleaning, I was cleaning out today too. &nbsp;Another one of our favorite past-times. &nbsp;Well, actually, now that I think about it...I can't think of anything we didn't enjoy doing together. &nbsp;Anyway, I have two bags and a box ready for donation next Saturday; 2 boxes of clothes, Rocky the rocking dog, and pewi for Jonah; 1 bag of maternity clothes for my friend Caitlin that teaches barre; the space saver high chair for Kerry Friel; one of the double strollers is going back to Kerri Vickers for a friend of hers; the 4 black tea light lanterns for Aunt Donna; and another 2 boxes of baby things to either donate or pass on to Emily for her cousin. &nbsp;With all that we donate throughout the year, I am amazed that we still have all of this stuff!! &nbsp;I am pretty excited about cleaning it all out and Bruce thought that was cute. <br />
In other news, we've been having a hard time with Evan lately. &nbsp;We've all been yelling, arguing, fighting, over-reacting and crying way too much! &nbsp;I try to stay calm and patient but I have been failing...just failing...at it. &nbsp;He is just so sensitive about every little thing right now...but then again, so am I. &nbsp;I could really use some strength to love him more and yell at him less! &nbsp;Any help would be greatly appreciated. <br />
Well it's getting late and I've been going since about 7:30 this morning when Logan woke up and then got sick in our bed because he was coughing so much so I think I'm gonna call it a night. &nbsp;What I wouldn't give to pick up the phone and hear your voice...I love you!<br />
<br />
Oh just one more thing before I go...Dad bought me the same card this year for my birthday that you bought me (I think) last year for my birthday...You taught him well! &nbsp;Love you!<br />
<br />
The photos are a little fuzzy...but you get the idea.<br />
