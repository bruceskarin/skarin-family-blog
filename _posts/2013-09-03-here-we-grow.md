Today was Evan's first day of preschool at Here We Grow in Auburn. &nbsp;As a mom who has been home with her little guy since the day he was born, this was a big day for us! &nbsp;It was the first time that we would be taking Evan somewhere and leaving him there for a few hours when he wasn't with friends or family or our trusted babysitter. &nbsp;I have known this day was coming since the spring when we decided which preschool to send him too and I experienced just about every emotion possible today. &nbsp;Happy, sad, anxious, excited, nervous...you name it, I felt it all. &nbsp;Would the other kids play with Evan...would he play with them? &nbsp;Would they be nice to him...would he freak out if something didn't quite go his way? &nbsp;Would he like it...would he learn a lot? &nbsp;Would he remember to use his manners...would he have an accident? &nbsp;I could go on and on. <br />
We have been talking about him going to school in the recent weeks, trying to explain a little bit of what he could expect. &nbsp;He would go from being excited about it to nervous to anxious. &nbsp;I guess he was feeling all of the same things as I was. <br />
Today, after I shed a few tears last night, we woke up, got dressed, had breakfast and headed out the door. &nbsp;We took a few minutes for some photos at home and then we were off. &nbsp;I was doing ok, a bit stressed but ok, until I started the car. &nbsp;Then the water works started. I tried hard not to let Evan know I was crying, I didn't want him to be upset. &nbsp;He was quite all the way there in the car and thankfully I was able to pull it together. &nbsp;We went in the building, washed hands as is the procedure at HWG, and got in line to head into his classroom. &nbsp;Evan got started at the play-doh table just inside the door and was doing ok. &nbsp;I on the other hand was fighting back tears the entire time (as I am doing now...and I am posting this 4 weeks later)! &nbsp;I kissed him on the top of the head and basically ran out of the room. &nbsp;I didn't want him to see me crying and I didn't want to be THAT mom. &nbsp;Thankfully Bruce was with us so he said goodbye to Evan and drove home so I didn't have to try and see through the tears. &nbsp;Deep down I knew he would be fine and I knew that with time he would come to love preschool but he is still my baby and is growing up way too fast! &nbsp;I went back to pick Evan up two and a half hours later and he had had a great day! &nbsp;Let's hope that this is the start of a very successful school career for our little man!<br />

(As I mentioned, I am posting this late and Evan has already completed 4 weeks of school. &nbsp;The second day drop-off was much harder on both of us because Evan was crying and he didn't want me to leave. &nbsp;There were a couple of other days when he was reluctant to go but not too bad and one day in particular that just broke my heart. &nbsp;He had been saying all morning that he didn't want to go to school, he didn't want me to start the car or park the car, etc. &nbsp;I knew it was going to be tough. &nbsp;He pulled it together for the most part but then when I went to leave and looked back at him, he was standing in the middle of the room, crying, sad and needing a hug. &nbsp;It took everything I had not to pick him up and take him home. &nbsp;That was week 2 or 3. &nbsp;Last week there were no issues and on Thursday when we pulled in to the parking lot, Evan shouted "School!" &nbsp;He is beginning to really like it and even sits with the other kids a circle time, which he didn't do for the first 3 days.)
