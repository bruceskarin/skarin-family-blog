Today was Evan's first field trip for school. &nbsp;The three-year olds from Here We Grow preschool went apple picking at Tougas Family farm. &nbsp;I would have loved to have chaperoned but there were no siblings allowed. &nbsp;So I dropped Evan off with his teachers, feeling a little guilty that I couldn't join them once I saw how many other moms were there. &nbsp;While Evan was off having a great time with his classmates, Logan and I had some fun of our own - snacking on apple cider donuts, picking apples, and playing at the picnic tables. &nbsp;<br />
