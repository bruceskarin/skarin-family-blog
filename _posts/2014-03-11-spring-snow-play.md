Today was the best of both worlds for a kid...warm enough to play outside for a long time but still plenty of snow to play in. &nbsp;We had the shovels and buckets out; we had chalk, bubbles and bikes out. &nbsp;It was beautiful! &nbsp;The boys stayed mostly dry in regular pants and rain boots and it was warm enough not to need heavy winter jackets. &nbsp;Let's hope there are more of these days in our near future!<br />
