We've had an amazing start to summer vacation! &nbsp;Yesterday was the first full day of vacation and we started the day playing school. &nbsp;We then took a "field trip" to Purgatory Chasm and West End Creamery. &nbsp;Today we went to the touch a truck event in Northboro then came home for slip n' slide fun and a visit from the ice cream truck. &nbsp;What a way to kick off summer! &nbsp;Not sure we'll keep up this much excitement all summer but if these last two days are any indication, we are going to have an awesome summer!!<br />
