Same activity, different monkeys, and different zoo than&nbsp;<a href="http://evan.skarin.us/2011/08/three-little-monkeys.html">before</a>. &nbsp;This time the monkeys were Danny, Sean and Evan and the zoo was Southwick's. &nbsp;What a beautiful day to visit Southwick's Zoo, especially considering that school is back in session. &nbsp;It felt as if we had the entire place to ourselves. &nbsp;We had a little bit of a rough start to the day which included a flat tire on Danny and Sean's stroller and Sean wanting to be able to just take a nap but we quickly got going and had a wonderful time. &nbsp;After lunch the boys played at the playground for a while before we completed our tour of the zoo and fed deer in the deer park. &nbsp;Southwick's is definitely a place worth visiting!<br />
