Evan won't be one for still a little over three weeks but he has already received some early birthday presents.  He got a lawn mower and tool bench from Mom and Dad and a ride-on giraffe and a sports center from Gramma and Papa.  He must have been a good little boy!  It will be a little while before Evan can really play with the mower and giraffe on his own but he is having tons of fun with the sports center (especially when the music plays) and loves it when we push him around on the giraffe.  Happy early birthday, little guy!
