Many Monday's are spent catching up from the weekend (aka doing laundry) or preparing for the week ahead (aka going grocery shopping). &nbsp;But not today. &nbsp;Today was all about fun! &nbsp;I ran to the grocery store quickly (aka on my own!) this morning before heading to drop off Bruce's car for new tires. &nbsp;I had the boys in the car already so after we dropped Daddy at home, we went to the playground for a while. &nbsp;We came home, had some lunch and spent the rest of the day outside. &nbsp;Who knew that so much fun could be had with a pair of rain boots, a bucket and 2 water blasters from the Dollar Store??? &nbsp;Such a beautiful, fun day in the sun! <br />
