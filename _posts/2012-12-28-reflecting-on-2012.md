As 2012 comes to a close, you might ask if it was a good year. &nbsp;I think so. &nbsp;We had a wonderful year, filled with amazing experiences and milestones. &nbsp;In case you missed any of it or want to go back and relive some of it, here are the highlights. <br />
We started 2012 with a <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/01/surprise-gramma.html">surprise</a> trip to Florida to celebrate Gramma's 60th birthday in January. We celebrated a few more special <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/02/happy-birthday-mommy.html">birthdays</a> and enjoyed lots of <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/02/providence-childrens-museum.html">cousin time</a> in February. &nbsp; &nbsp; &nbsp;Evan <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/03/evan-meets-george.html">met Curious George</a>, we enjoyed an <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/03/spring-has-sprungone-week-later.html">early start to spring</a>, had lots of fun at Danny and Sean's <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/03/danny-seans-bowling-birthday-party.html">bowling birthday party</a> and enjoyed another great trip to Fort Myers, including Evan's<a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/03/evans-first-visit-to-fort-myers-beach.html"> first trip to Fort Myers Beach</a>. April was filled with a lot of <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/04/accessorizing.html">silliness</a> and starting to get ready for our new little guy and moving Evan into a <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/04/prepping-and-painting-big-boy-room.html">big boy room</a> with <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/04/big-boy-furniture.html">big boy furniture</a>. &nbsp;In May, we celebrated <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/05/curious-george-birthday-party.html">Evan's 2nd birthday</a>, had a wonderful visit with <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/05/happy-mothers-day.html">Omi and Grandpa</a>, and took Evan to <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/05/uncle-bobs-cabin.html">Uncle Bob's cabin </a>for the first time. &nbsp;June and July were filled with summer fun including a <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/06/week-with-danny-sean.html">week with Danny and Sean</a>, lots of&nbsp;<a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/06/pool-timefinally.html">pool time</a>&nbsp;so that pregnant mommy could try to beat the heat, and enjoying our last moments as a <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/07/one-last-hurrah.html">family of three</a>. &nbsp;In August, we <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/08/welcome-logan.html">welcomed Logan</a> into this world and our family! In September, we had another nice visit with Omi and Grandpa so that they could <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/09/grandpa-and-omi-meet-logan.html">meet Logan</a>, celebrated Daddy's birthday with a trip to <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/09/lets-go-red-sox.html">Fenway park</a> and celebrated <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/09/papa-grammas-40th-anniversary.html">Papa &amp; Gramma's 40th anniversary</a>. &nbsp;By October, it was clear that Evan was going to be a<a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/10/brothers.html"> great big brother</a>. &nbsp;We also had a (semi) surprise <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/10/gramma-papas-anniversary-party.html">party for Papa and Gramma </a>and had a fun <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/10/halloween.html">Halloween</a>. &nbsp;November was full of <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/11/i-was-thinking-about-doing-little.html">projects</a>, Logan's <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2013/01/two-little-turkeys.html">first Thanksgiving</a> and getting ready for <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/11/beginning-to-look-at-lot-like-christmas.html">Christmas</a>. &nbsp;December reminded us what is <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/12/hug-little-tighter-hold-little-longer.html">really important </a>in life and about being thankful for wonderful family and friends during <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/12/merry-christmas.html">the holidays</a>. <br />
Seems like a pretty good year to me. &nbsp;And those are just the highlights! &nbsp;There are so many wonderful memories that we shared with all of the amazing people in our lives. &nbsp;We are so thankful to have each of you and are glad to be able to share a little piece of our life with you, our loyal readers! &nbsp;Thank you for reading and stay tuned for more adventures of our little (getting big too quickly!) guys!<br />
