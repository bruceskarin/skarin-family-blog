Today I took the boys to the Touch Tomorrow event at WPI. &nbsp;It is a festival of science, technology and robots, on a beautiful campus and it is an awesome event for children of any age. &nbsp;Plus it's free!! &nbsp;Pretty much a no brainer as far as I'm concerned! &nbsp;Evan and especially Logan are a bit on the young side for many of the activities and still require quite a bit of help for most things but there was plenty for them to do and try. &nbsp;They had fun driving a Mars Rover simulator, playing catch with a robot, watching Curious George build a tower of cups, learning about the screech owl and red-tailed hawk, making a volcano erupt, and making slime, among other things. &nbsp;It was a great day for the event too...mostly cloudy and overcast but not too hot and the rain held off. &nbsp;This is certainly a great way to get kids excited about science and technology. &nbsp;Definitely an event that we will be attending in the years to come!<br />
