Today we attended a small Memorial Day remembrance ceremony and parade in Millbury. &nbsp;We wanted to do something as a family to give the boys a little idea of what Memorial Day is all about. &nbsp;No, its' not just the unofficial kick off of summer, filled with beach parties and BBQ's. &nbsp;It was a small parade but the ceremonies were nice, one at the WWII memorial at town hall and one in Central Cemetery.<br />
In the afternoon, we went to Chelo's Waterfront to hear Sweet Tooth and the Sugar Babies. &nbsp;We met Gramma &amp; Papa, Jen &amp; Mike and Nicole. &nbsp;It wasn't a spectacular day weather wise but we still had a great time singing and dancing with friends and family!<br />
