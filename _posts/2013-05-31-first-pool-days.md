After the weather of the last couple of days it is hard to believe that less than one week ago we were all "freezing"! &nbsp;Summer has arrived!! &nbsp;And is hopefully here to stay!! &nbsp;We have been enjoying keeping cool by playing in the water table, the little pool and the "big" pool. &nbsp;On Thursday, Logan wasn't sure what to think of the kiddie pool...it was so chilly! &nbsp;But after sitting beside it for a bit, he was eager to climb in. &nbsp;When he scooched himself over to the water table Evan said, "I'll get my McQueen chair so that Logan can enjoy the water table". &nbsp;What a sweet big brother! &nbsp;But today, Logan was ready to "jump" right in...I'm sure it helped that the pool was set up on an incline and there was only about two inches of water where Logan was sitting. &nbsp;Evan had so much fun getting in and out, splashing, "swimming" and jumping!<br />
