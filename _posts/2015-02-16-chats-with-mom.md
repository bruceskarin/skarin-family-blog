As I sit here and type to you, Mom, Evan and Logan are in the bathroom and Evan is teaching Logan how to use the potty. &nbsp;Evan has the little Elmo potty and the seat that goes on the big toilet. &nbsp;Evan is encouraging Logan to try and Logan is pretty receptive to it...until I go in. &nbsp;Then Logan wants to put his diaper back on and doesn't want to try. &nbsp;It is the cutest thing! &nbsp;Evan is saying "I'm so proud of you, Logan" to which Logan responds "I'm so proud of you too!" &nbsp;Evan said he will give Logan a quarter of his own. They are sweet boys! &nbsp;Ok, Evan just came in to tell me that "Logan doesn't really want to do it now but maybe he'll do it later. &nbsp;He can't really do it right now because it just happens sometimes." &nbsp;Logan just came out and said "Mom, I want you to help me in the bathroom. &nbsp;The Elmo potty isn't really working." &nbsp;I can almost hear you laugh! &nbsp;I hope you can see these two monkeys! &nbsp; And I have been meaning to tell you how much Logan has changed since Christmas...It's amazing! &nbsp;His vocabulary, his expresssions, his fine motor skills and how much he knows...Just unbelievable! &nbsp;He calls me a silly goose. &nbsp;Guess he isn't going to stay little forever! &nbsp;Gotta run...Love you!<br />
<br />
And obviously on the coldest day of the year, these two monkeys are running around, dancing in their undies!!<br />
