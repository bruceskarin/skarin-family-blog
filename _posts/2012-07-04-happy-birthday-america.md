Today was quite possibly one of the most relaxing 4th of July celebrations we have ever had. &nbsp;The little boys were all well behaved, had a great time swimming, took good naps (well Sean and Evan anyway) and loved the new floats - a manta ray, a blue whale and a pirate ship - that Papa brought home from Florida. &nbsp;I lounged in my new mesh float for most of the afternoon...quite possibly the best $20 I have spent this year! A rather low key day but a perfect one as well! &nbsp;Happy birthday, America! <br />
