Today we spent the day helping out a good friend who due to back trouble isn't able to drive right now. &nbsp;We picked him up at his house in Hopkinton and brought him to his doctor's appointment in Worcester. &nbsp;While he was at his appointment, we took care of a few things at the Auburn mall - got mom and dad's watches fixed, had a pretzel snack and played in the kids play area for a bit. &nbsp;Once he was done with his appointment, we went to the 99 for lunch and then helped him run a few more errands. &nbsp;It was a lot of time in the car for the boys today but they were great...Never complaining at all! &nbsp;So nice to be able to help out a friend who has done SO much for us in the past! &nbsp;After dinner, my special valentine's got to open a few little gifts!<br />
