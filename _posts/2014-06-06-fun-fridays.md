Today started out a little bit cool but it was perfect weather for a bike ride and walk so we grabbed some sweatshirts and headed to the bike path. &nbsp;Each time we go, Evan is better and better at balancing on his balance bike. &nbsp;He'll be ready for a 2-wheeled pedal bike in no time. &nbsp;We found a couple of caterpillar friends, picked some flowers and enjoyed the beautiful day. &nbsp;After a good ride with lots of snacks and a quick nap for Logan, we went to West End Creamery for ice cream and Del's. &nbsp;Looking forward to a fun filled summer!<br />
