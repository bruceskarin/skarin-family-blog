Five months old!! &nbsp;Growing and thriving!!<br />
<br />
Last night as I was putting Logan to bed, going through the bedtime routine (bottle, read, more bottle and songs) in his room, I began to realize that we haven't really established a bedtime routine with him yet. &nbsp;For the most part, we have been keeping Logan downstairs with us until he is ready to go to sleep so that he doesn't wake up Evan. &nbsp;I also realized a couple of other things.<br />
<br />
He is a wiggler not a snuggler. &nbsp;When Logan starts getting tired, he doesn't snuggle in to your shoulder the way that Evan used to. &nbsp;Instead he wiggles and wiggles as if to say, "Just put me down."<br />
<br />
He is <strike>unpredictable</strike>&nbsp;whimsical (it sounds better). &nbsp;We have yet to find a pattern or schedule with Logan's sleep habits, day or night, or with his eating habits. &nbsp;Just when we think we have something figured out or things have been roughly the same for a few days, he changes. &nbsp;He always keeps us guessing. <br />
<br />
One thing is for sure. &nbsp;He is lovable, happy and sweet and we love our little guy very much! <br />
