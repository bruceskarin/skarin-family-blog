Last week while Daddy was walking for the <a href="http://www.nhrebellion.org/">NH Rebellion</a>, the boys and I took an unexpected trip to see Papa &amp; Gramma in Florida. &nbsp;Two weeks by myself with the boys in the cold snowy weather or a trip to warm, sunny Florida to see two of our most favorite people and get some help with these two little handfuls...I'm sure you can see why we chose option #2! &nbsp;It was a great trip, not as warm as we had hoped but still certainly much better than MA. &nbsp;The boys were amazing at the airport and on the flight down! &nbsp;We had fun being Papa &amp; Gramma's first house guests in their new home at Indian Creek and I think they loved having the boys (and me) there. &nbsp;We didn't spend much time at the pool, only about an hour and a half, but we were able to get out and run around, go to playgrounds, mini golf and even a quick (windy and chilly) trip to the beach. &nbsp;I don't think I would have survived 2 entire weeks on my own. &nbsp;Thank you, Mom and Dad, for all of your help...I don't know what I would do without you!<br />
