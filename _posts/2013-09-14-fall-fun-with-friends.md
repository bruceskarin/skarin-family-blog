Today we spent the day with Leah and Caleb. &nbsp;We hadn't seen Caleb since last July and I can't get over what a little man he has become. &nbsp;He is such a sweet, smart and kind little boy. &nbsp;He certainly takes after his momma! <br />
It was a beautiful day so we spent as much time outside as possible. &nbsp;The boys played soccer for a while in the yard, then we had lunch on the deck. &nbsp;After lunch and Logan's nap, we headed to West End Creamery for an afternoon of fall fun and ice cream! &nbsp;They have everything there for a complete day, ranging from mini golf and the barnyard jump to the corn maze to tractor rides and tire climbs. &nbsp;What a fabulous late summer/early fall day spent with friends who we don't get to see nearly often enough!<br />
