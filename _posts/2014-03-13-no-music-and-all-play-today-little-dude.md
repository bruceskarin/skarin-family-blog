We went to music class today to find that we were the only ones there for music due to the somewhat unexpected snow. &nbsp;So instead of music, we joined the play class. &nbsp;This was Logan's first organized play class and he had a great time. &nbsp;Lots of children his age with lots of room to run, jump, climb and play. &nbsp;Ms. Suzanne even put on some of the Irish/Celtic music that we should have listened to in music class. &nbsp;His favorite part was popping the bubbles!<br />
