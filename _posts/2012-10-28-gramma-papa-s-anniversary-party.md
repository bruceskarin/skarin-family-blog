Last night we had a 40th anniversary party for Gramma and Papa at Richard's Pub. &nbsp;They celebrated their 40th anniversary on <a href="http://blog.skarin.us/2012/09/papa-grammas-40th-anniversary.html">September 16th</a> and we wanted to have a party with friends and family to honor them and all they have done for us throughout our lives. &nbsp;There was a lot of planning and organizing that went into the night and it turned out exactly as we had imagined. &nbsp;The evening was complete with wonderful company including lots of family, old friends and new; great food; a photo slideshow; a little dancing; and some "Remember when" stories. We are so happy that so many people joined us to celebrate the long lasting love that Papa and Gramma have for each other. &nbsp;We love you!<br />
