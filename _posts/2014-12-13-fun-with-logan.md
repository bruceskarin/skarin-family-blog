I have really been enjoying my time with Logan this fall. &nbsp;It is nice to have some time with just him while Evan is at school. &nbsp;It's kind of funny because when Evan was the age that Logan is now, I didn't really get much time alone with him because we had Logan. &nbsp;It is nice to see Logan's personality developing and to watch him change and grow so quickly...too quickly! &nbsp;We have fun doing all sorts of things - running errands, playing at home, meeting friends for morning play dates, or just snuggling on the couch together. &nbsp;He is certainly my little buddy and I love it! <br />
<br />
Some of my favorite Logan-isms right now are:<br />
"I do good job, mommy?"/"I good helper, mommy?"<br />
"Wanna play with me, mommy?"<br />
"That's good singing back there" - after a song ends in the car<br />
and so many more...<br />
