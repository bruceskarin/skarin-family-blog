Every day Evan says things that are funny and memorable. &nbsp;The problem is that often by the end of the day I forget what those things are. &nbsp;Here are a few of his most recent gems. <br />
<br />
While trying to give him a kiss after changing his diaper - "No kisses, Mommy. &nbsp;When you're pretty you do give me kisses but when you're stinky you don't." "And I'm stinky now?" "Yup." "Why am I stinky?" "You're stinky from your bed. &nbsp;But when you have your pretty dress on, you do give me kisses."<br />
<br />
While Bruce was away for the weekend, Evan helped me wrap Christmas presents. &nbsp;I made the mistake of saying "And we wrapped presents for Daddy." &nbsp;Evan's response was "But where is the suitcase, mommy." &nbsp;I tried to play it off like he was talking about the red suitcase in the closet upstairs to which he replied, "No mommy, the green one."<br />
<br />
While on the toilet...Obviously! &nbsp;"Here comes more poop...look out!"<br />
<br />
Never a dull moment in our house!<br />
