Evan has been using the potty since Saturday and has had very few accidents. &nbsp;He has been wearing big boy undies all day, everyday with the exception of nap time and bed time. &nbsp;Today while he was on the toilet, we had a classic conversation. <br />
<div>
<br /></div>
<div>
Evan to me while pooping: I only have little poops, Mom. &nbsp;Not big poops. &nbsp;I'm done.</div>
<div>
Me: Well, why don't see if you have to go more?</div>
<div>
Evan: I don't</div>
<div>
Me: Just try</div>
<div>
(He tries)</div>
<div>
Evan: WOW! &nbsp;Those were big poops! &nbsp;They were like crickets.</div>
<div>
Me: Crickets?</div>
<div>
Evan: Yeah, crickets. &nbsp;Stinky poops are like crickets.</div>
<div>
<br /></div>
<div>
Don't ask me why or how but that is what he said. &nbsp;I could write a book with all of the gems he comes up with while sitting on the toilet. &nbsp;I'd call it Toilet Talks! <br />
<br />
All in all, I have to say that potty training Evan was a snap. &nbsp;We tried a few times (once in December, once in February, and then again just a few weeks ago) but he wasn't quite ready. &nbsp;Then this past weekend when we cancelled our trip to NJ, we decided to try again. &nbsp;We put Evan in big boy undies sometime mid-day Friday and by Sunday morning he was telling us when he needed to go and going on his own too! &nbsp;We even made it through a trip to the grocery store with no accidents today. &nbsp;Everyone told us when they are ready, they are ready...it really was true! &nbsp;Let's hope Logan is as easy!<br />
<br />
These pictures are from mid-April...I just love that little bum in big boy undies! &nbsp;Evan will likely hate me for posting these pictures someday. <br />
