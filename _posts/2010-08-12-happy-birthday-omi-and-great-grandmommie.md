Today was a very special day.  Not only was it Omi and Great-Grandmommie’s birthday but Evan was able to meet most of the extended Skarin family.  We spent the day at Great Aunt Linda and Great Uncle Jim’s house and were able to introduce Evan to Aunt Kristina, Uncle Ty and Hannah, Great-Grandmommie and Great- Grandpa, Jim and Linda’s family (complete with 4 grandchildren of their own), Great Uncle Marc and Sharon, Great-Great Aunt Muriel, as well as a few of Grandpa Bob’s cousins.  It was such a treat to see all the kids (young and old) enjoying each others company.  The bounce house was a big hit with all despite the oppressive humidity!  Thanks to Jim and Linda for hosting such a wonderful birthday celebration!<br />
