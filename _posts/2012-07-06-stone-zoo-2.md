Today was the second week of <a href="http://www.highlandstreet.org/special-programs/free-fun-fridays.html">Free Fun Friday's</a>, &nbsp;Evan and I attended a couple of these events last summer and while they are generally relatively crowded, it is a good way to check out a new place or activity. &nbsp;Of the five locations today, we decided to go to the Stone Zoo. &nbsp;We had been last year with Bruce but unfortunately I didn't remember what our experience was like until we were already there! &nbsp;It was hot, hot, hot today and the zoo was super crowded. &nbsp;The layout of this zoo is not the best so it gets very crowded quite quickly. &nbsp;It was so hot that most of the animals were hiding in the shade. &nbsp;Needless to say, we zipped through the zoo, stayed for about an hour and then went to have lunch (inside a nice office with cool AC) with daddy. &nbsp;At least lunch was fun! &nbsp;I guess we should have gone to the Boston's Children's Museum instead! &nbsp;Oh well, next time!<br />
