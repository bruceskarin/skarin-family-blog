We had a great day yesterday celebrating Logan's 2nd birthday with a sports themed party. &nbsp;For a number of reasons, we decided to keep it low key and it was perfect! &nbsp;The weather was beautiful! &nbsp;The 4 little monkeys and one lovely lady (Annalia) had lots of fun together...although I'm not so sure that anyone really played any sports...at least not until after Logan opened his presents! &nbsp;Jonah didn't get to join in much of the fun but he was still super cute and good. &nbsp;It was a wonderful day, celebrating our little guy who is growing up faster and faster by the day. &nbsp;We all love you so much, Pal! &nbsp;Keep making us laugh with that little twinkle in your eye!<br />
