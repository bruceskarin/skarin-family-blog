We have returned to a family of three after 5+ days as a family of five thanks to a little extra help from some very loving, helpful and caring grandparents. &nbsp;Over the course of the 5+ days, I learned (and confirmed) a lot of things.<br />
<br />
I have learned that...<br />
...taking care of 2 two-year olds and a five-year old is more work than I thought it would be (especially being 7+ months pregnant)<br />
...having family members who are willing to help is invaluable<br />
...being the oldest child is not always easy or fair<br />
...you need your mom and dad whether you are 2 or 33<br />
...having one-on-one time is priceless<br />
...having a child who sleeps until 8 am (and often later) is rare (and wonderful!)<br />
...getting out of the house at a decent time requires me to be up and showered before the kids wake up<br />
...every child has a unique personality with special quirks and gifts<br />
...nap time is important for everyone, especially mom<br />
...brothers share a special bond from a very early age<br />
...having fun doesn't always require spending money and going somewhere<br />
...some days you just need to cry<br />
...having three kids totally changes your defense plan, kick out man-to-man and bring in the zone<br />
...doing craft-type projects with a five-year old is a lot more fun than I thought it would be, especially once I got over the "everything needs to be perfect" feeling<br />
...using your imagination takes you to amazing places<br />
...reading bedtime stories to three sweet little boys is wonderful<br />
...watching my husband read to those same three boys is even sweeter<br />
...having more than one of the same toy often prevents arguments but not always<br />
...sometimes you just need to sit and snuggle with someone you love and who loves you<br />
...boys, even little ones, eat and drink a lot<br />
...changing diapers for two kids isn't as bad as I thought it would be<br />
...as nice as it is to return to our family of three, I already miss those little guys<br />
...I love my little nephews as if they were my own sons!<br />
