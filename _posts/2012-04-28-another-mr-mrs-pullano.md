This weekend we celebrated the marriage of another wonderful couple, Scott &amp; Jill. &nbsp;We brought Evan to the Cathedral with us and he was, once again, better than expected during the ceremony. &nbsp;We were a few minutes early (shocking, I know!) so we let Evan run around and dance in the plaza outside the cathedral. &nbsp;A group of people were filming a music video and Evan was feeling the groove. &nbsp;After the ceremony, we dropped Evan off at Uncle Dan and Auntie Karin's so that he could spend the night with Danny, Sean and Kelly, the babysitter. &nbsp;He had a fun night, as did we. &nbsp;Great food, fun dancing, wonderful people...and the best rendition of "Shout!" I have ever heard! &nbsp;I think the highlight of the night could have been Uncle Pat dancing to Apple Bottom Jeans! &nbsp;Congratulations to the newlyweds!!<br />
