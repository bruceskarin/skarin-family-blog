So far this winter, we have had very little snow, especially compared to the record totals of last year. &nbsp;I am generally not a huge fan of snow but I know a little guy who does like to get out and play in it. &nbsp;We have tried to take advantage of the mild weather and get outside as much as possible. &nbsp;Today, we finally had a chance to play in the snow!<br />
