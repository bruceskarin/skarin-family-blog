Today we celebrated Uncle Gary's 60th birthday from the Champion Club seats at The Great American Ballpark. &nbsp;Unfortunately the Reds lost to the Phillies but we had a great time anyhow. &nbsp;It was nice to be able to go inside to get food and drinks for "free" and to cool off on such a hot day. &nbsp;Evan was a little confused as to why we were cheering for the Reds and not the Red Sox but he still yelled "Go, go, go, Papi!" &nbsp;After a nap and a snack inside, we caught the end of the game before leaving the ballpark. &nbsp;We rounded out the afternoon and evening with dinner at Brio Tuscan Grille at Newport on the Levee. &nbsp;All in all, a great celebration of a very special guy, even if he is a Reds fan!<br />
