Today we went to the Boston Museum of Science with Omi and Grandpa. &nbsp;We were looking for something fun to do and while the MoS is a big more than what the boys, especially Logan, are ready for, we still had a great time. &nbsp;We started with a little animal show with a story, puppet and live tarantula while we were waiting for Daddy and Grandpa to join us. &nbsp;Then we spent some time in "A Bird's World". &nbsp;Quite fitting seeing as we were there with Omi! &nbsp;After checking out a few other exhibits on the lower level, we went to see the dinosaurs. &nbsp;We saw the 65-million year old triceratops fossil and full size t-rex model. After lunch, we played on the musical stairs before heading to an electricity show. &nbsp;It was quite loud and dark in there so Logan, Evan, and I didn't stay for long. &nbsp;The boys had fun in the full-size model of the Apollo capsule and then had fun exploring scale models of all sorts of things including a giant grasshopper and a set of very large teeth. &nbsp;We ended the day in the Science in the Park exhibit where the boys could run, jump, swing, play and explore before heading back to the car for a long ride home. &nbsp;There is so much more that we didn't get to explore and we will certainly be heading back to the MOS many more times with our budding scientists!<br />
