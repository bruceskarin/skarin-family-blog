After such a busy March and first few days of April, we have been enjoying a relatively quiet month since returning home from Florida. &nbsp;The weather has been quite nice and warmand sunny enough for us to get outside most days. &nbsp;It has been fun to watch Evan rediscover "old toys" and to watch Logan figure out "this outside thing". <br />
We have also enjoyed our time inside, with boys being boys, taking extra special care of "Baby George", and dancing. &nbsp;Evan has been asking me quite often to "dance with me, Mommy?" &nbsp;That is a request I simply can't refuse! &nbsp;I hope he still wants to dance with his mom when he's 15! <br />
