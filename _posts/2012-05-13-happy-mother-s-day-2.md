What a beautiful day to celebrate lots of special moms! &nbsp;Today started with what is becoming tradition in our house...omelets and mimosas made by Daddy and Grandpa. &nbsp;After a delicious brunch, we headed out to the New England Wild Flower Society's <a href="http://www.newfs.org/visit/Garden-in-the-Woods">Garden in the Woods</a> in Framingham. &nbsp;It is a place we had never been to but had been talking about going for quite some time. &nbsp;We walked through the gardens, exploring the main trail, making a couple of stops along the way to let Evan run around, have a couple of photo shoots and have a snack. &nbsp;When we were done exploring the gardens and seeing all of the plants, we went to the garden shop to purchase a few native plants for our own garden. &nbsp;They will be a great addition to some of the plants already established.<br />
For the second part of the day, we went to Uncle Dan and Auntie Karin's house for a family Mother's Day gathering. &nbsp;As always, the boys had fun playing together and running around. &nbsp;Uncle Dan and Daddy went to Iggy's to pick up some chowder and clamcakes, which was great because no one (especially the mom's) had to cook, plus I was craving some yesterday! &nbsp;A perfect RI weekend...chowder, clamcakes and Del's (yesterday at the zoo)!<br />
