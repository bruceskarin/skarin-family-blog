With a trip planned to Washington for Christmas this year, we celebrated Christmas with the Kavanagh's the weekend before Santa was set to arrive. &nbsp;Sean decided he needed a nice long nap at home so we were able to have a good visit with Bumpa, Grammy, Gramma and Papa before everyone else arrived. &nbsp;Evan was entertaining everyone and enjoying the&nbsp;hors d'oeuvres. &nbsp;Once everyone arrived, our house looked as if Santa had already arrived! &nbsp;It is crazy to think about how much money is spent on gifts when we all say that we "don't need anything"! &nbsp;We had fun opening presents and watching the little guys get so excited about each gift they opened...well except for the clothes! &nbsp;The biggest hits were the Elmo guitar from Uncle Dan and Auntie Karin and the keyboard from Gramma and Papa. &nbsp;Shortly after we finished opening gifts Santa drove by on a fire truck. &nbsp;It was a little disappointing though because usually he comes by twice (up and down the street) but he only came once this year. &nbsp;Oh well...Evan was the only one who really liked it anyway. &nbsp;We also enjoyed Bruce's wonderful lasagna and other side dishes and of course we all ate too much! &nbsp;And to think...this is just the beginning of the Christmas celebration! &nbsp;Oh boy!
