Today we had a nice visit with Papa &amp; Gramma for the Pats game. &nbsp;I know you are probably thinking that we see them all the time and that we often get together for Pats and Sox games but today was different because it was just the five of us. &nbsp;We hosted it at our house and Evan was the star of the show all day long! &nbsp;I don't think there was any time at all that he wasn't talking or being silly. &nbsp;At the end of the day, Papa wanted to test his vocabulary a little bit so he tried to think of a word that was somewhat difficult to say and that Evan had never heard before. &nbsp;Papa said, "Evan, can you say <i>Ardvark?</i>" and Evan turned to look at Papa and said, "<i>Ardvark</i>!" as if he had said it a million times before! &nbsp;We watched a good game and had so much fun. &nbsp;Evan even learned what to do when the Pats score a touchdown. </div>
