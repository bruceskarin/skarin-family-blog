Everyday when Evan is on his play-mat, he has a new favorite friend.  First, it was himself (in the mirror), then it was the giraffe, then the parrot, then the monkey and today it was the elephant.  He was having such a good time talking to and grabbing at his little purple elephant.  It was adorable!!  He was grabbing the elephant with his left hand but was so excited that his right hand kept swinging and getting stuck in the rings on one of his other toys.
