Yesterday we celebrated the wedding of Pete and Christine in Wellfleet, MA. &nbsp;It was a beautiful day for a wedding that took place at an amazing location. &nbsp;It actually reminded me of our ceremony at Smith's Castle. &nbsp;It was an intimate gathering with lots of non-typical wedding things - hoagies, cornhole, and s'mores just to name a few - and it was awesome!! &nbsp;Christine thought of every detail to perfectly execute her garden theme. &nbsp;The entire day was perfectly Pete and Christine! &nbsp;We wish them a very happy marriage with years of love, happiness and good health to come!<br />
