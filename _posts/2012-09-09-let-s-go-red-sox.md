Back in March, we purchased 4 right field roof Red Sox tickets for today's game figuring we would have no trouble finding two people to join us at the game. &nbsp;Little did we know that this was going to be the Sox worst season in years! &nbsp;After having no luck selling the other two tickets, we decided to take Evan to his first major league game at Fenway. &nbsp;He had a blast! &nbsp;He has been so excited about the Sox all season (at least someone has been!) and it was a great experience to share with him. &nbsp;It was also a special treat for Omi who grew up watching lots of baseball, mostly the Cincinatti Reds. &nbsp;The start of the game was a little chilly but the rain held off ans overall it was a nice day. &nbsp;We saw Wally, if only from a distance, and were able to walk along the warning track after the game. &nbsp;We even made it on the jumbo tron twice! &nbsp;Not bad for a first time experience. &nbsp;The only thing that would have made it better would have been if the Red Sox won but in Evan's eyes that doesn't matter too much. <br />
