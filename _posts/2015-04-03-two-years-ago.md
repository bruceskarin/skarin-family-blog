Hi Mom! &nbsp;It's me. &nbsp;Just checking in. &nbsp;It's been a little while since I wrote. &nbsp;I can hear you say "Are you mad at me?" :-) &nbsp;Not at all...not ever! &nbsp;I've had a couple of tough days this week, especially Wednesday. &nbsp;I'm not really sure why but I didn't even want to get out of bed. &nbsp;I almost skipped boot camp but I didn't and I'm glad that I went (both Wednesday and today). &nbsp;It is hard to believe that just 2 years ago this week, you and Dad were buying the house at Indian Creek. &nbsp;I still remember how happy you were when you were talking about it and when we went to see it for the first time. &nbsp;I'm so glad that you were able to enjoy it there with him for at least one winter. &nbsp;I wish you guys had decided to buy something sooner but then you wouldn't have bought that house and that one is just perfect! &nbsp;Right down to the depression glass dishes. <br />
I've been missing you like crazy, feeling overwhelmed with everything and this winter that just won't end. &nbsp;We finally had a couple of nice days this week and I'm hoping the temps in the 30s are gone for this year. &nbsp;The boys have been pretty good and super sweet when they know I'm upset or having a tough day. &nbsp;Evan has been drawing me all sorts of pictures. &nbsp;The other night when I was putting Logan to bed, this was our conversation:<br />
<br />
Me: I love you<br />
Logan: I love you little bit<br />
Me: &nbsp;What did you say<br />
Logan: I love you, little tiny bit (making a teeny tiny space between his fingers)<br />
Me: I love you, little tiny bit or lots, lots, lots?<br />
Logan: Lots, lots, lots<br />
Me: Who loves you little tiny bit?<br />
Logan: Gramma!<br />
Me: No buddy, Gramma loves you lots, lots, lots!!<br />
<br />
It was adorable! &nbsp;He is just something else. &nbsp;You should see him dance to Uptown Funk...and sing...It's priceless.<br />
<br />
I guess that's it for now. &nbsp;There so much more that I want to say but I can't seem to find the words right now. &nbsp;I think about you all the time...wishing for just a bit more time, one more phone call, one more lunch date...one more minute! &nbsp;I love you!<br />
