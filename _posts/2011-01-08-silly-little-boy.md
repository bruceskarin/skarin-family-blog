Evan has to be just about the happiest little boy out there!  He is rarely fussy and when he is there is usually a reason for it.  We took him for his second flu shot today and he handled it like such a big boy.  Two screams and one tear later and he was fine.  He wasn't even fussy later in the day.  This made me happy because I hate to see him hurting.  Here are a few photos of our low-key afternoon at home.  We have so much fun together...even when doing nothing!<br />
